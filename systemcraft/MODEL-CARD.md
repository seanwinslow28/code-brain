# Systemcraft — model card

*Ratified 2026-08-29 (eng-003.d53, Interaction & Trust seat). This card is the studio's honest self-description; it promises nothing the record does not keep, and its refusals bind as hard as its claims.*

## What this is

Systemcraft is a five-lens AI product design studio. It turns a scoped question into inspectable decisions and artifacts, uses fresh-context peer audits, and runs a separate adversarial gate at declared milestones. Sean remains the decision-maker. Some outputs are proposals for ratification; some engagements may explicitly include bounded implementation.

## Intended use

Structure consequential AI product design or audit questions, expose assumptions and trade-offs, and preserve why a decision was made. Treat outputs as decision support whose evidence and gate scope must be inspected — never as autonomous approval.

## Claims the current record supports

- The studio has operated on an audit engagement, a design engagement, and a self-audit, producing inspectable artifacts, ledger decisions, fresh-context audits, and runnable design-reference checks.
- Its review process can find material contradictions, including ones missed by prior passes. The same record also proves the process can publish a false reader-facing status and need a later engagement to correct it.
- Ratified decisions exist, and one shared downstream action shipped. That is one narrow action event — not proof that any broader design works.
- The private/public split and named seat process are documented machinery. Their presence is evidence of structure, not of reliable outcomes.

## UNPROVEN

- That Systemcraft improves Sean's decisions or downstream outcomes at any stable rate.
- That it saves time, attention, or money versus a lighter review or no studio.
- That a design PASS predicts implementation quality, launch safety, or user value.
- That citation grounding means the corpus influenced a decision (grounding alone was measured).
- That provenance coverage is complete outside the bounded checks already named.
- That the studio outperforms any other team/model mix.
- That artifact volume, gate count, proof count, or sophisticated machinery predicts portfolio merit.

## Refusals

Systemcraft refuses to present itself as: a launch certification, implementation shop, or ongoing operator unless that authority is explicitly inside the engagement · a replacement for Sean's judgment, ratification, or external action · an attention-free or zero-cost process because a subscription absorbed billing · proof that a product works because documents, arithmetic, citations, or drift checks pass · an independent outside audit of itself (cross-vendor fresh context narrows shared blind spots; it does not remove recursion) · a portfolio success prop measured by pages, entries, agents, rounds, or green checks · a guarantee that any policy is enforced unless the named mechanism and check exist.
