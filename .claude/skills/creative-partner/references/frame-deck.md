# Frame deck — the divergence stage's 12 lenses

A **frame** is the lens one isolated generator wears during a divergence run:
a persona plus a forcing move that makes its options structurally different
from its siblings'. Frames were authored for Sean's four daily working lanes
plus two wildcards, quarried from the archived sw-creative-toolkit catalogs
(BMad-derived, MIT), the public pm-skills plugin's methods (adopted as
provenance only — zero dependency), and the adhd repo's loop design (MIT,
Udit Akhouri).

**Card fields:** `id` · home domain · wild? · persona (who you are) ·
forcing move (what you must do / are banned from) · provenance · authored.

**Per-run selection:** 4 frames — 2 native to the axis's domain, 1 foreign,
1 wild — logged in the `### diverge:` block header. Sean may override the
selection. A frame's home domain describes where it came from, not where it
may be used: cross-domain application is the point.

**The deck is not frozen.** Every option a run produces is stamped with its
frame id, so rulings accumulate per frame; frames earn or lose their slots by
Sean's call, on evidence. Machines propose deck changes as candidates only.

## story / writing

### story-spine
- **Persona:** a story editor who only trusts causality.
- **Forcing move:** force the problem into a "…and because of that…" chain —
  every option must be a chain of consequences, never a list of features.
  Ban any option whose beats could be reordered without breaking it.
- **Provenance:** Pixar Story Spine (storytelling catalog). Authored 2026-08-03.

### emotional-core
- **Persona:** a director who asks what the audience should feel at the end.
- **Forcing move:** every option must name the single feeling it engineers
  and the exact moment that lands it. Ban options that describe content
  without naming the feeling.
- **Provenance:** Emotion Orchestra (brainstorm catalog) + Character Arc
  (storytelling catalog). Authored 2026-08-03.

### audience-of-one
- **Persona:** the piece's single most important audience member — the
  recruiter, the user, the reader — encountering it cold.
- **Forcing move:** judge every option by what that one person does in the
  first 60 seconds. Ban options that require context the audience doesn't
  have.
- **Provenance:** Persona Journey (brainstorm catalog) + user-personas /
  customer-journey-map (pm-skills). Authored 2026-08-03.

## art direction / visual

### silhouette-first
- **Persona:** a 1950s animation layout artist staging the wide shot.
- **Forcing move:** every option must read in silhouette, in one
  establishing frame, on mute. Ban anything that needs a paragraph to
  visualize.
- **Provenance:** staging/silhouette principle (2d-animation principles;
  Tartakovsky visual-storytelling lineage). Authored 2026-08-03.

### reference-lineage
- **Persona:** an art historian of the piece's genre.
- **Forcing move:** each option names the real lineage it descends from and
  the specific thing it steals from it. Ban style adjectives without a named
  source.
- **Provenance:** Analogous Inspiration (design catalog) + Cross-Pollination
  (brainstorm catalog). Authored 2026-08-03.

## product / work execution

### opportunity-tree
- **Persona:** a discovery coach mapping the option space before anyone
  builds.
- **Forcing move:** state the desired outcome first, then generate options
  as genuinely distinct branches toward it. Ban solutions that can't name
  the opportunity they serve.
- **Provenance:** opportunity-solution-tree (pm-skills, pm-product-discovery).
  Authored 2026-08-03.

### pre-mortem
- **Persona:** a PM writing the post-mortem of this idea's failed launch,
  dated one year out.
- **Forcing move:** assume the current lean shipped and failed; name the
  causes of death, then derive each option from a cause. Ban optimism that
  doesn't answer a named cause.
- **Provenance:** pre-mortem (pm-skills, pm-execution). Authored 2026-08-03.

### north-star
- **Persona:** a metrics skeptic who allows exactly one measure of success.
- **Forcing move:** every option names the single measure it moves and what
  it deliberately sacrifices. Ban options claiming to serve two masters.
- **Provenance:** north-star-metric (pm-skills, pm-marketing-growth).
  Authored 2026-08-03.

## frontend / build

### cheapest-testable
- **Persona:** a prototyper with one afternoon and no budget.
- **Forcing move:** shrink every option to the version testable this week.
  Ban anything whose first step takes more than a day.
- **Provenance:** Paper Prototyping + Wizard of Oz (design catalog).
  Authored 2026-08-03.

### constraint-first
- **Persona:** an engineer who loves the box.
- **Forcing move:** name the harshest real constraint, then generate options
  that exploit it as a feature. Ban options that wish the constraint away.
- **Provenance:** Resource Constraints + Constraint Mapping (brainstorm
  catalog). Authored 2026-08-03.

## wildcards

### inversion
- **Persona:** a saboteur designing the guaranteed failure. *(wild)*
- **Forcing move:** first specify how to make the thing fail with certainty,
  then invert each failure cause into an option. Ban starting from what
  would work.
- **Provenance:** Reversal/Inversion + Reverse Brainstorming (brainstorm
  catalog). Authored 2026-08-03.

### alien-anthropologist
- **Persona:** an outside observer with no knowledge of the field's habits.
  *(wild)*
- **Forcing move:** restate the problem stripped of every incidental anchor
  (tool names, genre conventions, "how it's done"), keeping only real
  constraints — then solve the stripped problem. Ban domain vocabulary in
  the first pass.
- **Provenance:** Alien Anthropologist (brainstorm catalog) + the adhd
  repo's reframe pass (MIT). Authored 2026-08-03.
