## Licensed moves: none

**Budget heat 0, room 1. Zero of thirty-six moves are licensed, and that is the ruling rather than a
gap** ([#162](https://github.com/seanwinslow28/code-brain/issues/162)).

This is the one document in the machine with no voice layer at all. Everything that makes Sean's
prose his — the deflation, the specificity that reaches for a named substance, the self-implication —
is banned here. What survives is the thing underneath the voice: **what he actually did, stated
plainly, in numbers a stranger can check.**

A resume that shows personality is not brave. It is a candidate telling a hiring manager he does not
know what this document is for.

## What a resume has to deliver

- **Traced claims only.** Every number, date, title, scope, tool and outcome came out of the
  interview. The gate blocks on anything else.
- **Outcome, not responsibility.** "Responsible for the agent fleet" describes a seat. What changed,
  by how much, is the bullet.
- **A measurement per bullet wherever one exists** — and no measurement invented where one does not.
  A bullet with no number is better than a bullet with a number he did not say.
- **Recency weighted.** The reader spends their attention at the top and it runs out.

## Format

- **Reverse chronological.** Role, organization, dates, then bullets.
- **One page** unless the history genuinely cannot compress; two is the ceiling.
- **Verb-led bullets**, one line each where possible, two at most.
- **Machine-parseable.** Single column, no text boxes, no tables, no graphics carrying information,
  no headers/footers holding content. Anything a parser drops is a claim the reader never sees.
- **The top third of page one is where the decision starts.** That is a format fact about how this
  document is read, not the Expressive lane's first-screen test, which does not apply here. Put the
  strongest traced outcome where the eye lands first.

## Negative specimens — what this must never look like

- **Resume-speak adjectives.** "Results-driven", "highly skilled", "passionate", "proven track
  record". Apply `substack-value-engine`'s strip-the-adjectives test: remove every self-describing
  word and see whether the decisions still prove competence. If removing them empties the bullet,
  the bullet was never evidence.
- **A borrowed string.** "Spearheaded cross-functional initiatives to drive alignment." Nobody says
  this. It came from a template, which means it came from a stranger — the lane's rule against
  borrowing strings, broken.
- **A responsibility list.** "Managed stakeholders. Owned the roadmap. Ran ceremonies." Three seats,
  no outcomes.
- **A hedged claim.** "Helped drive significant improvements in agent reliability." This is what an
  untraced claim looks like after someone softened it to get past the gate. The lane forbids it: ask
  or strike.
- **An invented metric.** Any percentage he did not say. This is the failure the origin block exists
  for, and it is the one with consequences outside the document.

## Gates, in order

Value → structure → shape → critique → humanity (neutral register) → **origin (blocks)** →
do-not-promote.

`writing-humanity-pass` runs its **FULL** scrub here, not voice-safe: plain neutral register is the
correct human voice for this document, and there are no signature moves to protect.

## Delivery

A ship packet: the document, the ORIGIN LEDGER, and the ASK LIST of anything he needs to confirm or
strike. The machine never sends an application.
