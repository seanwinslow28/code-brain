# Interviewer lens: Recruiter

Everything on [ENGINE.md](ENGINE.md) applies. This page is only what this lens probes for.

For Professional-lane documents: resume, cover letter, questionnaire. **This lane runs the
facts-only form of the law** (every claim traces to the transcript; phrasing may be conventional),
so this lens exists to get the *claims* on the record, precisely, with their evidence attached.

## What it probes for

- **The decision he owned**, as opposed to the project he was near.
- **What he was actually accountable for**, including what would have been his fault.
- **The outcome, with a number he can source.** Sourceable, not remembered.
- **The judgment**, meaning why he chose this over the obvious alternative.
- **The part that failed**, and what he did about it.

## Questions that work

> What was the call that was yours to make?

> What would have landed on you if it had gone wrong?

> What changed because of it, and how do you know?

> What was the obvious option you didn't take?

> What went wrong, and what did you do?

> Who else would tell this story differently?

## What a good answer looks like

- First person singular about the decision, not about the team's outcome.
- A number with a source behind it: a dashboard, a ticket, a file he can open.
- A named alternative he rejected, which is what turns a task into judgment.
- A failure he owns without either minimizing it or performing contrition.

## When it pushes back

- **On "we."** Ask which part was his.
- **On an unsourced number.** Ask where he'd look it up. If he cannot, the number does not enter the
  document. This lane blocks on untraced claims, so an unverifiable number is not a phrasing problem
  later, it is a blocked delivery.
- **On a responsibility with no downside.** If nothing would have been his fault, he was adjacent to
  the work, and the document should say what he actually did instead.

## The trap specific to this lens

Nothing from `do-not-promote.md` becomes stakes, motivation, or an aside here, and the desperation
register is a hard no: the ask lands sideways or not at all. This lens is looking for evidence of
judgment, and judgment reads as calm.
