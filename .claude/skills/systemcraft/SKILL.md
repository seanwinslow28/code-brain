# Systemcraft — master skill

The studio's orchestrator. **The skill knows the process, the seats know the craft, CLAUDE.md knows the law**: craft lives in [systemcraft/bench/](../../../systemcraft/bench/README.md) (contracts, templates, audit stakes, baselines, corpus discipline), law in [systemcraft/CLAUDE.md](../../../systemcraft/CLAUDE.md) (boundaries, explain-why, fresh-context audits) and, for what every -craft team shares, [craftwork/law.md](../../../craftwork/law.md). This file owns only what happens when, and never restates the other two. Excluded from export groups — useless without this repo's corpus and bench.

## Engagement types and routing

*(Amended 2026-08-29 — eng-003.d41, ratified.)* Type by the question being answered and the output owed, not by whether a target already exists.

| Type | Selection test | Routing |
|---|---|---|
| **Design a new project or operating model** | The engagement must choose a new organizing premise or produce a new full artifact chain, including a replacement for an existing system. | All five seats in pipeline order (Strategist → Architecture → Trust → Evals → Ops), no skipping; typed design gates fire. |
| **Audit / improve an existing system** | The engagement tests the behavior, evidence, or fitness of an existing organizing premise before deciding whether to preserve, repair, reframe, or replace it. | Every seat whose lane the target actually has, fresh-context; one audit-close gate. |
| **Support landing a role** | The output is a derivative used in a real role pursuit. | Owning seat(s) only; **real ledger entries, never hypotheticals**; no gate. |
| **Serve employer work** | The target and authority belong to employer work. | Route by the ask's shape; preserve the privacy law absolutely. |
| **Bounded one-off question** | One reversible framing or judgment question can be answered by one owning seat without changing governed state. | One seat, one decision entry, no PRD, roster, build, state mutation, or gate. Adjacent lanes are named only. If the answer creates dependent decisions, implementation, a hard-to-reverse commitment, or a P0-equivalent candidate, stop and retype before further work. A one-off cannot waive an existing phase gate. |

**Self-targeted modifier (eng-003.d41).** When the studio, coordinator, or studio law is the target, Open adds the five duties of [craftwork law § Self-targeted modifier](../../../craftwork/law.md#self-targeted-modifier) (shared law since 2026-10-01, text unchanged).

**Handoff modifier (Productcraft build map #271, ratified 2026-09-11).** When the engagement opens from another studio's handoff brief — Productcraft's Delivery seat crossing a *design* ask (typed *Design a new project*) or an audit finding (typed *Audit / improve*) — the authority is [the handoff contract](../../../craftwork/handoff-contract.md) and Open must additionally: (1) have the **Design Strategist run a fresh-context intake check** on the brief — its first act `python3 craftwork/trace/freeze.py verify` on every inbound copy — and issue its typed state — `accepted`, `input-required` (named gaps, no engagement opened), or `rejected` (reason, no engagement opened) — into `crossing.md` on both sides and this engagement's Open entry; the check asks only *can I do my job from this packet*, never whether the product decision was right; (2) set `originates_from: <brief id>` on the brief and every ledger entry; (3) treat the six **frozen, hashed copies** in `handoff/inbound/artifacts/` as the seats' upstream artifacts — the PRD's problem, users and assumptions inherit the sender's graded evidence; the studio never re-runs discovery or re-litigates the product decision, and a Productcraft artifact edited after the crossing does not move the target. Close then packages the return per [handoff-return.md](../../../systemcraft/templates/handoff-return.md) (see the Close checklist), freezing its copies with `freeze.py copy` against that file's strip list so Systemcraft's own process parts stay home (#326; the first return this applies to is eng-005's). Reasoning never crosses in either direction; ids do.

## Standing success measure

*(eng-003.d40/d11/d42/d43, ratified 2026-08-29.)* An engagement succeeds only on outside use at Close + 14 days, every Close-frozen P0-equivalent finding carries a dated Sean disposition, and Close is declared `ADMINISTRATIVE CLOSE — OUTCOME PENDING D+14`, never success. The authority, including the P0-equivalent definition, is [craftwork law § Standing success measure](../../../craftwork/law.md#standing-success-measure) (shared law since 2026-10-01, text unchanged).

## The five phases

**1 — Open.** Type the engagement (table above). Write a one-paragraph brief to `open-brief.md`, opening with the header the trace kit reads (`id`, `name`, `type` — `design` | `audit` | `role-support` | `employer-work` | `one-off` — `opened`, `closed`, `pass_budget`; [craftwork/trace/README.md](../../../craftwork/trace/README.md)). Pull relevant past ledger entries via `systemcraft/ledger/index.md` (two hops: index line → entry). Assign the engagement id (`eng-NNN-slug`). Run the **thin-lane check**: each rostered seat's lane manifest against the engagement's topics; a lane with no pointer for its topic drafts one tier up, declared in the pass budget before anything runs.

**2 — Route.** State the roster and each seat's runtime **from its own seat file's `model:` line** — never inherit the session model silently. Every runtime named is a row of [the runtime registry](../../../craftwork/templates/runtime-registry.md): a baseline only from a row whose standing is **ruled** or **baseline-eligible**, a substitution or a trial with its row and standing stated. State every deviation now or as it arises, each with a one-line why against a named trigger (below). **Run the alias probe** ([craftwork § The alias probe at Route](../../../craftwork/law.md#the-alias-probe-at-route)) before the first Claude pass: one line per alias into the Route entry, outside the budget; a mismatch goes to Sean before any seat runs. Dispatch forms are the registry's rows verbatim: Claude seats on row 1 with the seat's `model:` set explicitly; Codex passes on row 3, always `--model gpt-5.6-sol`.

**3 — Run.** Seats draft in pipeline order; hand **full artifacts forward, never summaries**. Every seat invocation is fresh-context: a subagent given its seat file, its lane manifest, and the upstream artifacts — never this conversation. **Trace every pass** (from eng-005): before firing a seat the coordinator writes the pass record's launch fields, after it returns the completion fields ([record-template.md](../../../craftwork/trace/record-template.md) — one file per invocation at `trace/pass-NN-<seat>-<kind>.md`, hashes of every input and output, `## Corpus read` from the transcript, `runtime:` from the transcript's model stamp, never the alias); every drafted or repaired artifact ends with its `## Moves` section. Audits per the bench's closed cycle, also fresh-context, on the auditor's own baseline. Material defects loop back to the drafting seat under [craftwork § Checks converge](../../../craftwork/law.md#checks-converge-and-the-ladder-has-brakes): one repair round per artifact per check, round two a diff-scoped verification pass on the other vendor, and every open series reported at each gate as *round / findings / repair size*. The PRD is not done until the Evals co-sign lands. **Ledger writes happen here**: the deciding seat writes an entry at the moment of a material decision (schema: [the shared ledger-entry template](../../../craftwork/templates/ledger-entry.md), with `## From the canon`, adopted 2026-10-01 on [#327](https://github.com/seanwinslow28/code-brain/issues/327); ids from `python3 craftwork/trace/nextid.py <engagement>`).

**4 — Gate.** Milestone engagements only, per [the red-team protocol](../../../craftwork/templates/red-team-protocol.md) (shared law) and [Systemcraft's gate schedule](../../../systemcraft/templates/gate-schedule.md): design engagements gate at PRD sign-off, design-complete, and pre-launch (the third fires only when an implementation candidate exists); audits gate once at close. Verdicts are typed per the protocol. **Vendor:** a gate runs on the vendor that did not last write its anchor artifact, and so does a verification pass ([craftwork § Model delegation](../../../craftwork/law.md#model-delegation)). FAIL → the owning seat redrafts one tier up and re-gates. A gate never silently skips; every gate writes its ledger entry.

**5 — Close.** Checklist, in order:
- [ ] Ledger entries complete; one line per entry appended to `ledger/index.md`.
- [ ] **Trace 1 — check**: `python3 craftwork/trace/check.py systemcraft/ledger/engagements/<eng-id>` — every rung-0 line PASS, or each FAIL fixed in the record it names, never by editing an artifact after its pass.
- [ ] **Trace 2 — render**: `python3 craftwork/trace/render.py systemcraft/ledger/engagements/<eng-id>` — writes `trace/eval.html` into the engagement; opened from disk, never hosted.
- [ ] **Trace 3 — labels**: every pass has a row with a verdict in `trace/labels.md`, coordinator-drafted and Sean-approved, and the file's provenance block says so ([#282](https://github.com/seanwinslow28/code-brain/issues/282) decision 4).
- [ ] **Trace 4 — registry numbers**: `python3 craftwork/trace/registry.py $(find productcraft/ledger/engagements systemcraft/ledger/engagements -mindepth 2 -maxdepth 2 -name trace -type d -exec dirname {} \; | sort)` — every traced engagement of both studios, since the registry's numbers are shared — and replace § Numbers of [runtime-registry.md](../../../craftwork/templates/runtime-registry.md) with its output: counts only, never typed by hand. Engagements before eng-005 carry no trace and are not engagements to the kit.
- [ ] **Residuals re-read against current state** ([craftwork § Checks converge](../../../craftwork/law.md#checks-converge-and-the-ladder-has-brakes), clause 7): a residual a later round already repaired closes as stale; duplicates route to one owner once.
- [ ] Corpus inbox sweep (`systemcraft/corpus/inbox.md`): file each entry or consciously defer — never silently skip.
- [ ] Live deferred work → one rule-8 ticket per item in `vault/00_inbox/tickets.md` (CLAUDE.md rule 8); everything else stays in the ledger for pull.
- [ ] Verify `git status` shows nothing under `systemcraft/{corpus,ledger,books}/` — the private layer never reaches git.
- [ ] Commit and push the ledger's own repo: `git -C systemcraft/ledger add -A && git -C systemcraft/ledger commit -m "<eng-id>: <close gist>" && git -C systemcraft/ledger push` — the ledger is its own git repo with a **private** remote (`seanwinslow28/systemcraft-ledger`, 2026-09-10); an unpushed ledger exists on one laptop only. Sessions are one per ticket, so every session that touched the ledger pushes before it ends, not just Close.
- [ ] Freeze the P0-equivalent denominator (Sean ratifies) and name the D+14 outcome-record date; Close is declared as `ADMINISTRATIVE CLOSE — OUTCOME PENDING D+14`, never success.
- [ ] **Handoff engagements only:** after Gate 2, the Design Strategist writes the return note per [templates/handoff-return.md](../../../systemcraft/templates/handoff-return.md), freezes the five artifacts with hashes into `handoff/outbound/`, copies the packet into the sender's `handoff/inbound/`, appends `returned` (or `returned-partial`, with the deferred lane's rule-8 ticket) to `crossing.md` on both sides, and confirms `supersedes_external` is set on every entry that overturned a product assumption. Gate 3 is recorded `NOT FIRED — IMPLEMENTATION ABSENT` until the sender's `candidate-note.md` arrives.
- [ ] Explain-why digest to Sean per [the close digest](../../../craftwork/templates/close-digest.md) — recommendation first, one question, statuses rendered per [the status vocabulary](../../../craftwork/templates/status-vocabulary.md) (both shared law).

## Model deviations (named triggers, per-pass, never per-engagement)

*(Adopted 2026-10-01 on [craftwork build 4](https://github.com/seanwinslow28/code-brain/issues/327), per [#282](https://github.com/seanwinslow28/code-brain/issues/282) decision 2; it replaces Systemcraft's earlier five-trigger ladder with Fable at its ceiling.)* The escalation ladder (Sonnet 5.5 → Opus 5.5 → Codex GPT-5.6 Sol High → Sol xhigh → ask Sean), its four triggers (gate FAIL, audit bounced at severity, thin lane at Route, owner-directed), what is *not* a trigger, one-tier-up-is-the-registry-row, the downshift, the gate-vendor rule and the pinning rule are [craftwork § Model delegation](../../../craftwork/law.md#model-delegation). Systemcraft's binding: the thin-lane trigger is decided by Open's thin-lane check, declared in the pass budget before anything runs. Every deviation carries a one-line why against a named trigger in the Route entry or as it arises. No silent deviations, ever.

**Aggregate budget (eng-003.d13/d04, ratified 2026-08-29 — words; tool enforcement gated).** Every Open declares an integer pass budget: the base pass manifest plus **two pre-authorized rounds per scheduled gate** (one owning-seat repair + one re-gate); Ops sets the caps, Sean ratifies them at Open, and **the coordinator's own session counts inside the budget**. An escalation needs both its per-invocation trigger and remaining aggregate allowance; exhaustion is a stop — actual-versus-cap plus one variance question to Sean, never a silent overrun and never a lane-crossing. Every invocation records a meter line (runtime-reported tokens/wall-clock, else `UNMEASURED`); a partial meter reports `known subtotal + N unmeasured passes`, never a precise total.

**Availability ladder (eng-003.d30, ratified as words).** An unavailable planned provider gets a dated Sean-approved substitution from a [runtime registry](../../../craftwork/templates/runtime-registry.md) row, named with its standing, that preserves seat identity, else a dated deferral of the dependent branch; never a merged lane or a silent switch. The authority is [craftwork law § Availability ladder](../../../craftwork/law.md#availability-ladder) (shared law since 2026-10-01).
