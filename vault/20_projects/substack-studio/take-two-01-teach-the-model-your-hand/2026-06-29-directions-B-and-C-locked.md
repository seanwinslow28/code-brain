# Directions A, B, and C — locked

Continuation of the prompts→systems pivot. This session ran fresh deep-research (15 sub-agents across all three directions), brainstormed each to a concrete shape, and ran each through the value gate. **All three PASS.** This doc is the synthesis; the cited evidence + full reasoning live in the three `direction-*-research.md` docs. (B and C were locked first per Sean's scoping; A followed in the same session.)

## The throughline (why A/B/C are one system, not three ideas)

> **It is all one primitive: a spec + a gate, at three scopes.**
> - **B writes *your* spec** — a personal map of your defensible creative edge.
> - **A enforces a spec *per project*** — declare creative intent, a gate checks the output before it passes (intent-engineering, applied to pixels).
> - **C builds the *shared, public* spec** — and benchmarks creative-AI work against it, fed by the fleet.

This is the spine of the whole publication post-pivot: not "teach a prompt," but "treat creative AI like a system — spec it, test it, version it, gate it." B is the emotional front door, A is the weekly engine that uses the spec, C is the serialization franchise that markets both and proves the discipline is real.

## Direction A — "Intent-engineering for creative work" (the spine) — LOCKED

**One line:** the teaching spine, re-engined. Take one creative task soulless→good by declaring intent as a brief and gating the output against it. The recipe is a SYSTEM, not a prompt. Image-first; the method travels.

- **Format identity:** A IS the already-locked teaching spine (spec §7 Format 1), sharpened into brief+gate — not a new format. It's the weekly engine.
- **Pain (real, current, about CONTROL):** the intent→output gap — "it won't do what I MEANT, it drifts, it's close-but-wrong and I can't pin down why." Named in HCI research (Promptify); the 2026 creator articulation is literally the intent-engineering thesis ("prompts fail because they're incomplete instructions; AI does what you ask, not what you meant"). The iteration tax is quantified (30-50 rolls per usable image; 3-10× cost; "slot machine"). **White space:** the *spec* instinct is taught informally but stops at vibe-checking; the *gate* half is open; spec-driven dev with executable gates is exploding in code, not images. Nobody packages "declare intent as a checkable spec + gate the output" for the individual creative.
- **Shape:** repeatable beat — soulless → name the defect → one intent move → reveal the delta → repeat → extract the move into the brief. Non-coder translation: brief = art-director's notes; gate = "does this survive revision?"; model = "literal-minded intern with no taste." **Design nuance (the load-bearing one):** the brief is *under-specified on purpose* (criteria + preclusions, not finished form) and the gate *evaluates after* the model widens the space, with a slot left to be surprised (from the CHI '24 fixation finding). **v1 gate = a copy-paste gate prompt.** **Flagship demo lane = pencil-test character / animation.**
- **Why it's defensible + Sean's:** A is the creative skin of his shipped **Intent Engineering MCP** (intent → audit → gate); the pencil-test pipeline (HF/SF codes + retry ladder + Em "proposes a fix, not pass/fail") is a live worked example. Nate owns the *thesis* (rejections compound, spec-leaves-room-for-confident-nonsense) but aims it at operators via text/strategy — never a live image-craft demo. ⚠ Competitive clock: Nate pivoted (2026-06-01) toward weekly hands-on "builds"; the lane won't stay empty.
- **Tie to B and C:** A is the verb. It *consumes* B's Edge Spec (your taste → the brief's criteria) and *produces* C's data (each gated run = a benchmark point).
- **Value gate: PASS (3/3).** Itch (the slot-machine pain is why anima exists); Solution (demos the loop on the pencil-test character with the real brief+gate+before/after); Transfer ("write an under-specified brief + run a copy-paste gate that catches drift and names the fix" — un-covered; **condition: the takeaway is the repeatable LOOP, not one specific brief**, or it's a fancy prompt). Honest-limits framing (no determinism; gate filters re-rolls; human keeps taste) is the anti-hype trust move.

## Direction B — "What AI Can't Flatten About You" (the on-ramp) — LOCKED

**One line:** VoicePrint, generalized to your whole creative self. A diagnostic that maps your defensible edge, converts AI dread into a usable tool, and ends by handing you the first draft of your intent spec.

- **Pain (verified real + current + narrow):** the loudest 2026 creative fear is *sameness / being flattened to the median*, not generic job loss (Sonarworks producer survey: originality is the #1 worry, above replacement; peer-reviewed homogenization: human writing adds 2–8× more collective diversity than GPT-4). The *concept* (taste matters / you won't be replaced) is saturated as essays; **a runnable, personalized creative-edge diagnostic does not exist** (null search; Nate ships essays + a company audit; Unpromptable is business-readiness-axed).
- **Shape:** a fast **gauntlet** (keep/kill reactions on examples in your lane — taste by negation, falsifiable, anti-Barnum) **+ a short self-interview**, fused into a one-page **Edge Spec** across 4 dimensions: the Cut, the Canon, the Intersection (personal monopoly), the Conviction — plus a negative-space block (what to let AI flatten) and a lock-list (markers that must survive). **Scope: broad** (any craft); **demo: single craft lane.** **v1 packaging: copy-paste prompt kit** (no install); hosted Claude Project is v2.
- **Why it's defensible + Sean's:** he already shipped the elicitation engine for *writing voice* (VoicePrint: interview/gauntlet/mine). B generalizes a proven loop. The psychology is the moat too: a self-authored edge map stacks self-affirmation + affect-labeling + restored agency — it *works where reassurance backfires* (reassurance is negative reinforcement; "you won't be replaced" triggers reactance in skeptics).
- **Tie to A:** the Edge Spec's fields ARE the first draft of an intent spec. B → A is a direct handoff.
- **Value gate: PASS (3/3).** Itch (Sean built against flattening — VoicePrint/anima/humanity-pass); Solution (he runs the diagnostic on himself in public, shows the real filled spec + median-vs-his comparison); Transfer ("produce a one-page spec of what's distinctly yours and use it to make AI output recognizably you, not the median" — un-covered).

## Direction C — "The Gate" (the serialization engine) — LOCKED

**One line:** the intent-engineering gate, made public and creative. Sean publishes a benchmark of creative-AI *control / taste / identity-retention*, fed by his fleet, and grows an open spec the audience contributes to.

- **Pain (two strong legs, one honest caveat):** the *evaluation/judgment* pain is the dominant 2026 discourse ("taste is the new bottleneck," "vibe checks aren't enough," ~28% of orgs have any formal AI-output eval; identity drift = the #1 named unsolved creative-AI problem, and it's a *measurement* problem). "No agreed bar for good vs. slop" is real (AI slop = Merriam-Webster 2025 WotY). **⚠ The one weak leg: creative *co-build* appetite is unproven** — the "build it in the open" energy is in the engineering-eval world, not the creative one. C must *create* that behavior, not ride it.
- **Shape (Sean's calls):** **lead with the published fleet benchmark + a versioned spec; co-building is one low-friction, attributed, *growing* layer** (vote on a verdict / propose a criterion the fleet tests / submit a "passed-but-shouldn't" case — credited by name). **Measure Sean's own fleet runs to start; fold in reader submissions once there's a following.** Spine: weekly **"Gate Check"** (one piece, verdict + fix + spec diff) → periodic **"State of Creative AI Control"** report. **Every issue ships the current spec as a copy-paste rubric** the reader runs on their own work.
- **Why it's defensible + Sean's (the strongest moat in the publication):** the fleet. anima's museum capture is already the publishing pipeline; Em's *calibrated* critic (κ 0.885; verdict 0.97/1.00/0.00) is the scoring engine AND the credibility receipts the research says are mandatory and **nobody else publishes**. The axis (control/taste/identity) is open vs. Nate (benchmarks models on operational reliability) and the arenas (capability/preference). Lead with the misses + the calibration — receipts, not claims.
- **Tie to A and B:** C is the communal scope of the same spec+gate primitive. The Gate's rubric is the shared "what good looks like"; B produces a personal version; A enforces a project version. C markets A and B every week.
- **Value gate: PASS (3/3).** Itch (arguably his most authentic — the whole critic stack + eval reset exists to answer "did this pass / is my judge trustworthy"); Solution (the artifacts already exist); Transfer (run the versioned rubric on your own work + see what good looks like measured — un-covered; **condition: ship the rubric every issue or it becomes spectating**). Real risks are cadence/maintenance + co-build adoption, both mitigated by lead-with-benchmark.
