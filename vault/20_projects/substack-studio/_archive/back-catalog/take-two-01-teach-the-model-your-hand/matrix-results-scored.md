# Matrix round 1: scored

## How to read the grade (the part that was missing)

Three axes, 0 to 2 each. The trick: **likeness came in near-max on all 12**, so it is not the differentiator. The decision lives in the other two axes, which is exactly the "more style, less me" tension.

- **Likeness** — recognizably Sean without a caption.
- **Hand** — does a deliberate style assert itself (real mark-making, a palette rule, a signature move), or is it a default the model reaches for?
- **Ownable** — could a stranger get here by typing "make it artistic"? Low = generic look anyone can prompt. High = you had to know something.

And the meta-point: **"good" depends on the job.** A hero, a recurring character, and a gallery-of-proof each weight these axes differently (see roles below).

## The pattern (your instinct, confirmed)

The styles that scored highest are the ones that **forced the medium to assert itself**: woodblock, riso, stained glass, biro, topo. The lowest are the ones where the medium is soft or photographic and the model just prettied up the headshot: tintype, gouache, watercolor, and the tame pencil-test. So "more art style, less me" is not a vibe, it is the correct optimization. The fix for the weak ones is to **push the register / commit to the medium's signature move harder**, not to make them more realistic.

## Tiers

- **Top (keepers):** 03 biro, 04 woodblock, 05 riso, 09 stained glass, 12 topo, 11 pixel.
- **Middle (competent, fixable):** 01 pencil-test (needs the full house treatment), 02 charcoal, 07 gouache, 08 watercolor, 06 flat.
- **Cut (or keep only as the "easy likeness, no hand" example):** 10 tintype.

## What to push on the weak ones (if we re-roll)

- **01 pencil-test:** add the missing house tells, faint blue construction underdrawing left visible, hole-punch marks on one margin, and dial the caricature UP (the house register is "absurd," this came back naturalistic). Right now it is the least-finished version of your own brand.
- **06 flat:** name the brow, jaw angle, and cowlick as deliberate shapes so the flattening does not average you toward generic-handsome.
- **07 gouache / 08 watercolor:** give them a stronger signature move (visible brush ridges / a hard ink contour holding the features) so they stop reading as "nice filter."
- **10 tintype:** probably not salvageable as a "hand" image; its honest role is the counter-example (likeness is easy, taste is not).

## Roles (what each is auditioning for)

- **Masthead hero / the "after":** 05 riso, 04 woodblock, or 09 stained glass. High likeness + high hand + high ownable. Riso is the most "editorial," stained glass the most "wow," woodblock the most "crafted."
- **Recurring series character:** needs to be simple, instantly you, repeatable. 03 biro or 11 pixel. (Flat #06 is the most reproducible but the least "you.")
- **The conceptual signature:** 12 topo, the one that says "this could only be Sean."
- **The gallery-of-proof:** all 12 as a set is itself a strong post artifact (see the open fork).

## Open fork (drives the next step)

The grade depends on which post these serve. Resolve before re-rolling or expanding:
1. **Gallery is the post** — "I taught the model my hand 12 ways; here are the ones that are actually me." The grid is the demo. Strongest, most unfakeable shape, and these 12 already carry it.
2. **One hero** — pick the single masthead winner, perfect it, the rest are B-roll.
3. **Recurring character** — lock one reproducible look for the whole series.

These are not exclusive; the likely answer is gallery-as-post + pull one recurring character out of it.
