## Why we stepped back (what drifted, what the soul actually is)

The publication kept getting molded into a "system framework" pitch (spec it / test it / version it / gate it → A/B/C). Useful as a destination, but it front-loaded the *architecture* and lost the thing Sean actually wants to preach:

> **Have the agent think like me.** You don't one-shot the art that's *you*. You build a creative *partner* — teach it your taste by brainstorming and interviewing with it (exactly how this very project was built) — and *then* you build the system/agentic architecture on top of that partner. The journey is the product.

The reorder: **partner first, system second.** The first posts are about using agents as a creative partner to bounce ideas off and develop your taste; they lead *into* the system/architecture posts (which is what the A/B/C research already mapped). The unique promise is the honesty: not "prompt this and it's you," but "here's how you build the partner, then build the system."

The Pi tagline rework is the mantra to land (Pi.dev: *"there are many agent harnesses, but this one is mine"* — itself a Rifleman's-Creed riff). Creative spin seeds (don't lock): *"There are many agents. This one thinks like me." / "Many models. One taste: mine." / "This one's mine."*

The aspirational model is **Matt Pocock** (senior engineer + YouTuber, https://github.com/mattpocock/skills.git): he ships skills/workflows that keep the human in the driver's seat while the agents do the work and serve as a thinking partner. Sean wants to be that, **in the creative realm** — teach the agent your taste, then develop the system.

## The refocused soul (carry this into the rebuild)

- **Mantra / north star:** have the agent think like *me* (Pi-tagline creative rework).
- **The arc:** build the creative partner (brainstorm + interview it into your taste) → then build the system/agentic architecture → then create art/branding that's uniquely you. Never one-shot.
- **First posts (the on-ramp):** the brainstorming / creative-partnership idea + packaged, opinionated **creative + PM skills** for creatives. Mix-and-match, geared to *ideation for creatives and whatever art they need*, with skills per medium: filmmaking, photography, music, comics, illustration, video editing, image editing, etc.
- **Then lead into:** taking the partner you've taste-trained and building the system (this is where the A/B/C research lands — as the *destination*, not the opener).
- **Audience hook (and it's honest):** bring the skills/workflows into tools people already search for — Higgsfield, ComfyUI, Claude Design, the video/image/audio models — with *Sean's opinionated spin* and the packaged skills/plugins to make it theirs. Plenty teach these tools; the differentiation is the taste-transfer method + the partner-then-system philosophy.
- **Assets to package + harden (not invent from scratch):**
  - Anima's creative skills: `/Users/seanwinslow/Code-Brain/anima/.claude/skills` — harden + universalize + differentiate via the research.
  - The `sw-creative-toolkit` bundle (brainstorm / design-thinking / innovation-strategy / problem-solving / storytelling / presentation) + the PM skill suite — re-geared toward creative ideation.
  - New skill idea: **"steal-like-an-artist"** — user describes and/or drops in a pile of references; the agent emits a unique prompt geared to that specific mix of references + description.
- **Keep (do not lose):** the working *process* itself — running ideas through the creative + PM skills and the voice chain (`substack-value-engine → storytelling-architecture → writing-voice-modes → writing-critique → writing-humanity-pass`) to reach a unique output. The value gate stays a hard gate. Anti-hype, demo-first, dive-bar grit, no em dashes.
