# Teach the Agent Your Craft

> Title locked 2026-06-30 (recast from the working "Make the Agent's Taste Yours", which read as an accidental double-entendre; "craft" also sidesteps Nate's semi-claimed "taste"). Hero concept to workshop: the Pencil & Prompt characters doing arts-and-crafts + a representation of context. The body still teaches taste-as-context; only the title framing moved to "craft".

**The relaunch flagship.** The Spine opener and the partner-build origin demo. The simple exercise: the same creative ask, prompted **cold** (no context, the soulless median) versus prompted **after** the agent has interviewed your taste out of you (references, what you like, what you reject, the rules of your hand). The delta is the lesson: that is *making the agent's taste yours*.

**Recasts** the old "Teach the Model Your Hand" capture ([../take-two-01-teach-the-model-your-hand/capture-plan-and-beats.md](../take-two-01-teach-the-model-your-hand/capture-plan-and-beats.md)). Reuses its capture discipline + the Taste Rubric (now the taste-context block in Part D), but the framing moved: this is no longer "teach a style the model has no prior for," it is "**context is the difference between the median and you.**"

**Value gate:** Itch and Transfer pre-cleared. **Solution stays empty until the capture exists.** This doc is the shoot plan, the beats, and the two gifts, NOT a draft. Write from the build.

**Ships both tiers:** the copy-paste kit (Part C1) AND the Creative Partner skill v1 (Part C2). Plants one forward line about memory (Part B, beat 8) as the bridge into the next post.

No em dashes. Anti-hype, dive-bar grit, the ask lands sideways, demo-first.

Sources: discovery synthesis [2026-06-27](../research/discovery/2026-06-27-discovery-synthesis-backlog-and-web-supplement.md), [Science Advances on AI + creative homogenization](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.adn5290).

Pocock's `grill-me` interviews you, but engineer-only. So the wedge is NOT the bare claim "give the model context." The uncontested ground: **the agent earns your taste by being interviewed out of you, demoed on a personal creative style going soulless to yours, shipped as a reusable partner.** The delta is the *method of building the context* + the creative-taste domain. Lead with the demo. Sources: Executive Circle MCP (Nate archive: "The Universal AI Skill: Good Taste," "ChatGPT 101"), [Pocock grill-me](https://www.aihero.dev/my-grill-me-skill-has-gone-viral).

## Part B, the beat skeleton (lock the shape, then run the voice chain)

Chain (CLAUDE.md §8): `substack-value-engine` then `storytelling-architecture` then `writing-voice-modes` (Sean Mode) then `writing-critique` then `writing-humanity-pass`. This shape is locked; draft only after Sean approves.

- **Tone dial:** Sean Mode, amber-warm (human/personal), dive-bar grit, anti-hype, self-deprecating. The ask lands sideways. No em dashes.
- **Length:** flagship, ~1,200 to 1,600 words. One lane (visual), one idea (taste-as-context, earned by interview), one promise (make AI output recognizably yours).
- **JTBD anchor:** the reader's job is to make AI produce work that is recognizably theirs, in the tool they already have. The second, flagship job is to show a new reader there is a real method here. Every beat serves the first; the partner-then-system arc is texture, never the thesis.

**The arc (but / therefore, cold-open to sideways-ask):**

1. **Cold open (the cringe).** You want one simple thing: a drawing of the Saturday-morning-cartoon moment. You type the lazy prompt and get the median back: glossy, Pixar-adjacent, heartwarming, dead. Competent, and nobody's. *(hero "before")*
2. **BUT** that is the exact output the skeptic quits on, and concludes AI is soulless. They are half right. The output is soulless, not because AI can't do soul, but because it has no idea what *your* soul looks like, so it drew the average of everyone's nostalgia. **THEREFORE** the missing input is not a better prompt. It is your taste, as context.
3. **The reframe (sideways, not preachy).** This is where someone tells you you're "prompting it wrong." You're not. You quit one step early. And the fix is not a longer prompt or a JSON schema (one sideways line: a schema locks a product label, that is the wrong tool for soul; soul is taught, not specified). The fix is to make the agent learn your taste the way an art director learns a new illustrator's: by interviewing them.
4. **The featured interview (the mechanism, the moat).** You open the Creative Partner and let it interview your taste out of you. Feature the 3-4 sharpest keep/kill exchanges where it makes you *say* what you believe about your hand. Output: your reusable taste-context block (the 8 fields), shown.
5. **The payoff (cold vs with-block).** Re-run the exact same scene with the block in context. The with-taste version is unmistakably your hand. The before/after pair carries the argument.
6. **The honest refine.** One reroll beat: it drifted back toward the average, you steered it back with one scoped correction. Name it as normal: the block gets you most of the way, then you fight the regression to the mean, in public.
7. **The gift (both tiers).** Hand over the method, runnable today: the copy-paste kit (the interview prompt + the block template + the "pick your lane" note) and the Creative Partner skill (symlinkable, the level-up). Transfer line: run it on *your* hand, *your* craft, whatever the model keeps flattening on you.
8. **Closer (the memory teaser + the sideways ask).** The Saturday-morning callback, now literal because the scene *was* the cartoons. Then the one forward line:
   > The interview gets the agent your taste today. Next, we give it a memory, so it keeps your taste and compounds it, session after session. The partner starts becoming a system.
   You taught the intern your hand; the cereal's still on the floor; go teach it yours. Ends on the work. Closer-line seed: *"Many models. One taste: mine."*

## Part C, the two shipped artifacts

### C1, the copy-paste kit (the on-ramp, no install)

Three pieces the reader can use today in the tool they already have.

**1. The Creative Partner interview prompt** (paste into Claude or ChatGPT; it interviews your taste and emits the block):

```
You are my Creative Partner. Your job is to interview my creative taste out of me,
then write it back as a reusable context block I can paste into any future prompt.

Do NOT flatter me and do NOT just agree. Push back. Make me say what I actually believe.

Run it like an art director meeting a new illustrator:
1. Ask me to share 3-5 things I made or love. Ask what the throughline is. Don't accept
   "I just like it", dig until I name the decision underneath.
2. Offer me two quick treatment directions for a sample piece. Make me KILL one and say why.
   The "why" is the taste.
3. Pull my NEVER-DO list out of me: the AI-look tells I ban on sight. Get 5-10.
4. Name the ONE move that carries the meaning in my work.
5. Then write my taste as an 8-field TASTE-CONTEXT BLOCK (template below), filled in from
   what I said. Keep it short. Every line should be a decision you could not guess from a
   generic style label. End by asking me what to fix.
```

**2. The taste-context block template** (the gift, fill-in, see Part D for the field detail).

**3. The "pick your lane" note** (tool-agnostic):
> Same method everywhere, the leverage is the taste-context, not the tool. ChatGPT one thread (simplest, does the interview and the image in one place). Claude for the interview + your image tool (Sean's setup). Gemini / Nano Banana if you want max image fidelity. Own the block as a file; it travels across all of them.

### C2, the Creative Partner skill v1 (the level-up)

The shipped `.claude` skill. **v1 scope only:** a user-invoked orchestrator that runs the taste interview (brainstorm-light, keep/kill, references, rules, the negative list) and emits the reusable taste-context block. Nothing more for v1. Memory is the next post; per-medium versions come later.

- **Type:** user-invoked orchestrator (may call discipline skills, never another orchestrator).
- **Hardened from:** `creative-director` (interview, propose 2-3 routes, critique) + VoicePrint (interview / gauntlet / mine, taste-by-negation) + `sw-creative-toolkit:brainstorm` (diverge, push back). Universalized off the Pencil Test pipeline to any craft.
- **The load-bearing principle (in the skill, not just the pitch):** the agent earns your taste by being interviewed, not by being told once.
- **Both tiers:** this skill + the copy-paste prompt in C1.
- **Hardening checklist (SKILL-PACKAGING-PLAN §4), to clear before ship:** small + composable (one job: taste to block); organized by pain (cold prompts return the median); model-agnostic; both tiers; user-invoked declared; the moat (taste-by-interview) is what it *does*; a proof built in (the before/after in this post); dogfood a stranger (run it as a non-Sean persona, zero-leakage + distinctness check); differentiation named (nobody ships a creative-taste interview).

## Part D, the taste-context block (the bridge artifact + the reader's gift)

The 8-field block the Creative Partner emits. **Durable taste, subject-agnostic**, it carries your hand, not this scene, so it pastes into any future prompt. Scene direction lives in the per-image prompt, not here. Keep/kill (field 3) and the NEVER-DO list (field 8) are the interview-featured, load-bearing fields.

```
MY TASTE-CONTEXT BLOCK (the Creative Partner fills this from the interview; the reader fills it for their own hand)

1. REFERENCES: 3-5 images of MY own work (or the exact hand I want). Attach them with the block.
2. MEDIUM / SUBSTRATE: what is it physically made of?
   (Sean: warm cream paper #FFF9F0, graphite + ink, animator's pencil-test feel, paper grain, hole-punch marks)
3. THE HAND (mark-making): how do my lines behave?
   (Sean: graphite linework, fine cross-hatching, faint light-blue construction underdrawing left visible)
4. COLOR RULE (stated as a rule):
   (Sean: monochrome graphite + ink, ONE restrained accent. Amber for human/personal.)
5. THE "SHOW THE WORK" TELL: what signals hand-made, not rendered?
   (Sean: construction lines left in, paper grain, hole-punch marks)
6. REGISTER (realistic <-> exaggerated):
   (Sean: wildly exaggerated, absurd caricature, never realistic)
7. THE ONE MOVE THAT CARRIES MEANING:
   (Sean: one amber accent on the element that holds the meaning + one soft amber watercolor bloom BEHIND the linework, never on top, never splatter)
8. NEVER DO (the negative list, 5-10 items, the AI-look tells to ban):
   (Sean: no gradients, no glossy 3D, no airbrush, no Pixar-smooth, no dead-eyed heartwarming, no text/letters/logos, no symmetry)

Then iterate: change ONE thing per turn, keep what works, re-feed the references when it drifts.
```

The reader gets the blank template; Sean's filled version is the worked example shown in the post.

## Risks / pre-mortem (baked into the plan)

| Risk | Mitigation (already in the plan) |
|---|---|
| Reads like Nate's "add context" productivity tip | The contribution is the *interview that extracts taste*, not "add context." Lead with the demo + the convergence, feature the keep/kill exchanges. |
| The cold "before" comes out competent, not cringe | The thesis holds: competent-but-generic is not YOU. Frame as "good, and still the average of everyone's nostalgia." |
| The interview half drags or competes with the image payoff | Feature only 3-4 sharpest exchanges (hybrid spine), not the full transcript. The image before/after is the payoff. |
| Two capture surfaces confuse the reader | The post threads them as one story: interview produces the block, the block transforms the render. The block is the visible bridge. |
| The arc is not a clean monotone climb (model is stochastic) | Capture rerolls + narrate the honest claw-back; the corrections are the story. |
| The recipe reads as "do what Sean did" (doesn't transfer) | Part C/D ship a fill-in block + interview prompt for the reader's OWN taste; the gift is the form, not the pencil-test. |
| Flagship tries to carry the whole masthead | Post 1 only proves the partner's first move (taste to yours). The arc + the memory teaser are texture, one line, at the closer. |
| Looks like a house-style ad | The lesson is "context is the difference between the median and you"; the cartoon scene + pencil-test are the worked example, explicitly not the point. |
