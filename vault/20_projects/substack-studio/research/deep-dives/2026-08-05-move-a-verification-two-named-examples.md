# Move A — Verification of the Two Load-Bearing Examples

The [citation tier audit](2026-08-05-citation-tier-audit.md) found that Topic 1's answer to *"does any library publish per-entry tested verdicts with honest failures?"* rested on two entities, both **self-sourced** to their own marketing pages. This is the independent check. Cost: $0.

## Verdict table

| Entity | DR report claimed | Verified reality | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Techpresso AI Academy** | "actively publishing honest failures alongside" successful prompts | 50 copy-paste prompts with descriptions and "pro tips." **No evaluation results, no pass/fail verdicts, no documented failure cases, no metrics, no failure rates.** The only limitation language on the page is an FAQ line: *"Most of these are ready to use after one edit pass."* Testing is asserted ("hand-curated and tested by the AI Academy team") with methodology and results undisclosed. | ❌ **FALSIFIED** |
| **Aksoy Capital** | 1-hour retraction SLA, URL preserved, public correction logs, L0–L5 autonomy policy | Policy is **real and precisely as described**: *"Within 1 hour of detection: Replace the artifact's body with a 'RETRACTED' notice that names what was wrong. URL stays the same to preserve search index continuity."* Originals move to `data/retracted/{slug}.json`; follow-up note in the next morning brief; new QA gate within 7 days. Full L0–L5 ladder, committed to stop at L4, named editor-in-chief, effective 2026-05-06. | ⚠️ **POLICY CONFIRMED, PRACTICE UNVERIFIED, OUT OF DOMAIN** |

## Detail on Aksoy Capital

The DR report did not exaggerate this one. The policy is more rigorous than most newsrooms run, and it is genuinely admirable prior art for the *retraction* half of the refocus's product idea.

Three caveats that keep it from being a counterexample:

1. **No evidence it has ever fired.** `/data/retracted/`, `/retractions/` and `/corrections/` all 404. A web search surfaced no Aksoy-specific retraction. A stated policy is a commitment, not a track record — which is precisely the distinction the refocus's "publish the failures" bet is built on.
2. **Out of domain.** This is algorithmic *financial research*, not a prompt or technique library. It shares the retraction discipline, not the artifact type.
3. **Per-artifact, not per-entry.** Retraction covers published research artifacts. Nothing here resembles a per-entry beat/tied/lost verdict on a reusable technique.

## What this settles

**Topic 1's direct answer is wrong as applied to the refocus's question.** No prompt or technique library was found publishing per-entry tested verdicts with honest failures. One claimed example does not do it at all; the other does something adjacent, in a different domain, with no demonstrated instances.

This **upgrades synthesis Findings 1 and 2 off probation** — but with the claim restated, not the original one:

> ❌ "Nobody publishes tested verdicts with honest failures." *(Too broad. Aksoy Capital's policy would falsify it, and a skeptic would find it.)*
>
> ✅ "No prompt or technique library publishes per-entry tested verdicts with honest failures. The retraction discipline exists in algorithmic financial research; the evaluation rigor exists inside private enterprise eval tooling. Nobody has brought either to a public library of creative technique."

That sentence is defensible, more interesting than the broad version, and it names the two nearest neighbours instead of pretending they don't exist — which is the honest-failure posture the masthead promises.

## Residual risk

This clears the two entities the DR report named. It does **not** clear the field, because the search that produced them was 65% vendor marketing and may have missed real candidates entirely.

**Move B (named-candidate falsification) must still run before this claim is published.** Coverage, not these two entities, is the remaining exposure.
