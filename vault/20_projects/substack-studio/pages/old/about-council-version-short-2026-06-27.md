# About

I taught myself animation and filmmaking, made a comedy short called *Break Time*, and wrote eleven screenplays in my twenties that nobody asked for and nobody read. These days a small fleet of AI agents handles a chunk of the grunt work while I sit here like a raccoon with a pencil, deciding what still feels like me.

Then AI showed up and everyone lost it. Half the feed turned into Miyazaki characters overnight, the other half wrote art's obituary on Medium and lit a candle. I didn't buy either one. The machine isn't here to replace you. It's a wildly talented intern fresh out of some prestigious school, taught by faceless strangers who have no clue what you'd call taste, and your job is to take this kid to happy hour and teach it yours after a few pints on an empty stomach. Somewhere in that mess I built anima, a 2D animation pipeline where the agents propose and I own the timing, the casting, and the taste. The intern never gets a vote on that.

I'm not going to preach that taste matters like some guy in a black turtleneck sniffing a wine cork. I'm going to show it. One real creative job at a time, I start on the soulless first pass that makes you cringe, drag it move by move until it has a pulse, then hand you the recipe.

Here's what you'll find:

- **Take Two:** a real piece walked from soulless to yours, plus the method.
- **Tim The Tool Man Taylor:** the occasional no-code tool that kills a grind.
- **Fronkenschteen:** send me your worst AI output, I fix it in public.

No hype, no obituaries, no guru selling you a webinar from a hotel lobby. Just the work, the wrong turns, and the recipe at the end.

If the machine hands you slop, don't close the tab and don't toss your laptop out the window. Your parents raised you better than that. That's just the intern asking to be taught.

*YouTube: youtube.com/@seanpwinslow ("I like getting creative with AI and making silly cartoons," where Break Time lives) · github.com/seanwinslow28 · seanwinslow.com · start with [Take Two #1].*
