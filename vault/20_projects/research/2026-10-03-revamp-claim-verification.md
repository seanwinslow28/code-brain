# Revamp research: claim verification

Fact-check of load-bearing claims in three notes written 2026-10-03:

- `2026-10-03-as-of-october-2026-what-does-current-evidence-show-about-des.md` (Gemini DR, "Gemini note")
- `2026-10-03-overnight-ticket-agents.md` ("overnight note")
- `2026-10-03-home-base-machine-sync-models.md` ("home-base note")

Each claim was checked against the paper, spec, or vendor doc it rests on. Gemini citations were resolved from their `vertexaisearch` redirects first. Verdicts: **CONFIRMED** · **PARTLY** · **MISREAD** (the source exists but says something else) · **NOT FOUND** · **UNVERIFIABLE** (primary source blocked). Tiers: A academic · B primary · C trade · D forum.

## Summary

- **Do not use in design decisions:** #1 (the 93% is KV-cache reuse, not note reuse), #2 (a vendor case study about dev effort), and the Gemini note's framing of #5 (a single 10-trial red-team case study, not a population rate).
- **Numbers need correcting:** #7, Advani. The source gives 45% and 48% (airline and retail), 3% for telecom, 75.8% for AppWorld, and a per-model range of 13–79%. The note says 44–52% and 13–89%.
- **Confirmed as stated:** #3, #4, #6 (with a caveat), #8, and the #9 items.
- **#10 rests on tier C:** OpenAI's primary pages for Dots return 403. Muse's MCP "dispute" resolves: there's no MCP-URL field, but Muse builds its own MCP client when asked through a Custom Connector.

## Corrections to carry into the redesign notes

1. Gemini note §1: drop the 93% "reuse" figure and the 52% / 2.1x figures as capture evidence. Neither measures note reuse or capture. The "notes get reused" claim in that section has no measured support left.
2. Gemini note §4: present the 80%+ as "one 10-email red-team case study (Microsoft), after the prompt told the agent to consult memory." It is not a deployment-wide rate.
3. Overnight note, lever 1 and §2: change to "45–48% (single-control tau2) and 75.8% (AppWorld), 3% where the environment verifies state independently; per-model 13–79%." The telecom 3% supports the note's own thesis: external state verification suppresses false success.
4. Home-base note: Muse MCP is "works only by asking Muse to write a custom connector to a public URL", not "conflicting."
5. Dots claims stay tier C until an OpenAI page is readable.
