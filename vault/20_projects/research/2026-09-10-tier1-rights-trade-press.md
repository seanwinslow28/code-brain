# Tier 1 rights and format audit: trade-press titles

Accessed: 2026-09-10 (United States)

Scope: determine whether four Productcraft canon books can be acquired as lawful, reusable EPUB/PDF knowledge sources. No purchase was made and no author, publisher, retailer, or institution was contacted.

## Executive finding

None of the four is verified ingestible before purchase. Amazon's current policy makes EPUB/PDF downloads available only to **verified purchasers** of books whose publisher has confirmed the title as DRM-free, through the purchaser's **Manage Your Content and Devices** page. The policy does not identify a public pre-purchase lookup or badge. I inspected each live US Kindle product page; none disclosed “DRM,” “DRM-free,” “EPUB,” “PDF,” or a downloadable-file entitlement. Therefore, Kindle is a title-by-title **post-purchase test**, not a verified acquisition path.

The clearest US retail evidence outside Amazon is Kobo: all four US editions are explicitly labeled Adobe DRM. The authorized library route (OverDrive/Libby) likewise lists Adobe EPUB editions; OverDrive says Adobe EPUB is DRM-protected, delivered through an ACSM/ADE workflow, and expires at the end of the loan. That is useful for human reading but not a lawful reusable corpus for the agent train.

No publisher- or author-direct DRM-free US EPUB/PDF was found for any title. No general institutional platform offering an unrestricted full-book or chapter-PDF download was found. PRH's Bookshare partnership and Macmillan's alternate-format program are disability accommodations, not general AI-ingestion licenses.

### Shared source rules

- Amazon: [Digital Rights Management](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GDDXGH9VR22ACM8U) — effective 2026-01-20, a verified purchaser may download EPUB/PDF only for a title confirmed DRM-free by its publisher; the download appears in Manage Your Content and Devices. For pre-2025 books originally published without DRM, the publisher must actively reconfirm the setting. Borrowed/subscription copies do not qualify. Accessed 2026-09-10.
- OverDrive: [Different reading options for library ebooks](https://help.overdrive.com/en-us/0012.html) — “Adobe EPUB” and “Adobe PDF” are protected by Adobe DRM; only “Open EPUB/Open PDF” are unprotected. Accessed 2026-09-10. [Where library ebook files are saved](https://help.overdrive.com/en-us/0290.html) states that the ACSM is a license/download instruction, and that downloaded library EPUB/PDF files expire. Accessed 2026-09-10.
- PRH permissions: [PRH Permissions Portal](https://permissions.penguinrandomhouse.com/) is for excerpts/portions, digital publication, and electronic reserve—not a published full-book AI/TDM license. Its [FAQ](https://permissions.penguinrandomhouse.com/faqs.php) says to choose “Other” if the desired format is absent and to email `permissions@penguinrandomhouse.com` with specific parameters when no project type applies. Accessed 2026-09-10.
- PRH accessibility: [PRH/Bookshare partnership](https://permissions.penguinrandomhouse.com/prh-bookshare.php) supplies DAISY/Braille-accessible files only to people with qualifying print disabilities under 17 U.S.C. §121. [Bookshare's membership agreement](https://www.bookshare.org/individualMemberAgreement) limits copyrighted downloads to the qualified member's personal disability access and prohibits sharing them with other people or organizations. This does not authorize adding a Bookshare file to a general agent knowledge base. Accessed 2026-09-10.

**Outcome: `purchase-test-needed`**

Identifiers: Crown Currency ebook ISBN 9780307886255; Kindle ASIN B004J4WKEC.

1. **Amazon status.** The live [US Kindle listing](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B004J4WKEC) identifies the seller as Random House LLC and the publisher as Crown Currency, but exposes no pre-purchase DRM-free or EPUB/PDF-download statement. Current pre-purchase result: **unknown**, not DRM-free.
2. **Author/publisher direct.** [Rumelt's official books page](https://www.richardrumelt.com/books) lists the book but offers no direct EPUB/PDF sale or download. [PRH's ebook page](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/208668/good-strategy-bad-strategy-by-richard-rumelt/9780307886255/) routes buyers to retailers and does not promise a DRM-free file. No author/publisher-direct DRM-free US edition was found. Accessed 2026-09-10.
3. **Institutional/download options.** OverDrive catalogs the Crown ebook as OverDrive Read and EPUB ([Seattle Public Library record](https://spl.overdrive.com/media/525312), ISBN 9780307886255), but its format rules identify Adobe EPUB as DRM-protected and expiring; it does not offer chapter PDFs. Bookshare may supply an accessible edition to a qualifying member through PRH's partnership, but the personal-use/no-sharing terms prevent use as a general train corpus. No unrestricted institutional full-book or chapter-PDF route was found.
4. **Permissions, accessibility, AI/TDM.** PRH offers excerpt/electronic-reserve requests through its [Permissions Portal](https://permissions.penguinrandomhouse.com/) and routes unlisted uses to `permissions@penguinrandomhouse.com`. It offers disability access through Bookshare. No public PRH page was found that grants or prices individual full-book AI inference, AI training, or text-and-data-mining rights for this title.
5. **Best lawful next action.** Make the $14.99 Kindle edition a controlled purchase test: immediately inspect Manage Your Content and Devices for an EPUB/PDF download and preserve the receipt. If no download appears, do not attempt conversion; request a narrowly described internal, non-redistributed agent-reference license from PRH permissions, specifying full text, storage, model/provider, retention, number of users/agents, and whether the use is inference-only (not model training).

US retail cross-check: [Kobo US](https://www.kobo.com/us/en/ebook/good-strategy-bad-strategy) explicitly lists “EPUB 2 (Adobe DRM).” Accessed 2026-09-10.

Accessibility has the strict school/disability/hard-copy prerequisites above.

The authors actively sell training/resources and are reachable rights stakeholders, making outreach more promising than an otherwise blind purchase test. If outreach fails or is slow, the $15.99 Kindle edition remains a post-purchase test.

## Decision rule for the purchasing workflow

For any `purchase-test-needed` title, a successful test requires Amazon to present an actual **Download EPUB** or **Download PDF** action in Manage Your Content and Devices for the purchased ASIN. “Send to Kindle,” “download to device,” offline reading in an app, an ACSM file, or an Adobe-authorized EPUB does not satisfy ingestibility. If Amazon does not present the file action, use the publisher-permission route rather than circumvention.
