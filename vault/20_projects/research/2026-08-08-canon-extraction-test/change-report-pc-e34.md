**Bible maintainability note:** After three eras the bible remains coherent because its skeleton — identity facts separated from evolution_events with evidence and reasoning — lets large changes (Pepper's aging, the black dress, whole new schools) land as auditable events rather than silent overwrites. However, contradiction pressure is accumulating in exactly the fields that were "settled" by evolution events: v2's hair-color evolution is contradicted by E34, the "standard later-era outfit" is now wrong on every visible page, and per-episode occasion wear (Saffron) risks being mis-registered as permanent redesign. The bible needs a structural distinction between era-scoped defaults ("as of E11–E16") and timeless facts, plus a rule that appearance evolutions require corroboration across two consecutive eras before overwriting the default — otherwise each new installment will keep relitigating cosmetic fields while the genuinely stable identity core (companions, school affiliations, personality, art color-scripting) is what actually holds the series together.
