## Buying shortlist

- **Best for ingestion:** buy *User Story Mapping* and *Product Roadmaps Relaunched* from eBooks.com; both listings explicitly provide DRM-free EPUB and PDF files.
- **Cheapest reading copies:** B&N NOOK for *Monetizing Innovation*, *Working Backwards*, and *EMPOWERED*; Apple Books for *High Output Management* and *Scaling People*. These are reading-only recommendations unless checkout/download proves an exportable DRM-free file.
- **Audio:** all seven titles have an unabridged audiobook. The lowest directly verified audio prices in this pass were $14.99 for *Monetizing Innovation*, *High Output Management*, and *EMPOWERED*; $19.23 for *User Story Mapping*; $19.99 for *Working Backwards* and *Product Roadmaps Relaunched*; and $21.99 for *Scaling People*.

## Source and comparison notes

Only publisher, author, and retailer product pages are used for purchase facts. Search-result snippets are discovery aids only, not final evidence.

- eBooks.com was visually checked in a US/USD browser session. Its pages explicitly displayed both the “DRM Free” badge and the file-level EPUB/PDF disclosures for the two O'Reilly titles.
- Barnes & Noble labels the selected products simply as “eBook” and routes reading through NOOK; because the cited pages do not state downloadable file type or DRM status, this note does not infer DRM-free status.
- Apple Books product pages identify price, publisher/seller, and—in audiobook listings—narrator and runtime, but do not disclose ebook exportability or DRM on the cited pages.
- Audible prices can vary by account, promotion, and membership state. Subscription trial prices were excluded; only visible “buy” prices are reported.
