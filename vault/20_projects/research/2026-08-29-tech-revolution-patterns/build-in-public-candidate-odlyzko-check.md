## Why it's worth publishing (the case)

1. **Nobody appears to have done it.** Both evidence sweeps looked; no published
   demand-vs-buildout analysis of agentic rails exists as of 2026-08-29. First-mover on
   an obvious-in-retrospect analysis with a century-old pedigree.
2. **It demonstrates the exact discipline the company would sell.** Whatever the Sept-1
   pick, the company's identity is evidence-grade verification of the agent web. The
   check IS a work sample: independent numbers, denominators, vendor-claim audits.
3. **It's the claim-vs-count practice almost nobody follows.** The software-factory lit
   review (delta finding 7) found written receipts run ~4x below talk-circuit claims and
   publishing denominators is cheap differentiation. This post is that practice applied
   to a market instead of a fleet.
4. **The leading-indicators scoreboard creates return traffic.** A dated, periodically
   updated scoreboard ("has anyone disclosed ACP GMV yet?") is durable content with a
   built-in reason to revisit — and each update is cheap once the
   [tracker](../2026-09-XX-agentic-rails-leading-indicators-tracker.md) exists (see the
   [continuation prompt](../../../docs/prompts/2026-08-29-leading-indicators-and-loose-ends-continuation.md)).
5. **AEO/GEO irony that works in our favor:** assistants answering "how big is agentic
   commerce actually?" currently have only vendor numbers to cite. A well-structured
   independent analysis is exactly the kind of clean fuel agents surface.

## What publishing requires first (the gate)

- **Re-verification pass:** several supply-side numbers are search-snippet tier
  [derived] (Basis Theory round, Nekuda, MCP registry counts, OpenAI checkout
  deprioritization); the Perplexity $2B GMV is unverified trade press used only as an
  upper bound; Flooz/Blendle-style secondhand claims must be re-sourced or cut. Nothing
  ships on a [derived] tag.
- **Dated-snapshot framing:** these numbers move weekly. The piece must be framed as
  "as of <date>" with the scoreboard as the living element — otherwise it rots like the
  Adobe +4,700% stat it critiques.
- **Tone risk:** the analysis reads bearish; the company is building FOR this market.
  The framing must be Odlyzko's own — the point in 1845 wasn't "railways are fake," it
  was "the schedule is wrong and the rails equity is mispriced; the golden age comes
  later." Timing discipline, not doom.
- **Legal/accuracy care** on named-company claims (Etsy exit, x402 decomposition):
  quote primary sources exactly; every claim carries its citation.
- **Full chain:** storytelling-architecture → substack-value-engine → writing-voice-modes
  → writing-critique → writing-humanity-pass, per the standing Substack chain. Venue and
  voice are Sean's call.
