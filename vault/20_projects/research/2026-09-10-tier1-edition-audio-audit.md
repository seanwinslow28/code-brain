# Tier-1 edition and audiobook audit

Scope: the fourteen books in **Book purchases, tier 1 (Sean)** plus the free *Shape Up* item. This is an edition/audio safety check, not a purchase record. Prices are spot prices visible on 2026-09-10, generally in the US store; they can change by account, region, or promotion.

## Important corrections

1. **Buy the 2026 second edition of *Obviously Awesome*, and choose the updated audiobook too.** April Dunford says old copies remain in circulation and instructs buyers to look for the yellow **“UPDATED AND EXPANDED”** cover mark. The new edition changes the method from ten steps to five and expands pre-work, differentiated value, multi-product positioning, and post-positioning execution. Paperback ISBN **9781999023058**; ebook ISBN **9781999023065**; audiobook ISBN **9781999023072**. The updated, author-narrated audiobook is real: unabridged, **2:49:42**, released February 27, 2026. Google Play showed a cash price of **$13.95**. Do not accidentally buy the 2019 print ISBN **9781999023003** or rely on an old audio-library entry with the same title. [Author announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aprildunford_announcement-the-new-edition-of-obviously-activity-7433152554546659328-jdp1), [author book page](https://www.aprildunford.com/books), [Google Play audiobook](https://play.google.com/store/audiobooks/details/Obviously_Awesome_How_to_Nail_Product_Positioning_?hl=en_US&id=AQAAAECaFWZV7M), [OverDrive metadata](https://lapl.overdrive.com/media/12959121), [Google Books second-edition record](https://books.google.com/books/about/Obviously_Awesome.html?id=dF3Z0QEACAAJ).

2. **The revised *Mom Test* is not due in December 2026.** The official publisher record now schedules the **Revised and Expanded / Completely Revised 2nd Edition for March 30, 2027** (144-page paperback ISBN **9798893312560**). Its unabridged audiobook is also a March 30, 2027 preorder, author-narrated, about **3.8 hours**, ISBN **9781668189160**, and Kobo showed **$15.99**. The current edition remains available now; its 2019 author-narrated unabridged recording is **3:50** and Spotify showed **$11.99**. Buy/listen now if timing matters; wait only if the refreshed examples and language are worth a six-and-a-half-month delay. [Official revised-edition page](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/The-Mom-Test/Rob-Fitzpatrick/9798893312560), [revised audio preorder](https://www.kobo.com/us/en/audiobook/the-mom-test), [current author store](https://www.momtestbook.com/), [current Spotify audiobook](https://open.spotify.com/show/2CtSEuimVcVKfLzOin8gRZ).

3. **No official unabridged audiobook was found for *Testing Business Ideas* or *Trustworthy Online Controlled Experiments*.** This is stronger than a generic web miss: Wiley lists *Testing Business Ideas* only as ebook and paperback, while Cambridge lists *Trustworthy Online Controlled Experiments* only as digital and paperback. Exact-title searches of Audible, Apple Books, Google Play, Kobo, and Spotify surfaced no publisher-authorized full audio editions. Short summaries and AI “capsules” are not the books. [Wiley product page](https://uat.store.wiley.com/en-us/testing-business-ideas-a-field-guide-for-rapid-experimentation-p-9781119551423), [Cambridge product page](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/trustworthy-online-controlled-experiments/D97B26382EB0EB2DC2019A7A7B518F59).

4. **The full text of *Scaling People* is not free online.** Stripe’s page is a purchase/companion-resource landing page: it exposes free workbooks and interviews, but not the complete book. Stripe links only $35 print purchases. A purchasable ebook is **$9.99** on Apple Books, and an unabridged audiobook exists: Claire Hughes Johnson narrates, **11:32**, **$21.99** on Apple Books (Audible also displayed a $20.39 cash option during this audit). [Stripe Press](https://press.stripe.com/scaling-people), [Apple ebook](https://books.apple.com/us/book/scaling-people/id6445743934), [Apple audiobook](https://books.apple.com/us/audiobook/scaling-people-tactics-for-management-and-company/id1684821018), [Audible audiobook](https://www.audible.com/es_US/pd/Scaling-People-Audiolibro/B0BXM9G44Z).

5. ***Shape Up* is genuinely free in full online and as a direct PDF; no authorized unabridged audiobook was found.** Basecamp offers the complete web book and its own PDF, plus a paid print edition. The Basecamp page does not advertise audio, and exact-title searches of the major audiobook stores returned podcasts, summaries, and discussions rather than a Ryan Singer audiobook. [Full web book](https://basecamp.com/shapeup), [official PDF](https://basecamp.com/shapeup/shape-up.pdf), [Basecamp books page](https://basecamp.com/remote-resources).

## Subscription-label warning

- Audible’s **“Try for $0.00”** and “free with 30-day trial” labels are membership offers. They are not a zero-dollar ownership price; use the separate **Buy for** amount when shown.
- Kobo’s **“free with Kobo Plus”** means included while the subscription remains active. The adjacent dollar amount is the cash purchase price.
- Everand’s “read free for 30 days” is likewise a trial, not a free ebook purchase.
- Library listings such as OverDrive are useful edition metadata, but borrowing is not a cash purchase and does not establish ownership.

## Confidence and remaining unknowns

- Edition corrections for *Obviously Awesome* and *The Mom Test*, and the positive audiobook records above, are high confidence because they come from authors, publishers, or retailer metadata.
- Negative audiobook findings are necessarily catalogue snapshots. For *Testing Business Ideas* and *Trustworthy Online Controlled Experiments*, publisher format lists plus five major-store searches support “no official unabridged edition found as of 2026-09-10.” For *Shape Up*, Basecamp’s own format choices and the same store sweep support the same conclusion.
- Audiobook prices are volatile and region/account dependent. Recheck the cash **Buy** line immediately before purchase.
