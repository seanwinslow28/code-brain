# Tier 1 rights and independent-format audit

**Scope:** US storefronts; access date **2026-09-10**. No purchase, subscription, account login, form submission, or rights-holder contact was made.

**Purpose:** Determine whether three independently published Productcraft canon titles have a lawful, agent-ingestible source, and identify the least-risk next step where the full book is still unavailable.

## How to read the outcome labels

- **verified-ingestible** — the complete book text is available in a directly downloadable, parseable, authorized format. This label does **not** grant permission to redistribute it or upload it to an unrelated third-party AI service. Official workbooks and excerpts are recorded as partial sources but do not qualify the complete book for this label.
- **purchase-test-needed** — Amazon's public product page does not disclose the information needed to know whether the purchaser will receive the 2026 EPUB/PDF download control. A verified purchaser would have to inspect **Manage Your Content and Devices** after purchase, or obtain confirmation from the rights holder before buying.
- **outreach-needed** — no usable public/direct path was verified, but a public rights-holder contact route exists.
- **no-path-found** — neither a usable source nor a public permission route was found.

## Amazon's 2026 rule applies to all three titles

Amazon's official KDP documentation says that, beginning **2026-01-20**, verified purchasers can download EPUB and PDF files for Kindle books whose publishers have chosen the DRM-free option. The files are obtained from **Manage Your Content and Devices**; Kindle Unlimited and other borrowers are excluded. For a title published before **2025-12-09** that was already DRM-free, the publisher must actively reconfirm the no-DRM choice before Amazon will enable EPUB/PDF downloads. Amazon also says a setting change can take 24–72 hours to appear. [Amazon KDP, “Digital Rights Management,” accessed 2026-09-10](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GDDXGH9VR22ACM8U)

The three public Amazon product pages inspected on 2026-09-10 did **not** expose a field saying “DRM-free,” “EPUB/PDF download enabled,” or equivalent. Features such as “screen reader supported,” Kindle page count, file size, and simultaneous-device limits are not evidence of an exportable file. Consequently, Amazon status is **unknowable before purchase from the public listings alone**. This is a limitation of the public listing, not evidence that the books do or do not use DRM.

Do not assume that ordinary Kindle access, screen-reader support, or a Kobo purchase makes the text exportable.

## Recommended sequence

1. **Immediately ingest the _Scaling People Workbooks_ PDF** as a clearly labeled first-party companion source.
2. **Index the official _Obviously Awesome_ 2026 articles/podcast pages and the _Continuous Discovery Habits_ Book Club guides** as companion material. Do not describe either corpus as the complete book.
3. **Send narrowly scoped permission requests** to April Dunford/Ambient Press, Product Talk, and Stripe Press before purchasing. The request should ask both about file delivery and permitted AI processing; “DRM-free” answers the technical-access question, not necessarily the license question.
4. If rights holders do not confirm, decide whether the uncertainty is worth three controlled Kindle purchases. For each purchase, inspect **Manage Your Content and Devices** for Amazon's EPUB/PDF control. A missing download button means the file is not available through Amazon's verified-purchaser feature at that time; it does not authorize DRM removal.
