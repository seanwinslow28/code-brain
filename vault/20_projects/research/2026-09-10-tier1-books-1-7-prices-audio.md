### Bottom line on audio

Five of the seven have verified unabridged English audiobooks: *Good Strategy Bad Strategy*, the updated 2026 *Obviously Awesome*, *Continuous Discovery Habits*, *The Mom Test*, and *Hacking Growth*. No audiobook edition was found for the highly visual/card-based *Testing Business Ideas* or for *Trustworthy Online Controlled Experiments*.

## Method and caveats

- “DRM-free” is reported only when the seller or publisher explicitly says so. Where the storefront does not disclose DRM, the format is labelled uncertain.
- “Audiobook exists” means an edition sold as an audiobook, not merely available through a subscription catalogue. Runtime, narrator, and abridgement status come from the publisher or retailer edition metadata.
- “Cheapest” means the lowest visible, currently purchasable new copy found among reputable U.S. publisher/retailer pages checked during this pass; it is not a guarantee that no transient coupon or local-store price is lower.
- Storefronts checked as applicable: the author/publisher, Google Play Books, Barnes & Noble, Kobo, Audible, Audiobooks.com, Downpour, Bookshop.org, Target, and Walmart. Search results were rejected when they were used/third-party marketplace copies, subscription-only, the wrong language or edition, unavailable, or of uncertain legitimacy.
