This week in animation and AI, a storm is brewing around **Amazon MGM** after a major animated AI announcement while **YouTube** continues its attempted clampdown on slop.

Let’s get into it!

## Streaming

This week **Amazon MGM Studios** announced a new initiative called the **GenAI Creators Fund** and with it the greenlight of three AI-assisted animated series including **Punky Duck** from **Jorge Gutierrez** (**Book of Life**), **Cupcake & Friends** from **BuzzFeed Studios**, and **Love, Diana Music Hunters** created by **Albie Hecht**. The program is designed to produce shows with the use of AI tools alongside traditional tools like **Blender** and **Maya**. ([via The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/tv/tv-news/amazon-orders-three-animated-series-generative-ai-1236606219/))

The blowback against **Amazon** was instant and intense. First, **Loryn Brantz**, the creator of **Cuppy** from **BuzzFeed** ’s **Cupcake & Friends**, made it clear she is not involved in the AI incarnation of her character in any way and is vehemently opposed to it saying:

> *“They repeatedly assured me in good faith that they would never do anything with Cuppy without my input, yet offered me no legal options, insisting I would never need them. The news that this character, who is based on my own personality and whom I created as a microphone to spread love and positivity, has been taken and turned into a soulless AI puppet feels like my intestines being pulled out of my body.”*

([via c21 Media](https://www.c21media.net/news/cuppy-creator-slams-buzzfeed-studios-over-amazon-ai-animation-based-on-character/))

And just days after the announcement, **Jorge Gutierrez** has walked away from the project altogether, citing a severe fan backlash that he said included death threats against him and his family. In a statement, he said in part:

> *“My intent was to showcase artists, both new and seasoned, both inside and outside the studios, driving this new tech. My sincerest apology to those I upset. I promise to do better moving forward. Thank you for your patience with me. I will try harder.”*

([via Cartoon Brew](https://www.cartoonbrew.com/artificial-intelligence/jorge-gutierrez-amazon-mgm-punky-duck-261798.html))

On the other end of the spectrum, **YouTube** has announced that it will redouble its efforts to identify AI-generated content on its platform. The company will make the tags for AI much more prominent and will begin to automatically apply the label to content it determines to have *“significant photorealistic AI use.”* ([via Variety](https://variety.com/2026/digital/news/youtube-ai-video-labels-automatic-detection-1236758865/))

## Film

Meanwhile, **Pixar** continues to take a refreshingly subdued and practical approach to AI technology. At an advance press screening for **Toy Story 5** the creative team discussed the use of AI at the studio. Co-director **McKenna Harris** noted, “ *Pixar is a technology company first, and* t *hings are changing. Pixar is figuring out how to change along with it.”* Director **Andrew Stanton** added:

> *“We’ve had machine learning since the beginning of Pixar as far as figuring things out for you occasionally, but it’s always been to make the tools work better. I have no interest in doing anything but working smarter, faster with another artist.”*

([via Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/news/toy-story-5-aging-woody-lilypad-1236750424/))

The **Tribeca Film Festival** has announced its decision to screen the entirely AI-generated feature film **Dreams of Violets** at the festival in June. The film centers around decades of Iranian civilian resistance to the regime. Tribeca’s co-founder **Jane Rosenthal** defended the inclusion of the film by calling it, *“a powerful example of how emerging technologies like AI can be used not simply as tools of innovation, but as vehicles for deeply human storytelling.”* ([via CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/news/entertainment/ai-generated-film-tribeca-9.7215627))

## Gaming

While the debate rages around AI-use in film and television, it continues to be adopted in games at a rapid pace. Despite recent financial troubles (or perhaps because of them), **Ubisoft** (**Assassin’s Creed**) is doubling down on AI, accelerating work on its first *“playable Generative AI experience.”* ([via Tech Radar](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/ubisoft-confirms-new-assassins-creed-far-cry-and-ghost-recon-games-are-in-the-works-and-will-launch-by-march-2029-alongside-the-first-playable-generative-ai-experience))

And **HoYoverse**, the creators of **Genshin Impact**, is reportedly planning to invest up to $14.6 billion in AI technology over the next three years. ([via Gaming on Phone Biz](https://www.gamingonphonebiz.com/hoyoverse-plans-14-6-billion-ai-investment-over-three-years-signals-push-toward-full-stack-ai-development/))

## VFX

Finally, VFX powerhouse **Digital Domain** has unveiled a new approach to incorporating AI across visual effects called **DDAI**. The effort looks to combine AI tools with their existing CGI workflows with an initial focus on the company’s visualization team. ([via Animation Magazine](https://www.animationmagazine.net/2026/05/digital-domain-launches-ai-integrated-vfx-infrastructure-ddai/))
