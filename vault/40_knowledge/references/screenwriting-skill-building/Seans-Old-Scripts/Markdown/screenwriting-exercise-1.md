INT. BIG VALUE RETURNS COUNTER \- DAY

The automatic door fights itself — half-open, shudders,  
gives up. DENNIS turns sideways to fit through.

He carries a grimy JUICER in both arms. It gets stuck in   
the door for a moment. He awkwardly tries to wrestle it   
through the door, making a disturbing amount of noise.

The clerk watches Dennis struggle and does nothing.   
He just stares at him, deadpan. No fucks given.

Dennis finally gets the juicer through, snapping a piece off  
In the process. He looks down. Picks It up, and tries to   
Piece it together. No dice. 

He walks up to the counter and  
sets the juicer down. He places the broken piece next to it  
And pushes it forward. Pauses for a beat,  
Then puts the piece on top of the juicer. 

The clerk stares at him with his baggy raccoon eyes.  
Eye lids half mast.

Dennis opens his mouth to speak.  
A flake of dried pulp breaks loose,  
drifts, lands on the counter. He brushes it away. 

The clerk looks at the juicer. Looks at Dennis. SNIFFS.

Dennis grabs the juicer, CHUCKLES, turns, and hustles toward the door.  
Grime and dried pulp trail behind him.

The broken piece falls off. He goes to grab it,   
then immediately shakes his head  
And continues for the door. 

He tries to squeeze through the door.   
The juicer gets stuck.
