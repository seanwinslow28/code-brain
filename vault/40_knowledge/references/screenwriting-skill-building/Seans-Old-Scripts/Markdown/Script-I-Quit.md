# Script I Quit

<p align="right"><i>FADE IN:</i></p>

## INT. LAW FIRM - DAY

**NARRATOR (V.O)**
> Gary was a very successful lawyer who spent a lot of time at the office.

GARY BRIGHT walks out of the elevator with a group of people around him handing papers to each other. He's talking on his cellphone spouting out important lawyer jargon with a briefcase in his other hand.

**GARY**
> Yes, the Feldman case has been handled. Piece of cake. Yes. uhh filibuster. Yes. Mhm. Plea Bargain. Yup.

**NARRATOR (V.O)**
> But his busy work life kept him from his family.

## INT. GARYS HOUSE - DAY

Gary's wife, LINDA, puts her jacket on by the front door. She has a set of keys and a baseball glove in her hands.

**LINDA**
> C'mon, time to go, Tommy!

TOMMY, Gary's son, runs downstairs wearing his baseball uniform.

**TOMMY**
> But, Dad's not here yet.

**LINDA**
> Dad said he's going to meet us there.

**NARRATOR (V.O)**
> Linda has been lying to her son for too long. And she's tired of it.

## INT. GARYS BEDROOM - NIGHT

Gary and Linda are in their pajamas getting ready for bed.

**LINDA (CONT'D)**
> I'm tired of lying to our son, Gary! You've been missing his important childhood events for too long now.

**GARY**
> I know. I know. I'm sorry. The office is a mad house. I'm this close to                     ____ cracking the Fredrickson case.

**LINDA**
> You literally say that every single time I confront you about this problem. Who's Fredrickson? Why is his or her case forever uncracked? Things have to change.

Linda gets into bed. Gary sits on his side of the bed and ponders.

## INT. COURT HOUSE - DAY

Gary's organizing the papers in his briefcase. His cellphone's ringing and few people are around him talking and passing papers back and forth.

**NARRATOR (V.O)**
> It was then that Gary realized things needed to change.

Gary stands up.

**GARY**
> That's it! There are more important things in life than work!

**JUDGE**
> Order! Order in the court! You have one more outburst like that and you're out of here, Gary!

Gary throws his cellphone against the wall and tosses his briefcase at the jury. Papers fly everywhere as Gary runs out of the court.

**GARY**
> I love my family!

## INT. GARYS HOUSE - DAY

Linda and Tommy are eating at the dinner table. Gary barges in.

**GARY**
> Honey! I quit!

Linda and Tommy jump with joy and they have a group hug.

**NARRATOR (V.O)**
> And so Gary, Linda and Tommy got to spend a lot more time together as a family.

**1 Year Later.**

## INT. KITCHEN - DAY

Gary and Linda argue in the kitchen. Bills are all over the table. Gary's drunk and smoking a cigarette.

**LINDA**
> The bills are piling up! This is getting ridiculous! I can't do this on my own!

**GARY**
> I've tried to get my job back! No law firm will hire me after the stunt I pulled!

**LINDA**
> Maybe you should have thought of that before you quit!

Beat. Gary lunges at Linda.

**GARY**
> You god damn bi--

## EXT. BASEBALL FIELD - DAY

Tommy's at bat. Gary's in the stands with a beer in his hand.

**GARY**
> C'mon, Tommy! You got this!

Tommy swings and misses.

**GARY (CONT'D)**
> *(angry)*
> God dammit, Tommy! Is this what I've been missing?! Is this what I threw my career away for?!

**This film really opened my eyes. - Newsweek**

**I wanted to go out and quit my job. Then I immediately reconsidered. - New York Times**

**I'm going to re-write Hook. - Steven Spielberg**

Tommy swings and misses.

**GARY (CONT'D)**
> *(sighs)*
> Jesus Christ.

## INT. KITCHEN - DAY

Gary's crying at the table drinking straight from the bottle. In one hand, a picture of his family. In the other, a picture of his briefcase and cell phone.

**NARRATOR (V.O)**
> It was at this point Gary realized he made a huge mistake.

**GARY**
> I've made a huge mistake.

**Family First**
