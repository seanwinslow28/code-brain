**INT. DENNIS'S KITCHEN \- NIGHT**

Dennis sets a stolen juicer on the counter. Still has the  
BIG VALUE sticker on it. He feeds it colorful foreign fruits, a fistfuls  
of kale, various powders, nuts, seeds, and a single mushroom cap.

Dennis presses the start button, WINCES, and shields his face.  
It WORKS. The juicer cranks and grinds.  
Dennis’ slowly puts his arm down. Eyes glimmer with hope.  
Thick gold sludge fills the glass.

He drinks. Closes his eyes. Pure bliss.  
Behind him, seven more juicers line the counter, all stickered.

We hear the front door open. Dennis’ wife BETH walks into the kitchen  
dressed in full workout attire carrying a large duffle bag.  
She’s sweaty and has a hickey on her neck. 

BETH  
Wha- What the hell’s with all of these juicers, Dennis?  
What kind of moron buys seven juicers? You’re unbelievable,  
Where are we going to put all of these? There’s no goddamn  
room in basement because of all of your Star Wars  
(air quotes)  
Collectables. God, Alejandro’s right.   
I should jus–

Beth’s voice trails off. Dennis’ eyes remain shut as he sips through  
her shrill nagging. His smile is real. 

Today, we spell victory: D-E-N-N-I-S.

**EXT. BUS STOP \- DAY**

Dennis sits on the bench, seven juicers in a trash bag  
at his feet. The bus pulls up. Doors open.

Dennis awkwardly tosses the bag over his shoulder.  
Juicers bashing against his back. The heavy boxes  
edges poke and prod, but Dennis is all smiles.

He walks on the bus, but jerks back. He turns around.  
The bag is stuck in the door. He tugs on it a couple of times.  
Dennis’ smile fades and his eyes fill with fear.  
He aggressively tugs and pulls, making  
An absurd amount of noise.
