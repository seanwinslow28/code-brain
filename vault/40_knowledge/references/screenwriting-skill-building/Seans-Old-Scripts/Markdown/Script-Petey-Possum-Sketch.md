# Script Petey Possum Sketch

## EXT. PETEY POSSUMS - DAY

A CHILD SCREAMS and LAUGHS.

**PARENT(O.S)**
> Beth. Get. Get down. Beth. I swear to CHRIST (Bill Burr shriek)

## INT. PETEY POSSUMS - PARTY ROOM - DAY

CHILDREN slam their fists on a long table with a devoured cake in the middle.

**CHILDREN**
> PETEY! PETEY! PETEY!

JACOB, a husky youth/birthday boy, sits at the head of the table.

**JACOB**
> Where's PETEY!?!

Dante's mother, GAIL, nibbles on cake.

**GAIL**
> He'll be out soon, Jacob. Why don't you--

Dante foams at the mouth. Gail stares in horror.

BEN, a Petey Possum Employee, grabs a microphone.

**BEN**
> Hey kids! Who's ready to meet PETEY!

The kids lose their shit. Ben looks at his phone.

**BEN (CONT'D)**
> Uh oh. Looks like he's running a little late.

The kids lose their shit.

**RANDOM CHILD**
> Get off the stage fart face!

**BEN**
> you’re a fart face. Ho-oh-oh okay. Don't you worry. He's on his way now! Let's see what ol’ Peteys up to.

Ben points to the TV with a shit-eating grin on his face.

CUT TO: Television with a Possum dancing in front of a colorful background.

## INT. PETEYS BEDROOM - DAY

An alarm goes off. Petey Possum shoots out of bed.

**PETEY**
> Oh jeez! I'm going to be late to (Dante is said in a monotone computer voice) Birthday! Don't worry buddy, I'm on my way

CUT TO: Possum dancing in front of a colorful background.

## EXT. PETEYS HOUSE - DAY

The car in the driveway REVS and SMOKES.

## INT. PETEYS CAR - CONTINUOUS

**PETEY (CONT'D)**
> Aw shucks. Oh well. When in doubt. Take your local bus route!

CUT TO: Possum dancing in front of a colorful background.

## INT. BUS - DAY

Petey sits on the bus. A PASSENGER approaches Petey.

**PASSENGER**
> Hey. You're in my seat, pal.

WIDE SHOT: Empty bus.

**PETEY**
> Hey now. There's no need for any hostility. Why don't you take the seat next to me and we can--

The Passenger takes out a switchblade.

CUT TO: Possum dancing in front of a colorful background.

## INT. AMBULANCE - DAY

AMBULANCE SIREN goes off.

Petey lays on a bed with blood squirting all over the place.

**EMT**
> I'm sorry!! It's my first day!!

CUT TO: Possum dancing in front of a colorful background.

## INT. HOSPITAL - DAY

A heart monitor BEEPS.

Petey lays in a hospital bed. A DOCTOR stands next to him reading a clip board.

**PETEY**
> Please! I've got to get out of here, doc!

**DOCTOR**
> You've lost a significant amount of blood. You'll need to rest here for a few days then you can--

**PETEY**
> No. You don’t understand. These kids. These kids rely on me. I’m not just providing a silly little service. I’m providing a moment of happiness. In times of struggle, they can look back and remember that things… things weren’t always so hard. Ya see, doc. Just as you were born to sustain life. I was born to remind people that their lives are worth living.

The Doctor wipes away a tear.

**DOCTOR**
> God damn. Who needs blood with you've got all that heart. Get out of here, Petey.

Cut to: Static TV

## INT. PETEY POSSUMS - PARTY ROOM - DAY

Ben still has a shit-eating grin.

**BEN**
> Oh! I think I hear the tap tap tappin of Petey's little paws on the other side of the door!

The kids lose their shit.

**BEN (CONT'D)**
> Ladies and Gentleman! Boys a girls! Heeeeeeeerrreee'ssss PETEY!

PETEY comes out in a gown.

**PETEY**
> Sorry, I’m late, everyone! I had... quite the adventure. Now. Where’s the birthday boy?

Jacob walks up and stares at PETEY.

Beat.

Jacob spits in his face.

**JACOB**
> You're not Petey, you're just a wannabe who looks like him!

The CHILDREN and the PARENTS laugh.

Closeup of PETEY as the spit slides down his bruised face.
