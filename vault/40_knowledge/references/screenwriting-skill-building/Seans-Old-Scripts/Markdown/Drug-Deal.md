# Drug Deal

## EXT. PARK - DAY

PARK GOERS walk, lounge around, and play. A few YOUNG GIRLS jump rope.

Girls go to college to get more knowledge;Boys go to Jupiter to get more stupider--

GERALD (20's), gaunt with an overall "I've given up" look, sits on a park bench next to RALPH (30's), a large sketchy- looking man with a beanie and a hoodie. High-p itched voice.

**RALPH**
> Get more stupider? Glad that college education's paying off.

**GERALD**
> Yea, uh. Why are we doing this here?

**RALPH**
> It's friggin' gorgeous out here! Just wanted to, yanno. Switch up the... the ambiance.

SHOT OF A FUNNY THING that you would see at a park. Maybe a homeless man pooping. Ralph SIGHS. Gerald raises an eyebrow.

**RALPH**
> I gotta be real with you, Ger.

**GERALD**
> Ger?

**RALPH**
> I had to bamboozle ya. I didn't. I didn't want you making a scene.

**GERALD**
> Why would I be making a scene?

Ralph takes out a piece of paper.

**RALPH**
> *(clears throat)*
> I miss our talks. I miss our walks. I miss ou--

**GERALD**
> What is this? What's happening here?

Ralph puts the paper down.

**RALPH**
> I'm interventioning. I'm. I'm intervening on you. On your drug problem.

**GERALD**
> You're... You're providing me with those drugs.

**RALPH**
> Let's not make this about me, Ger.

Beat.

**GERALD**
> Alright, I'm out of here.

Gerald attempts to stand, but he's handcuffed to the bench.

**GERALD (CONT'D)**
> What the f--

Ralph holds up a key.

**RALPH**
> Did you forget that I'm an amateur magician in my off-hours?

**GERALD**
> That's literally never come up in conversation.

**RALPH**
> I've turned a corner, Gerry. I realized the harm I've been causing.

**GERALD**
> That's good to hear, but--

**RALPH**
> I'm strictly selling weed now.

**GERALD**
> I'm happy for you, man, but I--

**RALPH**
> And psychedelics too. Sometimes.

**GERALD**
> Listen to me. You're wasting your time...
> *(chuckling)*
> I don't have a problem.

**RALPH**
> I was afraid you'd say that.

Ralph puts his phone to his ear.

**RALPH (CONT'D)**
> Ok. Come over.

**GERALD**
> Who was that? Who's coming over?!

**RALPH**
> I found out where your family lives and told them about the situation.

**GERALD**
> You got my family involved?! Why do you think I'm addicted to drugs in the first place?!

**RALPH**
> Ha! So you admit you have an addiction! One step closer, buddy.

**GERALD**
> You have to get me out of these cuffs. You have no ide--

**BETTY(O.S)**
> Ya fuckin' kiddin me, Gerald?!

BETTY, Geralds Mom, walks over with HANK, Geralds Brother. Hank pushes Geralds grandmother, LOLA, in her wheelchair.

**GERALD**
> *(sighs)*
> Son of a bitch.

**BETTY**
> Ya doin drugs now?!

**RALPH**
> Thank you for coming, Mrs. Galamad--

**BETTY**
> And I have to find out from this shmuck?

**RALPH**
> ... Alright.

**GERALD**
> I don't know what this guy told you, Ma, but I'm perfectly fine.

**RALPH**
> --This guy?--       ___

**BETTY**
> You look like human garbage.
> *(to Hank)*
> Tell him, Hank.

**HANK**
> Yea, human garbage.

**BETTY**
> Look at ya grandmotha. Fuckin' distraught.

SHOT OF: Lola looks old and decrepit.

**LOLA**
> EHHHHHHGGGGG

**RALPH**
> Oh my god--

**HANK**
> Beside herself.

**BETTY**
> What do you have to say for yaself, huh?

**HANK**
> Yea, what do you have to say?

**BETTY**
> Huh?

**HANK**
> Huh?!

**GERALD**
> ALRIGHT!!

Everyone's silent.

**GERALD (CONT'D)**
> I will check into a rehab and get myself straightened out, ok?! Are ya happy!? Beat.

**BETTY**
> *(endearing)*
> That's all we ask, sweetie.

**HANK**
> Yea, that's all we ask.

SHOT OF: Lola, looking decrepit.

**GERALD**
> Ralph.

**RALPH**
> Yea?

**GERALD**
> Please uncuff me.

**RALPH**
> Oh, yea. My bad.

Ralph fiddles with the keys. It takes a moment.

**RALPH (CONT'D)**
> *(chuckles)*
> These things. They can be pretty tricky.

Ralph uncuffs Gerald.

**RALPH (CONT'D)**
> There we go. Ya free! heh.

**RALPH (CONT'D)**
> I'm happy for you, Gerry! Florida livin's the best! I got a cousin out there! Stone crabs for days!

**RALPH (CONT'D)**

**RALPH (CONT'D)**
> Hey. You, uh. You kids ever try pot?
