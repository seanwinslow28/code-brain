You write a post, an email, a LinkedIn update. Claude helped. Fine.

But you re-read it and **something feels off.**

It doesn’t sound like you.

Polished, sure. Technically correct.

**But it reads like content, not like a person.**

You swap “leverage” for “use.”

**Still flat.**

You publish anyway.

**That’s the AI Voice Trap.**

The problem isn’t that you used AI.

It’s that **AI text has fingerprints** — and most people don’t know how to remove them.

Here’s what’s worth knowing: **AI-assisted content that is well-edited performs 12% better in AI search citations than purely human-written content.**

But only when the human layer actually changes something. That’s exactly what this skill does. [Presenc AI](https://presenc.ai/research/ai-content-creation-statistics)

---

### 🔍 What it does

Most “humanize” tools just swap vocabulary.

Replace “leverage” with “use,” done.

**humanAIzer works in three layers.**

**Layer 1:** vocabulary blacklist.

**Layer 2:** structural pattern diagnosis — catching things like the mechanical “It’s not X, it’s Y” structure, flat sentence rhythm, false balance.

**Layer 3:** the rare one. It puts soul back in — opinions, uncertainty, first person, specific feelings. Because clean-but-voiceless writing is still dead writing.

**One input. Three layers fixed.**

---

### ⚡ When to use it

Any time you’ve used AI to draft something and the output **doesn’t quite sound like you** — before you publish, send, or post it.

Useful if you are:

- Founder
- Content Creator
- Newsletter Writer
- Copywriter
- Solopreneur
- Marketing Manager

It won’t replace your editing instinct. It just makes it faster.
