### Get ahead of emerging markets with micro-industries

Micro-industries help you move beyond broad industry classifications to identify emerging niche markets, define precise ICPs, and uncover opportunities before they become obvious. With more than 5,100 micro-industries available in Crunchbase, you'll gain a more granular view of where new opportunities are taking shape.

**Available in:** API (micro\_categories endpoint) and UI (Crunchbase Business)
