## Definition

This concept defines 'slop' not as low-quality content, but as the erosion of trust caused by a circulation loop that fails to function as a learning loop. When agents generate content without mechanisms for automated truth maintenance, the architecture becomes a self-referential echo chamber rather than a knowledge accumulator. This deficit forces Sean to manually audit outputs against high-fidelity baselines to detect drift.

## Context

Sean's ability to trust his vault depends on the integrity of the synthesis loop. If the system circulates stale or low-signal concepts, it undermines the strategic value of the entire infrastructure.

## Evidence

> The architecture is currently a circulation loop, not necessarily a learning loop.

> He should periodically audit the 'taste' of his outputs against a small, high-fidelity baseline to detect drift.

## Examples

- Without a mechanism for automated truth maintenance, the vault's value per unit of effort declines as scale increases.
