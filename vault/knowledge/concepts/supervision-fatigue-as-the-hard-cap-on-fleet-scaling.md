## Definition

This mechanism defines the limit of agentic scalability imposed by human cognitive bandwidth, where the ability to audit and correct agent output degrades faster than the volume of automated work increases. As the fleet grows in complexity and frequency, the supervisory load becomes unsustainable, leading to a state where critical review agents are neglected or allowed to go stale. The system hits a hard cap not on processing power, but on the availability of high-fidelity human oversight required to maintain semantic accuracy.

## Context

Sean’s role as the ultimate arbiter of his vault's quality is the bottleneck. When he stops actively engaging with the critic agent, the entire synthesis pipeline loses its grounding in his current strategic intent. This concept explains why scaling automation without scaling supervision leads to systemic decay rather than efficiency.
