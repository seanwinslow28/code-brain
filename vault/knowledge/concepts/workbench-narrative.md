## Definition

A Workbench Narrative is a method of presenting technical work that reveals the builder's taste and principles through the feedback loops they have made visible and manipulable. It moves beyond showing tools to showing a feedback loop becoming visible, manipulable, and morally necessary, thereby arguing about the working conditions for agentic builders. The mechanism here is that the artifact makes feedback visible at the moment of decision, enforcing principles like zero-cost overnight learning loops and intent specs that stop agents from optimizing the wrong thing. This allows the builder to write beyond tool descriptions into arguments about the principles their tools enforce.
