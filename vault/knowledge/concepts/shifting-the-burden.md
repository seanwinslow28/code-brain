## Definition

This system archetype describes a structural dependency where a symptomatic quick fix provides immediate relief but actively undermines the capacity for a fundamental solution. In Sean's context, using AI to generate code or content acts as a painkiller that reduces the immediate pressure of production, yet it atrophies his ability to perform the underlying discipline required for long-term success. The dependency on the shortcut grows until the fundamental skill is lost, creating a feedback loop where the user becomes less capable of solving the problem without the tool.

## Context

Sean's reliance on AI for curriculum creation and agent orchestration risks eroding his own product judgment and systems thinking capabilities. If he does not actively maintain the 'fundamental' discipline of writing less code, he will become dependent on the tool's output quality, losing the ability to distinguish good design from mere automation.

> Writing as little code as possible is identified as the single most urgent discipline in software development in 2026.

- Automating synthesis tasks that require deep human judgment and taste
