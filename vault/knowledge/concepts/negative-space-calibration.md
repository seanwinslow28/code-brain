## Definition

This mechanism defines strategic gaps by their absence or pain points rather than by the solutions they might invite, anchoring analysis in verified user problems instead of agent-generated opportunities. It requires that every strong gap presentation explicitly states the need or pain first, ensuring the gap is framed as a deficit in knowledge or capability rather than an opening for feature creation. This calibration prevents the drift toward 'solutioneering' by treating gap maps as a rigorous hygiene check for knowledge deficits, maintaining signal-to-noise ratio and preventing the accumulation of low-validity content.
