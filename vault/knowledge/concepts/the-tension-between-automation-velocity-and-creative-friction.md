## Definition

This concept defines the structural conflict where systems optimized for maximum throughput inadvertently suppress the 'negative capability' required for original insight. When agentic workflows eliminate all procedural resistance, they remove the productive interruptions that force deeper reinterpretation of intent. The mechanism operates by smoothing out the cognitive roughness necessary for creative agency, turning complex navigation into simple execution.

## Context

Sean's current infrastructure prioritizes speed and reliability in his vault synthesis runs, but this efficiency risks creating a feedback loop where automation replaces critical thinking rather than augmenting it. He must consciously engineer constraints to prevent his systems from becoming too smooth, ensuring that creative oversight remains distinct from automated execution.
