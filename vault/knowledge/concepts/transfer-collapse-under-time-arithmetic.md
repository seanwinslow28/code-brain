## Definition

This mechanism describes the failure of knowledge to transfer across domains when the cognitive cost of re-contextualizing information exceeds the time available for deep processing. It is not a deficit in content coverage, but a structural inability to map concepts from a familiar, high-fidelity context (like Sean's own fleet) to an unfamiliar one because the 'time arithmetic' of learning new mappings is too steep. The learner defaults to surface-level recognition rather than deep application, causing the knowledge to collapse when faced with novel constraints.

## Context

Sean's job hunt and creative studio work require applying his agentic engineering skills to entirely new contexts (e.g., a new employer's stack). If he relies on artifacts that only reinforce his existing fleet's patterns, he will suffer transfer collapse when interviewing or building for others.
