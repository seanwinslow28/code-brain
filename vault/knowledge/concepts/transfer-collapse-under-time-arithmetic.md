## Definition

This mechanism describes the structural failure of knowledge transfer when the cognitive cost of re-contextualizing information exceeds the available time for deep processing. It is not a deficit in content coverage, but an inability to map concepts from a familiar, high-fidelity context to an unfamiliar one because the 'time arithmetic' of learning new mappings is too steep. The learner defaults to surface-level recognition rather than deep application, causing the knowledge to collapse when faced with novel constraints.

## Context

Sean's job hunt and creative studio work require applying his agentic engineering skills to entirely new contexts, such as a new employer's stack. If he relies on artifacts that only reinforce his existing fleet's patterns, he will suffer transfer collapse when interviewing or building for others, mistaking familiarity with the tooling for mastery of the underlying principles.
