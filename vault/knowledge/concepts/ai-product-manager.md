## Definition

This role represents a structural shift where the product manager transitions from managing model-as-feature or model-as-platform to operating as the manager of semi-autonomous software teams. The core value proposition lies in designing the operating model for these agentic systems, requiring ownership of the failure taxonomy, eval set, acceptance threshold, and release gate rather than just the roadmap. This distinction separates the role from generic prompt fluency or traditional product management by focusing on the architecture of agent-fleet reliability and accountability.
