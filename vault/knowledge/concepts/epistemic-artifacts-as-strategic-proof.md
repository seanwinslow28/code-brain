## Definition

This mechanism identifies how raw, unpolished creative outputs serve as verifiable evidence of human agency and intent in automated systems. Unlike final rendered assets which can be generated by any capable model, artifacts that retain 'construction lines,' 'paper grain,' or 'hole-punch marks' function as cryptographic proofs of the creator's physical presence and decision-making process. These artifacts shift the value proposition from the result (which is commoditized) to the provenance (which is unique), allowing the creator to leverage their specific history and taste as a differentiator against synthetic homogeneity.

## Context

Sean uses his 'ships-with-cheese' writing samples and pencil tests not just as portfolio pieces, but as strategic proof of his 'provably mine' status. This concept explains why he insists on saving 'flops' and 'rerolls': they are the data points that prove the human was in the loop, countering the 'Illusion of Competence' in AI outputs.
