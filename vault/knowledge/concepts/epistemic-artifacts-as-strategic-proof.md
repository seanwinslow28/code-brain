## Definition

This mechanism redefines portfolio items from mere execution records into evidence of strategic judgment. By treating code and documentation as artifacts that preserve decision traces, the creator demonstrates how they navigate uncertainty rather than just displaying technical proficiency. This shifts the signal from operational speed to diagnostic capability, allowing the work to serve as proof of higher-order reasoning skills. The artifact becomes a boundary object that translates creative studio output into job-hunt signaling by highlighting the 'why' behind the 'what'.

## Context

Sean faces the risk of appearing merely operational in his job hunt. By framing his vault and code as epistemic artifacts, he can bypass the 'too junior' filter by showing how he diagnoses problems and makes architectural choices under uncertainty.

## Evidence

> This artifact is not proof that I know X; it is proof that I notice Y, revise Z, and preserve decision trace Q.

> The tension lies between the need to demonstrate technical depth and the risk of appearing merely operational.

## Examples

- Rewriting portfolio descriptions to focus on decision traces and judgments made, not just the features shipped.
- Shifting interview narratives from 'what I built' to 'how I diagnosed the problem and why I chose this architecture over alternatives.'
