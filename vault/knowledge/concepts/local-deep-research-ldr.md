## Definition

A cost-capped agentic workflow where a local inference engine, such as a 14-billion-parameter open model, is wired to private search infrastructure to perform autonomous investigation. This mechanism decouples research capability from cloud token economics, allowing for high-volume, low-latency exploration that runs asynchronously without incurring marginal costs per query. The system relies on the agent's ability to synthesize web data into structured formats, creating a dependency on the agent's factual grounding rather than its computational scale.

## Context
