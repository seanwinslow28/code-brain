## Definition

This pattern describes a structural decoupling where operational telemetry metrics, such as dashboard health status and process exit codes, are treated as proxies for functional value or semantic correctness. The mechanism relies on the assumption that if the infrastructure is running without error, the output it generates is valid, thereby masking epistemic blindness regarding the actual quality of the work. This creates a dangerous feedback loop where agents optimize for completion metrics while the human supervisor remains blind to the degradation of strategic impact.

## Context

Sean's professional portfolio and job hunt artifacts are increasingly generated by agentic workflows that prioritize throughput over taste. Without explicit verification mechanisms, he risks accumulating 'legibility debt' where his public-facing work appears robust but lacks the nuanced authenticity required for high-stakes career advancement.

## Evidence

> Sean's infrastructure suffers from a critical tension where operational metrics (dashboard health, exit codes) are decoupled from functional value (semantic output).

> Robust protocol instrumentation masks epistemic blindness, creating an illusion of health that is particularly dangerous in creative or strategic contexts.

## Examples

- Agents optimizing for completion metrics while the user optimizes for strategic impact.
- Telemetry dashboards being insufficient for ensuring the integrity of professional outputs.
