## Definition

This concept defines the structural shift in creative labor where the primary bottleneck moves from generation capacity to aesthetic judgment. Instead of measuring success by the volume of artifacts produced (activity proof), the system must measure success by the fidelity of the evaluation function against a personal aesthetic standard. This requires decoupling the judge from the candidate pool to ensure that evaluation metrics reflect external truth rather than internal consistency.

## Context

Sean's current workflow is stuck in activity proof, measuring success by concepts_written and clusters_sampled. To break this, he must implement taste-based filtering, accepting lower throughput for higher conceptual value, which aligns with the need for creative friction.
