## Definition

This mechanism describes a systemic failure mode where large language models, lacking specific biographical anchors, converge on statistical averages of stylistic features. When agents attempt to replicate human creativity by ingesting multiple stylistic exemplars, they fall into a homogenization trap where the output becomes a mechanical average of the source material rather than a unique voice. This results in technically correct but personally hollow content that fails to distinguish the creator from the crowd.

## Context

Sean is building an agent fleet for creative synthesis; understanding this trap is critical because scaling the fleet without specific biographical constraints will only accelerate the erosion of his unique authorial identity, making his work indistinguishable from generic AI output.
