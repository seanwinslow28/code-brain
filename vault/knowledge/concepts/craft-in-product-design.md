## Definition

Craft is a multi-dimensional evaluation rubric that transcends the binary of visual polish versus functional utility, requiring designers to integrate business acumen, architecture, and visual execution into a unified generalist skillset. This framework emerges as a necessary counterweight to the flattening effect of generative AI and design systems, which raise the baseline quality of output while simultaneously devaluing the distinct human judgment required to navigate subjective trade-offs. The mechanism relies on treating visual design not as a secondary 'pixel-pushing' task but as an equal partner to UX strategy, ensuring that the final artifact possesses both use value and aesthetic coherence.

## Context

Sean is navigating a job hunt in 2026 where AI tools have commoditized the '80%-good' baseline of design execution. Understanding this rubric allows him to articulate his value not just as a user of AI tools, but as a generalist who can make the high-stakes subjective judgments that AI cannot, thereby differentiating his portfolio from candidates who rely solely on prompt engineering or template-based workflows.
