## Definition

This tension arises when the metrics used to monitor agent health (such as uptime or process existence) diverge from the actual value generated by those agents (semantic integrity and knowledge synthesis). High visibility into operational states can mask low semantic value, leading to a false sense of progress. The system prioritizes the appearance of activity over the reality of insight generation, creating a gap between what is monitored and what matters.

## Context

Sean needs to ensure that monitoring strategies elevate 'deferred' states caused by hardware unreachability to critical alerts, rather than treating them as routine operational noise. This ensures that the true state of knowledge production is visible and actionable.
