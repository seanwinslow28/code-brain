## Definition

This tension arises when the metrics used to monitor agent health, such as uptime or process existence, diverge from the actual value generated by those agents, specifically semantic integrity and knowledge synthesis. High visibility into operational states can mask low semantic value, leading to a false sense of progress where the system prioritizes the appearance of activity over the reality of insight generation. This creates a structural gap between what is monitored for health and what actually matters for the utility of the vault.

## Context

Sean needs to ensure that monitoring strategies elevate 'deferred' states caused by hardware unreachability to critical alerts, rather than treating them as routine operational noise. This ensures that the true state of knowledge production is visible and actionable, preventing the silent accumulation of legibility debt where the system appears healthy but produces no new insights.
