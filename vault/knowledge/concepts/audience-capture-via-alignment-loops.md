## Definition

This pattern occurs when the feedback loop between content generation and audience response optimizes the creator into a caricature of their previous success, rather than allowing their learning frontier to evolve. Tighter alignment among content, audience response, and product strategy creates a local maximum where the creator produces endless variations of high-performing topics even as their actual expertise moves elsewhere. The mechanism traps the agent in a performative loop where relevance is mistaken for growth.

## Context

Sean's current vault synthesis relies on maintaining consistent output across domains like job-hunt and creative-studio. If the agents optimize solely for engagement or consistency metrics, they will reinforce existing narratives rather than challenging Sean to develop new, potentially less popular but more valuable skills.

## Evidence

> That loop can optimize Sean into a caricature: endless 'agent fleet' content because it performs well, even when his learning frontier has moved elsewhere.

> It contradicts the article’s unexamined premise that tighter alignment among content, audience response, and product strategy is always desirable.

## Examples

- Continuing to produce 'agent fleet' content because it performs well, despite Sean's learning frontier having moved elsewhere.
- Optimizing for engagement metrics rather than allowing the learning frontier to dictate new directions.
