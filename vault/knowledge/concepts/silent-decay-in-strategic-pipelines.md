## Definition

Strategic pipelines degrade not through catastrophic failure but through the accumulation of zero-value outputs that pass all operational checks. When agents successfully execute their protocols without generating meaningful connections or insights, the pipeline remains 'healthy' in the monitoring layer while the strategic value it is supposed to produce evaporates. This decay is invisible to standard health checks because they measure process completion rather than semantic density.

## Context

Sean's job hunt and creative studio workflows depend on consistent, high-quality output from his agents. If the job feed or synthesizer produces empty results without erroring, he loses strategic momentum without immediate awareness, requiring explicit content-volume thresholds to detect.
