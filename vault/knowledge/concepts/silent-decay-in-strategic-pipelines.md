## Definition

Strategic pipelines suffer from silent decay when critical quality assurance or validation steps are skipped or fail without alerting the user. The mechanism is a gap in the feedback loop where no agent checks the coherence or freshness of the synthesized output, allowing errors to accumulate unnoticed. This results in a degradation of the vault's strategic value over time, as Sean loses visibility into the actual state of his projects and job applications.

## Context

Sean's job hunt and creative studio workflows depend on accurate, up-to-date information. Silent decay means he might continue pursuing outdated strategies or miss opportunities because the system failed to flag critical changes.

> The `vault-critic` has not run since 2026-08-31, leaving no recent assessment of the quality or coherence of the synthesized concepts and connections.

> Sean must implement a 'semantic health' metric that measures the freshness and coherence of outputs, not just the uptime of agents.
