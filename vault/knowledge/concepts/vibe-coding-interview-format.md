## Definition

A situated human-machine-human interaction where the candidate’s competence is revealed through the repair, redirection, and reframing of a live plan in real-time. This format shifts the evaluation from static knowledge retrieval to dynamic problem-solving under ambiguity, requiring the candidate to demonstrate how they navigate and correct course when initial assumptions fail. The core mechanism is the visible struggle and resolution of technical constraints, which serves as a proxy for how the candidate will handle production incidents or shifting product requirements.
