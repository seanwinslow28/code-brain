## Definition

In environments where information generation is cheap and abundant, the primary value shift moves from access to curation. The mechanism here is that the sheer volume of low-signal or biased content (such as vendor marketing disguised as education) creates a noise floor that makes raw data ingestion useless. The scarce resource becomes the ability to identify 'structural gaps'—areas where the absence of information is itself a signal of market failure or strategic blind spots.

## Context

Sean's YouTube watchlist reveals that 'coverage is uneven,' with M4 (interaction/trust) being the thinnest. This unevenness is not just a gap; it is a strategic indicator that the human half of AI product work is under-served, making Sean's ability to curate and fill these gaps a competitive advantage in his job hunt and studio output.
