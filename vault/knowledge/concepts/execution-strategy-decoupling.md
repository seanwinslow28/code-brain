## Definition

This mechanism separates the cognitive labor of drafting and refining content from the mechanical act of publishing it. By completing all creative iterations (voice-pass, critique) before the execution phase, the agent creates a deterministic state where the remaining work is purely operational. This decoupling reduces context-switching costs and allows for batch processing of high-friction actions like posting or committing, treating the final output as a static asset rather than a dynamic workflow.

## Context

Sean has completed the 'voice-pass' for his job hunt materials, leaving only the execution steps (commit, record, post). This allows him to treat the publishing sprint as a mechanical checklist rather than a creative task, reducing the mental load required to maintain momentum in his job hunt.
