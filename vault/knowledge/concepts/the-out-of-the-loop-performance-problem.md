## Definition

This mechanism describes the paradox where increased automation degrades human operator competence by removing them from active decision-making loops. As agents handle more routine tasks, the human supervisor loses situational awareness and judgment calibration, becoming least prepared for the abnormal cases that do arise. The optimum is not maximum automation but designed engagement to preserve 'operator taste' and diagnostic capability.

## Evidence

> Excessive review creates overload; insufficient active participation causes situation-awareness and judgment decay.

> The optimum is not maximum automation or minimum output, but designed operator engagement.

> Automation leaves humans supervising precisely the abnormal cases for which passive supervision leaves them least prepared.
