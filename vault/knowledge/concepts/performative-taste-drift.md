## Definition

This mechanism describes the endogenous change in an evaluator's standards caused by repeated exposure to generated outputs. The fleet does not merely overwhelm pre-existing standards; it actively reshapes them through volume, leading to a gradual lowering of aesthetic thresholds. As Sean consumes more automated content, his own taste adapts to the new baseline, making it harder to distinguish high-value insights from generic synthesis.

## Context

Sean's vault shows a consistent pattern of high-volume synthesis via qwen3.6-35b-a3b-32k. If his taste drifts downward due to this exposure, he may fail to recognize the 'de-differentiated' nature of his own output, leading to a false sense of quality in his job hunt materials.
