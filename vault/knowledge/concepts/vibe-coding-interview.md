## Definition

The Vibe-Coding Interview format functions as a situated human-machine-human interaction where the candidate’s competence is revealed through the repair, redirection, and reframing of a live plan. It serves a dual purpose by testing real-time coding skills while simultaneously assessing cultural fit through interpersonal dynamics. This format inverts the traditional pair-programming dynamic by forcing the human to act as the navigator while the AI assumes the driver role. The evaluation shifts from static code artifacts to the candidate's ability to manage the ambiguity of not knowing with operational precision.
