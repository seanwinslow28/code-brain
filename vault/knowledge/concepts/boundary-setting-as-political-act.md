## Definition

Boundary setting is the deliberate act of defining what a system includes versus what it treats as an exogenous constant, which inherently excludes certain feedback loops from visibility. This exclusion creates a structural blind spot where variables outside the boundary can exert dominant influence without being modeled, leading to interventions that fail because they address symptoms within the map rather than causes outside it. The mechanism relies on the tension between model completeness and stakeholder persuasion, as wider boundaries often reduce predictive utility while narrower ones increase the risk of unanticipated retaliation from excluded actors.

## Context

This matters to Sean because his AI PM curriculum explicitly warns that teams who skip mapping discover loops they didn't know existed, and those who map too narrowly miss the very forces (like competitor moves or provider pricing) that often determine product viability. In his own fleet operations, failing to boundary-set correctly means treating infrastructure status as given rather than modeled, leading to silent failures when external dependencies shift.
