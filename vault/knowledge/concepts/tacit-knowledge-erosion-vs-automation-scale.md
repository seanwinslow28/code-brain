## Definition

This pattern defines the loss of nuanced, context-rich understanding as automation scales beyond a certain threshold. When agents generate concepts at high velocity, they strip away the implicit, hard-to-articulate details that give Sean's work its strategic value. This erosion is not just a loss of detail but a loss of 'tacit knowledge'—the kind of insight that cannot be easily codified or retrieved by standard search mechanisms. The consequence is that Sean's vault becomes a repository of generic facts rather than a tool for deep, creative synthesis.

## Context

Sean's work in the 'creative-studio' and 'job-hunt-2026' domains relies heavily on tacit knowledge—nuanced understanding of market dynamics, creative trends, and personal brand. If his synthesizer erodes this knowledge, it undermines his competitive edge. This concept highlights why Sean must prioritize depth over breadth in his automated workflows.

## Evidence

> Creatives in the comments echo that this material is impossible to build good narratives or campaigns on.

> These sentiments often appear in threads where creatives try to use AI for ideation or outlines and get generic, repetitive suggestions with thin or missing narrative.

## Examples

- The high rejection count (78) in the 2026-06-23 run suggests that many generated concepts lacked the necessary tacit depth to be useful.
- The shift to qwen3.6-35b-a3b-32k in July resulted in fewer but higher-quality outputs, suggesting that larger models better preserve tacit knowledge.
