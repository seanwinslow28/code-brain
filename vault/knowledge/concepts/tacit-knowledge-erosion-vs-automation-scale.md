## Definition

This invariant describes the erosion of tacit knowledge as automation scales, making it difficult to build good narratives or campaigns on automated material. The mechanism involves the loss of nuanced, context-specific understanding that is essential for creative work, which cannot be easily captured by automated processes. This erosion is a significant risk because it leads to a homogenization of content and a loss of unique voice.
