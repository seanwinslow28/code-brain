## Definition

This concept describes a structural inversion where the marginal cost of content generation approaches zero, thereby shifting the primary bottleneck from production capacity to editorial discernment. As tools lower the barrier to entry for creation, they simultaneously raise the cognitive load required to evaluate and curate that output, forcing creators into a supervisory role that erodes their strategic perspective. The mechanism operates by decoupling the act of making from the act of judging, creating a dependency loop where the creator must constantly switch contexts between generative flow and critical analysis.
