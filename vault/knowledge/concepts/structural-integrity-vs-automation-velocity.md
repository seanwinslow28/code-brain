## Definition

This mechanism defines the trade-off where increasing the speed and volume of automated knowledge processing leads to a disproportionate increase in structural fragmentation and semantic isolation. As agents prioritize throughput over coherence, the vault accumulates 'legibility debt' because the connections between concepts become brittle or contradictory. The system sacrifices long-term retrievability for short-term production metrics, creating a fragile infrastructure that requires manual intervention to repair.

## Context

Sean's job hunt and creative studio workflows depend on rapid information synthesis. When automation velocity outpaces the vault's ability to maintain structural integrity, Sean faces increased cognitive load during retrieval, undermining the very efficiency the agents were meant to provide.
