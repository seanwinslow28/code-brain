## Definition

A backlog of unprocessed information items that requires active agent intervention to resolve. When the queue is empty, it indicates a lack of background data sweep or a failure in the ingestion mechanism, leading to a stagnation in knowledge growth and a missed opportunity for automated insight generation. This state reflects a passive system rather than an active one, where potential value remains unharvested.

## Context

Sean's deep-researcher agent is designed to fill gaps in knowledge automatically. An empty queue suggests either a lack of new triggers or a failure in the triggering mechanism, which Sean must manually address by specifying topics like '15th month check' to restart the value generation cycle.

## Evidence

> Deep research synthesis queue was empty, indicating no background data sweep today.

> Trigger deep-researcher on a specific financial topic (e.g., 15th month check) to fill the queue gap.

## Examples

- The agent reported 'no unchecked items' despite the presence of potential new information sources.
- Sean must manually trigger specific topics to overcome the inertia of an empty queue.
