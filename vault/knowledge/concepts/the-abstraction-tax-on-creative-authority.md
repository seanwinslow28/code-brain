## Definition

This mechanism describes the structural erosion of authorial control that occurs when a creator relies on high-velocity automated synthesis to bridge the gap between raw data and final output. As the volume of processed information increases, the creator's ability to verify the fidelity of the synthesized result decreases, forcing them to trust the 'black box' of the agent rather than their own judgment. This creates a dependency where the agent's interpretation becomes the de facto reality, effectively taxing the creator's authority by substituting their taste with the model's statistical average.
