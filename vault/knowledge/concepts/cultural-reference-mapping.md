## Definition

A structured elicitation process that forces an agent to extract specific, idiosyncratic cultural touchstones rather than accepting generic categories. By requiring the user to name exact lines, moments, and associations for their interests, the system builds a dense network of personal context that prevents the model from defaulting to 'average' or 'prestige' stereotypes. This mapping serves as the foundational layer for voice calibration, ensuring that the model's output is anchored in Sean's actual lived experience rather than abstracted ideals.

## Context

Sean needs his writing to sound authentic, not like a generic professional. He uses this mechanism to drag out the 'real stuff' of his taste—the shows he quotes at 2 a.m., the music he defends—creating a unique fingerprint that distinguishes his voice from the standard AI-generated content.

## Evidence

> Left to guess, a model pegs you as a person of tasteful, prestige, respectable interests. You are not that person.

> This drags out the real stuff: the shows you quote at 2 a.m., the music you'd actually defend, the embarrassing tier.

## Examples

- Identifying the specific line in a movie that Sean quotes frequently.
- Naming the 'embarrassing tier' of music he genuinely enjoys.
- Listing the specific places and slang from his hometown that inform his perspective.
