## Definition

A distribution surface is the specific interface layer through which a product is discovered and consumed by a target audience. When the audience shifts from humans to autonomous agents, the distribution surface must transition from visual UIs to machine-readable protocols like MCP servers and structured AGENTS.md files. This shift requires treating API documentation and agent-facing copy as primary marketing assets rather than secondary technical details.

## Context

Sean is currently building the Project CTO Media Hub and preparing for a job hunt in AI Product Management. Understanding that agents are a distinct distribution channel allows him to position his portfolio projects as 'agent-native' products, directly addressing the hiring market's demand for AI fluency and agentic engineering signals.

## Evidence

> Your product’s next million users won’t have eyes. They won’t visit your website, sit through your onboarding, or read your changelog. They’ll be AI agents, autonomous software that discovers, evaluates, and integrates tools programmatically.

> If your product cannot be parsed, authenticated, and executed by an agent, you are invisible in the fastest-growing software channel.

## Examples

- Stripe, Cloudflare, Shopify, GitHub, Asana, Zapier, and others have already shipped agent-facing surfaces.
- These are the most important piece of copy you’ll write for the agent channel. They’re to agents what landing page copy is to humans.
