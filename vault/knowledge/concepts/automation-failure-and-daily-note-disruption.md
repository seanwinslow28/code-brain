## Definition

This pattern identifies the cascading effect where a single point of failure in the morning synthesis pipeline results in the complete absence of the daily operational context. The mechanism is not merely a technical error but a structural gap: because the daily note is generated by an automated agent, its non-generation leaves a void in Sean's timeline that cannot be filled by other agents running later in the day. This creates a 'context cliff' where the user must manually bridge the gap between the last successful synthesis and the current moment.

## Context

Sean's workflow depends on the daily note existing to maintain historical context for ops review. When the morning agent fails, the 'Daily note exists' flag becomes false or stale, disrupting the continuity of his job hunt and creative studio tracking for that day.

## Evidence

> The daily note was generated, maintaining the historical context structure for Sean's ops review.

> daily-driver morning failed due to budget exhaustion (max_budget_usd), halting key operational synthesis.

> Specific fleet issues to fix or build. Tied to the observation that autonomous agents in headless mode can't access MCP — so MCP-dependent content is missing from Sean's daily note.

- On 2026-06-12, the daily-driver morning agent failed at 08:32 AM, leaving the daily note generation incomplete for that specific date.
- The deep-researcher queue was empty, meaning no background research could compensate for the missing morning synthesis.
