## Definition

This mechanism describes the causal link between a recurring business pressure and the hiring of an automation artifact. It requires defining skills by the progress event that triggers them rather than the technical output they produce. The invariant is that users hire a skill to produce a trusted artifact without a specific failure mode, meaning the skill's value is defined by the avoidance of pain rather than the generation of utility.

## Context

Sean currently proves he shipped skills but lacks a framework to decide which skills deserve to exist. Applying this mapping allows him to evaluate candidate skills by trigger frequency and pain intensity, shifting his narrative from 'I built three' to 'I know which three should be built first.'

## Evidence

> When [recurring business pressure] creates [manual coordination drag], users hire the skill to produce [trusted artifact] without [failure mode].

> When ETF news requires fast publication under compliance-sensitive formatting constraints, editors hire `etf-page-creator` to produce a publishable WordPress draft without hand-copying structured market data.

## Examples

- Evaluating candidate skills by trigger frequency, pain intensity, trust requirement, and artifact repeatability.
