## Definition

This concept defines the emerging competitive advantage in agentic workflows where the value of human oversight is no longer measured by volume but by the specificity of taste transfer. The mechanism involves a continuous calibration loop where the human teaches the agent their aesthetic standards until the agent can produce work that is recognizably unique on demand. This creates a dependency where the agent's utility is directly proportional to the depth of the user's taste memory and the efficiency of its encoding into machine-readable constraints.
