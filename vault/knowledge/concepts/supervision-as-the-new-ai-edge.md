## Definition

As the marginal cost of agentic execution approaches zero, the competitive advantage shifts entirely to the quality of human oversight and strategic direction. This mechanism defines supervision not as error-correction but as the active curation of intent and taste, requiring deep domain expertise to evaluate outputs that machines can generate trivially. The edge is no longer in building the loop but in knowing which loops are worth running and how to interpret their results.

## Context

Sean's job hunt strategy must pivot from applying to more roles to deeply supervising the narrative of each application, ensuring his 'taste' is the differentiator. In his creative studio, this means prioritizing the refinement of core concepts over the generation of new ones, as the latter is now a commodity.

## Evidence

> I think we sometimes oversimplify agentic engineering where we're like it's just prompts it's just loops or whatever and I think it's more like no we need to know what we're doing we need to like learn more

> The consequence is that Sean may optimize for throughput (more runs, more concepts) while neglecting the harder task of ensuring those outputs actually contribute to his GDP or creative goals.

## Examples

- Shifting job hunt strategy from volume of applications to quality of supervision over each application's narrative.
- Learning agentic engineering deeply rather than treating it as a simple prompt-engineering task.
