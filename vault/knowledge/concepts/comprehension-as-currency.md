## Definition

In the generative era, technical documentation functions not merely as a record of implementation but as a primary signal of cognitive fluency and strategic intent. The value of an explanation lies in its ability to demonstrate how the author thinks, specifically by foregrounding the honest mechanics of failure and resolution rather than polishing the surface narrative. This shifts the document's purpose from neutralizing technical debt to showcasing the depth of understanding that allows one to distinguish between a demo and a real system.

## Context

Sean is using these EXPLANATION.md files as high-leverage artifacts in his job hunt, where recruiters evaluate 'how you think' rather than just what was built. By treating comprehension as currency, he ensures that the technical substance serves as proof of fluency, making the documentation itself a persuasive tool for career advancement.

## Evidence

> These aren't docs to neutralize. They're how a recruiter reads *how you think*, and they exist to showcase AI fluency, so they read like you wrote them.

> The technical substance stays 100% intact, because that substance *is* the fluency proof.

## Examples

- Dialing reflective halves (Why / What I learned) to ~55-60% to give the recruiter a window into Sean's thinking process.
- Scrubbing em dashes and sharpening closers to create a register that sounds like Sean rather than a generic technical writer.
