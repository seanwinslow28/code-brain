## Definition

Graceful extensibility is the capacity of an adaptive system to expand its boundary conditions under surprise, rather than merely collapsing or reducing scope when those boundaries are exceeded. It contrasts with brittleness by identifying that collapse occurs at the edge of adaptive capacity, whereas extensibility allows the system to recruit new resources, redistribute work, or alter strategy to maintain function in novel states. This invariant requires a fleet saturation map that tracks base capacity, boundary conditions, and sources of additional maneuver for each agent.

## Context
