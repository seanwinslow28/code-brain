## Definition

Agentic Experience Design (AXD) is a formalized discipline that translates academic research from CHI and FAccT into loadable design skills for AI agents. It operates through six distinct layers, including model-interaction-design and ai-alignment-reasoning, to structure how humans and AI take turns in mixed-initiative flows. This discipline provides a vocabulary and set of practices, such as handoff protocols and error personalities, that allow designers to shape system behavior and anticipate harm during agent interactions.
