## Definition

A longitudinal learning pattern where the quality of an agent's output is determined by the volume and specificity of historical interaction data rather than the sophistication of a single prompt. The system treats each session as a discrete data point that compounds over time, requiring repeated exposure to specific references and tonal nuances before the model can replicate the user's voice accurately. This shifts the workflow from 'prompt engineering' to 'data curation,' where the primary effort is building a dense, verifiable record of the user's actual behavior.

## Context

Sean is moving away from one-shot prompts for his job hunt and creative work. He recognizes that a single interaction is insufficient for capturing his complex voice. By accumulating evidence across multiple sessions (the 'pile'), he creates a robust dataset that allows the model to learn his specific cultural references and stylistic quirks over time.
