## Definition

Competitive analysis functions as a strategic inflection point detector rather than a static market map. It requires identifying when the underlying basis of competition shifts, forcing a re-evaluation of which activities create sustainable advantage. This mechanism transforms the practice from a descriptive exercise into a predictive tool for career positioning and product strategy. By mapping the activity systems that reinforce each other, it reveals why imitation is difficult due to tradeoffs rather than just feature parity.

## Context

This matters to Sean because it reframes his transition to agentic engineering not as a personal reinvention, but as a response to a strategic inflection point. It allows him to articulate his value through the lens of activity system mapping, showing how his vault and agent infrastructure create compounding advantages that competitors cannot copy by cloning a single repo.

## Evidence

> Competitive analysis should not only map rivals; it should identify when the basis of competition is changing.

> A technique for analyzing competitors by the activities they perform differently, not by surface features or positioning claims.

- Teardown of Code-Brain as an activity system showing why pieces compound.
