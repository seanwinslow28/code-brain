## Definition

Competitive analysis functions as a strategic inflection point detector rather than a static market map. It requires identifying when the underlying basis of competition shifts, forcing a re-evaluation of which capabilities constitute a defensible moat. This mechanism treats market position not as a fixed coordinate but as a dynamic state dependent on the current rules of engagement. The primary value lies in anticipating structural changes before they become visible in standard financial or operational metrics.
