## Definition

The signal of agentic engineering is defined by the ability to orchestrate complex, multi-step workflows where the agent's output is not just a static artifact but a dynamic state that evolves through feedback loops and tool use. This signal is distinct from simple automation because it requires the engineer to design the 'intent' and 'constraints' of the agent, ensuring that the system can handle ambiguity and failure modes without human intervention. It manifests in the hiring market as a preference for candidates who can demonstrate how their agents solve problems that were previously too expensive or complex for manual labor, rather than just those who can write prompts.
