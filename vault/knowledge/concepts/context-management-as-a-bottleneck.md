## Definition

Context Management as a Bottleneck identifies the constraint where the volume and complexity of information flow limit agent effectiveness more than computational power does. This mechanism involves the continuous generation, filtering, and injection of relevant context into agent workflows to prevent overload or misalignment. It requires pre-generated briefs and automated updates that personalize information based on the user's current focus, ensuring that agents operate with a coherent and up-to-date understanding of the state.

## Context

Sean's daily drive agent and creative studio workflows are directly impacted by this bottleneck. If context management fails, his automation pipelines break or produce irrelevant outputs. His job hunt efforts also suffer if he cannot efficiently manage the context of multiple applications and interviews. Solving this bottleneck is key to scaling his personal productivity and demonstrating technical depth in product design.

## Evidence

> A natural-language summary of the last 24 hours, generated by Simon AI, personalized to the user's focus area serves as a pre-generated brief that updates automatically.

> The tension lies between the technical complexity of agentic orchestration and the product strategy goal of making the system usable for non-technical stakeholders.

## Examples

- Automated daily briefs that summarize key events and action items for the user
- Context windows that dynamically adjust based on the current task phase in a creative workflow
