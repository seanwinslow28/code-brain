## Definition

Portfolio Projects for Interview Prep are artifacts designed not to demonstrate technical output, but to demonstrate the candidate's ability to think like a hiring manager in a specific domain. They function as 'stress-test' simulations where the candidate invites critique to reveal their depth of understanding. The project's value lies in its 'honest gaps' and its alignment with the specific regulatory or operational constraints of the target industry. It is a proof of 'accountability fluency' rather than a proof of 'coding fluency'.
