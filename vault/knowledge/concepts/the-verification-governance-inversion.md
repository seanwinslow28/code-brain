## Definition

This inversion describes the failure mode where 98% of people write opportunities as solutions, framing the problem in terms of the desired outcome rather than the underlying need. The mechanism is a cognitive bias that prioritizes the elegance of the solution over the rigor of the gap analysis, leading to a systemic misalignment between identified problems and actual requirements. This pattern persists because it is easier to propose a fix than to define the pain accurately.

## Context

Sean must guard against this inversion in his own gap maps and those generated by his agents, ensuring that opportunities are stated as needs/pains rather than solutions.

## Evidence

> 98% of people write opportunities as solutions and solution-framing is the dominant failure mode of gap mapping.

> Every strong gap presentation does the same three things to each gap: (1) states it as a need/pain, not a solution.
