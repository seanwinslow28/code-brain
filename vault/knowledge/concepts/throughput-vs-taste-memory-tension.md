## Definition

This mechanism describes a structural conflict where the expansion of automated sampling capacity directly degrades the fidelity of creative taste signals. As the system scales to process more clusters, it accumulates noise that obscures the specific stylistic constraints required for high-quality output. The agent fleet prioritizes volume over precision, leading to a systemic trust deficit because the user must intervene more frequently to correct the hollowed-out results.

Sean is observing this tension in his vault synthesizer runs, where increasing cluster sampling leads to lower quality concepts and higher rejection rates or stylistic drift. This matters because it threatens the core value proposition of his 'Creative Partner' agent, which is supposed to reduce friction rather than create it through voluminous but useless output.
