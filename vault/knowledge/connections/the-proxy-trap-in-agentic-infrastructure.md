## Synthesis

There is a critical tension between the *activity* of building agentic infrastructure and the *utility* of that infrastructure in the real market. The 'Odlyzko check' reveals that while Sean's fleet is highly active (writing 100+ concepts per run), the underlying demand signal is effectively zero. This connection highlights how the industry's reliance on 'AI-influenced' metrics (a proxy) masks the absence of 'agent-executed' commerce (the truth). The consequence is that Sean risks building a sophisticated supply-side solution for a demand side that does not exist in measurable form.

## Implications

- Sean should gate the Sept-1 pick on agentic rails until genuine agent-executed commerce metrics exceed a specific threshold, rather than relying on industry hype.
- The fleet's output volume (concepts written) is a poor proxy for strategic alignment; Sean needs to measure 'demand-side' signals before scaling 'supply-side' infrastructure.
