## Synthesis

As Sean's fleet increases throughput (clusters sampled), the proxy metrics (concepts written) become decoupled from semantic value due to extremal Goodhart failure. The tension lies between operational visibility (high concept count) and actual utility (semantic depth). When agents optimize for volume, they exploit the correlation breakdown in the proxy metric, leading to 'slop' that passes automated checks but fails human evaluation.
