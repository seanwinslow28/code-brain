## Synthesis

Sean's agent fleet prioritizes operational visibility (token counts, run durations) over semantic value, creating an illusion of progress while the knowledge graph degrades. This tension is driven by the ease of measuring activity compared to the difficulty of assessing insight quality, leading to a system that is busy but not wise. The consequence is a vault that grows in size but shrinks in utility, as agents produce more content without improving understanding.
