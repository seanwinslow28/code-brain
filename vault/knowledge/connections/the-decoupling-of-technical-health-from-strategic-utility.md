## Synthesis

Sean's fleet exhibits a critical decoupling where technical health metrics (uptime, process existence) no longer correlate with strategic utility (knowledge synthesis, job leads). The agents report 'healthy' status while their core functions are either blocked (synthesizer host unreachable) or empty (job feed zero results). This creates a dangerous blind spot where Sean believes his infrastructure is working because the logs say 'success', while in reality, his knowledge vault is stagnating and his job hunt automation is silent. The consequence is that Sean must shift his monitoring focus from technical availability to semantic output validation.

## Implications

- Sean should implement semantic health checks that verify the *content* of agent outputs, not just their process status.
- The 'healthy' status of the job-feed is misleading and requires manual verification of the underlying data sources.
- The synthesizer's host unreachability is a critical failure that must be prioritized over other technical issues.
