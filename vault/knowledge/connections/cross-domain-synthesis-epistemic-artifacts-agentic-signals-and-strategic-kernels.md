## Synthesis

The tension lies between the need to demonstrate technical depth in job hunting and the risk of appearing merely operational. By treating portfolio items as epistemic artifacts, Sean bridges the gap between creative-studio output and job-hunt-2026 signaling. The consequence is that his code becomes evidence of strategic judgment (diagnosis/policy) rather than just execution speed, allowing him to bypass the 'too junior' filter by showing how he navigates uncertainty. This requires applying a Strategy Kernel Check to ensure every artifact explicitly names its diagnostic logic, and framing the agentic infrastructure as a signal of inspectability rather than just connectivity.
