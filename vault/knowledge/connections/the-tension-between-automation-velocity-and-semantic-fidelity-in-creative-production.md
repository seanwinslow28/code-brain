## Synthesis

Sean's vault exhibits a fundamental tension between the desire for high-velocity, low-cost automation (evidenced by the 'Fleet Memory Index' and cost metrics) and the need for deep semantic transformation in creative work (evidenced by the 'brain dump to prose operations' research). The mechanism is that as the system prioritizes velocity (more runs, lower cost per run), it sacrifices the 'knowledge-transforming' operations necessary for high-quality output. This creates a 'dead' draft problem where the system produces volume but lacks the rhetorical depth required for compelling narrative. The consequence is that Sean must manually intervene to add the missing 'tier 1' and 'tier 2' operations, effectively negating the efficiency gains of automation.

- Sean must explicitly define 'tier 1' and 'tier 2' operations as non-negotiable steps in the automation pipeline, even if they increase cost or reduce velocity.
