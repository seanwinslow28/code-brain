## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the desire to scale output via agent fleets and the biological limit of human supervision. As Sean increases his agent count, he risks hitting 'Supervision Fatigue,' where the cost of managing the fleet exceeds the value of its output. This forces a strategic choice: either accept a hard cap on solo productivity or adopt a pyramidal structure that requires hiring humans to manage other agents.

## Implications

- Sean should cap his active agent fleet at a level where he can still provide clear, high-quality direction without becoming a bottleneck.
