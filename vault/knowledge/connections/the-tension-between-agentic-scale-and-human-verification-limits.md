## Synthesis

Sean's attempt to scale his creative production via an agent fleet collides with the biological limit of human attention, creating a 'verification bottleneck' where adding more agents increases total output but decreases average quality due to unmanageable variance. This tension forces a shift from 'generation-first' to 'triage-first' workflows, where the primary value is not in the raw output but in the structured reduction of options. The consequence is that scalability is no longer defined by compute power but by the efficiency of the human-in-the-loop validation protocol.
