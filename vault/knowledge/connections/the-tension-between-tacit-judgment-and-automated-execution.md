## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the tacit nature of professional judgment and the explicit, automated nature of AI execution. While AI tools can handle the 'hands' of coding, they cannot replicate the navigator's ability to make invisible moves visible under pressure. This creates a dependency where Sean must explicitly narrate his decision-making process to demonstrate value, as the artifact alone is insufficient to convey the tacit knowledge required for high-level engineering roles.

## Implications

- Sean must develop a 'specimen interview' mode that explicitly narrates his decision-making process to demonstrate professional taste rather than just tool fluency.
- Portfolio projects should be designed as boundary objects that facilitate negotiation of meaning across different stakeholder groups, not just as technical demonstrations.
