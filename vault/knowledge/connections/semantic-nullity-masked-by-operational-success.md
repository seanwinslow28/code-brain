## Synthesis

The fleet's health dashboard creates a dangerous illusion of progress by reporting 'healthy' or 'success' states for agents that are producing zero strategic value. This tension arises because the monitoring layer validates operational completion (did the script run?) rather than semantic outcome (did it produce useful leads?). The consequence is that Sean may perceive his job hunt infrastructure as robust while it is actually silent, leading to a false sense of security and delayed intervention in broken pipelines.
