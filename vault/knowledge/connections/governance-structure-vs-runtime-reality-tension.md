## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the static, verifiable nature of governance structures (like role rosters and constitution rules) and the dynamic, often opaque nature of runtime agent behavior. While the governance layer provides a clear 'accountability plumbing' that defines who is responsible, it does not inherently prevent semantic drift or operational failure in the agents themselves. This creates a risk where the system appears healthy due to its structural integrity, while actually suffering from silent decay in its execution logic.
