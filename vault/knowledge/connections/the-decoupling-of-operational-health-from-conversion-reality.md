## Synthesis

This connection reveals a critical tension where operational metrics mask semantic stagnation. Agents report health based on process execution, while knowledge integrity depends on successful conversion at the bottleneck. When Sean's fleet optimizes for general efficiency, it creates an illusion of progress that delays necessary interventions. The consequence is a systemic drift where high activity levels coexist with declining offer rates, as resources are wasted on non-constraining stages.
