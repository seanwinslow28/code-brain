## Synthesis

This connection reveals a fundamental tension between the scalability of automated synthesis and the human capacity to verify its output. As Sean’s vault synthesizer scales up concept production, the semantic value of each artifact decreases, requiring more supervision to filter low-quality outputs. This creates a feedback loop where increased automation leads to increased cognitive load, ultimately capping the effective scale of the system.
