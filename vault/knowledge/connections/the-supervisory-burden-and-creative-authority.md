## Synthesis

The tension between AI's generative competence and the user's need for rigorous supervision creates a paradox where creative authority is eroded rather than enhanced. As users spend more time fixing AI output, they lose the 'flow' state essential for deep work, forcing them into a supervisory role that requires high-level evaluation skills often underdeveloped in traditional training. This dynamic shifts the market value from generation to 'taste', yet the tools themselves fail to support taste development, only enabling mimicry.

- Sean's Substack series must focus on teaching 'evaluation loops' and 'context discipline' rather than just prompting techniques, as these are the true bottlenecks for creative professionals.
