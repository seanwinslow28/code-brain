## Synthesis

Sean's agent fleet exhibits a critical decoupling where operational metrics (status=success, cost=$0.00) are optimized independently of strategic value (insight density, connection strength). This creates a 'busy work' trap where agents perform their routines flawlessly but fail to advance Sean's core objectives in job-hunt or creative-studio domains. The consequence is a knowledge vault that is technically robust but strategically stagnant, requiring a shift from monitoring operational health to evaluating semantic impact.
