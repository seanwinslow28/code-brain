## Synthesis

This connection reveals a fundamental shift in the value chain of creative work, where the primary bottleneck moves from generation capacity to aesthetic judgment. As AI lowers the cost of production to near zero, the scarcity shifts to the ability to discern and refine quality. This creates a tension where the creator must become a sophisticated editor, using their taste as a filter for infinite possibilities, rather than a producer of raw material. The consequence is that creative authority is no longer defined by manual skill but by the clarity of one's aesthetic standards and the ability to enforce them on automated systems.
