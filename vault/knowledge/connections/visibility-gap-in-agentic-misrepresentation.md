## Synthesis

The tension between operational visibility and semantic value creates a blind spot where businesses cannot distinguish between legitimate traffic and harmful agent behavior. This gap allows misrepresentation to accumulate silently, eroding trust before any corrective action is possible. For Sean, this implies that his own agent fleet needs robust internal logging and verification mechanisms to prevent it from becoming part of the problem it seeks to solve.
