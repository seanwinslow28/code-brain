## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the scalability of Sean's agent fleet and the specificity of his creative taste. As the fleet scales (sampling hundreds of clusters), the probability of generating 'template' content increases, which directly contradicts the core value proposition of Pencil & Prompt. The consequence is that Sean must invest more time in 'voice calibration' and 'pressure-testing' to maintain quality, creating a bottleneck where the creator's taste becomes the limiting factor for fleet throughput rather than the fleet's compute power.
