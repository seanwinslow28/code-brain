## Synthesis

There is a critical tension between the commoditization of agent infrastructure and the scarcity of intent governance. As agentic tools become more capable, the value shifts from building the engine to steering it, creating a dependency where robust intent specifications are required to prevent infrastructure from executing efficiently but incorrectly. This pattern implies that Sean's focus on `intent-engineering` is not just a niche choice but a strategic necessity to remain relevant as the underlying infrastructure becomes generic.
