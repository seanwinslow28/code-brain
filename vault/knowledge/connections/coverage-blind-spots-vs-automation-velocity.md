## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the velocity of data ingestion and the accuracy of market coverage. Sean's prior runs show that increasing clusters sampled does not linearly increase valid concepts written; instead, it often increases rejected counts or redundant data. The consequence is that high-velocity automation can mask low-quality coverage, leading to 'audience capture' where the system optimizes for feed compliance rather than job market reality. This requires a shift from measuring 'jobs fetched' to measuring 'unseen inventory' via capture-recapture techniques.
