## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the natural tendency of agentic workflows to automate away friction and the job-hunt requirement to preserve and articulate that friction. When agents handle tasks seamlessly, the human loses the 'story' of the problem-solving process. Sean's system must intentionally inject 'explainability' steps into the automation to ensure the output is not just functional, but narratively rich enough for interview defense. This transforms the workflow from a production tool into a pedagogical one.
