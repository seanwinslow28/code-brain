## Synthesis

This connection reveals the tension between the desire for automated knowledge synthesis and the inevitable rise of 'supervision fatigue' as a hard cap on scaling. As Sean’s vault grows, the agent’s ability to generate novel insights diminishes due to context degradation, forcing Sean to shift from a creator to a reviewer. This creates a systemic risk where the cost of verification exceeds the value of the generated content, effectively making automation counter-productive beyond a certain threshold of complexity.
