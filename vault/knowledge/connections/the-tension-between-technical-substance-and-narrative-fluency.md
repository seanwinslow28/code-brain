## Synthesis

There is a critical tension between preserving the raw, unpolished technical reality of a system and adapting its narrative register to signal high-level cognitive fluency to external evaluators. Sean resolves this by keeping the technical substance 100% intact while adjusting the 'dial' of reflection and ownership to ~55-60%, ensuring that the documentation reads as if he wrote it rather than being sanitized for consumption. This balance allows the technical depth to serve as proof of competence while the narrative style demonstrates the strategic thinking required for senior roles.
