## Synthesis

The tension lies in the devaluation of the visual artifact (canvas) and the simultaneous elevation of the textual specification (prompt). As AI tools make rendering trivial, the 'canvas' becomes a disposable output rather than the primary design object. This forces a re-evaluation of what constitutes 'design work' in an AI-augmented workflow, shifting the locus of value to the intent layer. The consequence is that designers and PMs must master language-based specification to remain relevant.
