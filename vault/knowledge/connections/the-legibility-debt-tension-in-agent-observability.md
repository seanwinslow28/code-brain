## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the need for simplified agent health metrics and the complex reality of human-agent joint cognition. When Sean designs monitoring layers that prioritize dashboard simplicity, he inadvertently creates 'legibility debt' where critical coordination failures at handoff boundaries become invisible to the control plane. This tension forces a choice between superficial reliability indicators and deep resilience engineering that accounts for measurement failure.
