## Synthesis

As Sean delegates more tasks to agents, the role of 'supervision' shifts from direct execution to monitoring and intervention, creating a paradox where increased automation leads to increased cognitive load for the human operator. This is evident in the contradiction between `context-management-as-a-bottleneck` and `supervision-as-the-new-ai-edge`, suggesting that while supervision is the new competitive advantage, it is also the primary point of failure when context windows are exceeded. The consequence is a 'watcher's fatigue' where Sean must constantly monitor agent health to prevent silent failures, effectively negating the time savings of automation.
