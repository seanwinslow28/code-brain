## Synthesis

There is a critical tension between the candidate's need for concrete data to make rational decisions and the employer's tendency to withhold this information to maintain negotiation leverage. This creates a paradox where the most relevant opportunities are also the most opaque, forcing Sean to rely on proxy signals like 'remote-friendly culture' rather than hard constraints. The consequence is that his decision-making process shifts from objective filtering to subjective risk assessment, increasing cognitive load and potential for error. This opacity acts as a trust deficit, requiring Sean to develop heuristics that go beyond standard market rates to evaluate total compensation and role fit.
