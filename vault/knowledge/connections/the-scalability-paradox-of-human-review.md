## Synthesis

As agentic output volume increases, the human review bottleneck becomes the critical path for quality control. This creates a tension where the very efficiency that makes agents valuable also threatens to overwhelm the human supervisor, leading to 'supervision fatigue' and eventual failure. The solution is not to reduce agent output, but to increase the determinism of the pre-work and post-work steps to make review faster and more reliable.
