## Synthesis

The tension lies between accumulating evidence for fidelity and defining taste for social charge. Evidence accumulation seeks to match the surface features of a voice, while taste positioning seeks to map the underlying status moves and class dynamics that give the voice its power. When these are decoupled, the output becomes technically accurate but socially inert. Integrating them requires treating voice not as a statistical average but as a strategic position in a social field.
