## Synthesis

As the cost of generation drops, the burden of verification shifts entirely to the human operator, creating a supervision bottleneck that limits scalability more than computational resources do. The agent fleet's efficiency gains are directly offset by the increasing cognitive load required to filter and validate its output, leading to a state where Sean must actively suppress automation to maintain quality. This tension reveals that the true constraint in Sean's system is not throughput but his capacity for high-fidelity judgment.

- Sean must implement hard constraints on agent output volume to prevent comprehension debt from accumulating in his vault.

- Supervision fatigue becomes the hard cap on fleet scaling, requiring periodic manual intervention to reset quality standards.
