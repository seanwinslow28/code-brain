## Synthesis

The tension lies between accumulating knowledge (content recall) and demonstrating competence under pressure (performance calibration). Sean’s existing infrastructure focuses heavily on indexing and synthesis, which builds familiarity but not fluency. By introducing retrieval practice and improvisational stance-shifting, the system forces Sean to expose gaps in his ability to deploy knowledge in real-time. This shifts the metric of success from 'how much do I know' to 'how well can I perform this knowledge in a specific social context.'

## Implications

- Sean must stop treating interview prep as a study session and start treating it as a rehearsal for specific social interactions.
- The definition of 'ready' shifts from having read the material to being able to recall and adapt it under timed constraints.
- Performance metrics must include not just technical accuracy but also status management and narrative control.
