## Synthesis

The tension lies between the ephemeral nature of AI sessions and the persistent need for organizational context. Most teams pay a 're-explanation tax' because they treat AI context as disposable rather than compounding. This creates a hidden cost center where value is lost every time a new session starts, preventing the team from leveraging its collective intelligence. The consequence is that individual efficiency gains are negated by systemic redundancy, making the team slower than the sum of its parts.
