## Synthesis

There is a tension between the commoditization of agent execution capabilities and the scarcity of high-quality domain ontologies. As agents become more capable and accessible, the value shifts to those who can define the bounded domains in which these agents operate effectively. This creates a strategic imperative for Sean to focus on defining clear, valuable domains rather than just building more agents.

## Implications

- Sean should prioritize defining unique, high-value domains for his vault rather than expanding the number of generic agents.
- His job hunt narrative should emphasize his ability to structure complex domains for AI consumption.
