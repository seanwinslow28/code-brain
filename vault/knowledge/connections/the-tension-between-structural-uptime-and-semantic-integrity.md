## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the metric of structural uptime (agents running without crashing) and semantic integrity (agents producing coherent, accurate insights). The current infrastructure prioritizes the former, leading to a 'masking effect' where successful execution logs hide the degradation of knowledge quality. This disconnect creates a dangerous feedback loop where Sean trusts the system's output because it is consistently available, even as its utility diminishes due to silent failures and context drift.
