## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the desire to automate creative workflows and the necessity of human taste to guide them. Sean's strategy resolves this by sequencing the relationship: first, the agent must be calibrated to the user's taste (partner phase), and only then can the system scale (system phase). This implies that any attempt to skip the calibration step results in 'soulless' output that fails to justify the automation effort.
