## Synthesis

The primary tension lies between the automated maintenance of structural links and the manual, labor-intensive verification of semantic integrity. As Sean's vault scales, the cost of verifying every connection's truthfulness exceeds the benefit of maintaining perfect link density, leading to a state where the graph is technically complete but cognitively hollow. This creates a dangerous feedback loop where the system appears healthy because it is well-linked, while the actual value of the knowledge within those links continues to rot unnoticed.

## Implications

- Sean must implement periodic 'semantic audits' that prioritize content verification over link checking, even if it temporarily breaks the graph's structural perfection.
- The definition of 'system health' for his vault needs to be decoupled from uptime metrics and redefined to include a measure of semantic freshness or relevance.
