## Synthesis

This connection reveals a critical tension where the efficiency of AI generation is directly offset by the increasing cognitive load of supervision, creating a bottleneck that undermines creative authority. As Sean scales his creative production through automation, the 'taste-fidelity decoupling' forces him to spend more time evaluating and correcting output than generating it. The consequence is a 'trust deficit' where the volume of output increases but the distinctiveness of the author's voice erodes, requiring a shift from 'prompting harder' to 'teaching taste' through evidence. This creates a dependency on high-fidelity models (qwen3.6-35b) that can better approximate Sean's aesthetic standards, reducing the need for manual correction.
