## Synthesis

This tension emerges from the economic reality that as AI generation becomes cheaper, the relative cost of human supervision increases, creating a bottleneck where the value of the output is determined by the precision of the intent specification. When the cost of verifying content exceeds its value, the workflow must invert from production-centric to supervision-centric, forcing Sean to prioritize constraint definition over model selection. The consequence is that 'Supervision as the New AI Edge' becomes a structural necessity, requiring significant investment in intent engineering to maintain operational velocity and aesthetic fidelity.
