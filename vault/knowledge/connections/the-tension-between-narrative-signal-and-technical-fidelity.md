## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the recruiter's need for narrative coherence (signal) and the engineer's need for technical precision (fidelity). Optimizing for signal often introduces ambiguity that undermines fidelity, while optimizing for fidelity can bury the signal in noise. The mechanism here is 'split-register architecture,' where the artifact is structurally divided to serve these conflicting needs without compromise. The consequence is that Sean must maintain two parallel tracks of authorship within a single document, ensuring that the voice sections do not leak into technical specs and vice versa.

## Implications

- Sean must audit every README for 'register leakage,' where voice bleeds into technical sections or vice versa, which would degrade trust with both audiences.
- The '35% split dial' is not arbitrary but a calculated equilibrium point that maximizes recruiter engagement without sacrificing engineer credibility.
