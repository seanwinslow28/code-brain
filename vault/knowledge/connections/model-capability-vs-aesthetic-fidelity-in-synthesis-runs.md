## Synthesis

There is a critical tension between the raw capability of the underlying model and its ability to maintain aesthetic fidelity during synthesis. While larger models like qwen3.6-35b-a3b-32k offer higher potential quality, they do not automatically guarantee better taste alignment; in fact, the data suggests that without strict supervisory mechanisms, increased model power can lead to different forms of semantic drift or over-standardization. The consequence is that Sean cannot rely on model upgrades alone to improve output quality; he must actively calibrate the rejection thresholds and sampling strategies to preserve the 'noise' that signals human authorship.
