## Synthesis

There is a critical tension between the ease of detecting misinformation via passive monitoring tools and the high cost of effectively remedying it due to the syndication trap. While detection has become commoditized, the structural complexity of data aggregation means that fixing one source rarely resolves the issue across the ecosystem. This gap creates an opportunity for a product that focuses on upstream provider correction rather than downstream monitoring, but it requires a deep understanding of data supply chains that most current tools lack.

## Implications

- Sean must position his product not as another monitoring dashboard but as an upstream data correction service to avoid the commoditization trap.
- The business model must account for the high friction of dealing with data providers, which may require manual intervention or specialized APIs.
