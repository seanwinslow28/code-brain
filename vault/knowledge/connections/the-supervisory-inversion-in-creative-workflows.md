## Synthesis

This connection reveals a critical inversion where the efficiency of AI generation is directly offset by the increasing cognitive load of supervision, creating a bottleneck that undermines creative authority. As tools lower the cost of production, they raise the cost of discernment, forcing creators into a supervisory role that erodes their 'flow' and strategic perspective. The consequence is a market shift where value moves from execution to 'taste', but existing tools fail to support this new requirement, only enabling mimicry.
