## Synthesis

The core tension lies in the fact that agent fleets optimize for statistical coherence across broad datasets, while Sean requires idiosyncratic fidelity to his personal context. When these forces collide without explicit engineering constraints, the output becomes homogenized and loses its strategic value. This pattern reveals that maintaining a 'personal' voice is an active suppression of the model's natural tendencies, not a passive result of using a specific model.

## Implications

- Sean must prioritize explicit context engineering over model selection to maintain fidelity in his vault synthesizer outputs.
- The risk of 'soulless' output increases as the agent's training data becomes more generic relative to Sean's specific needs.
