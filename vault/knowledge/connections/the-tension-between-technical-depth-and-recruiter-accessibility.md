## Synthesis

Sean’s job hunt strategy creates a tension between the need to demonstrate deep technical competence (via agent architecture) and the requirement to remain accessible to non-technical recruiters. This is resolved by using 'Control Architecture as Evangelism' to frame complex technical decisions as business value, while applying 'Negative Space Calibration' to avoid alienating readers with jargon or desperation. The consequence is a professional persona that appears both highly competent and culturally safe, bridging the gap between engineering reality and hiring manager perception.
