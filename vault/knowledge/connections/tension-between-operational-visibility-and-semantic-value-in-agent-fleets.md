## Synthesis

The core tension lies between the ease of monitoring operational metrics (uptime, success rates) and the difficulty of assessing semantic value (accuracy, relevance). Agents can appear healthy based on operational indicators while producing low-value or incorrect outputs, leading to a false sense of security. This disconnect prevents Sean from detecting subtle degradations in his vault's utility until they become critical failures.
