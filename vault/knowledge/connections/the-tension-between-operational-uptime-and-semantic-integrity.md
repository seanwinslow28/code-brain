## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the operational uptime of Sean's agent fleet and the semantic integrity of his knowledge vault. While agents may successfully execute their scripts (uptime), they often fail to maintain the logical consistency of the underlying concepts (integrity). This disconnect creates a dangerous blind spot where Sean perceives his system as healthy because it is running, while in reality, the content it produces is becoming increasingly contradictory and unreliable. The consequence is that Sean must invest significant cognitive effort into auditing the 'health' of his data rather than trusting the automation to maintain it.
