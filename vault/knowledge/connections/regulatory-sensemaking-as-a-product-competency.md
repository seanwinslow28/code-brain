## Synthesis

The core tension lies in the requirement for product managers to design under legal ambiguity without becoming lawyers, which forces a shift from traditional UX optimization to regulatory sensemaking. This creates a dependency where the product surface must simultaneously satisfy market incentives and legal constraints, a duality that standard SaaS playbooks ignore. The consequence is that Sean's value proposition hinges on demonstrating this specific capability, as it differentiates him from candidates who treat compliance as a post-launch hurdle rather than a design constraint.
