## Synthesis

The tension lies between the agent's need for continuous, high-bandwidth context to maintain semantic integrity and the physical reality of infrastructure instability. When the network fails, the agent does not just lose connectivity; it loses the ability to distinguish between relevant and irrelevant data, leading to a collapse in synthesis quality. This creates a dependency where creative output is no longer limited by cognitive capacity but by the reliability of the underlying hardware mesh.
