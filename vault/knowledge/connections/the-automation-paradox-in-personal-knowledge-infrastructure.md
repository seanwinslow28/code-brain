## Synthesis

Monitoring and automation intended to protect creative work can inadvertently damage it by creating a dependency on operational visibility rather than semantic value. When agents prioritize completing runs over generating meaningful progress, the user becomes trapped in a loop of supervising healthy but useless outputs. This tension forces a shift from measuring uptime to measuring 'creative continuity,' where the cost of monitoring is weighed against the loss of creative momentum during failures.
