## Synthesis

There is a structural tension between the 'seller's desire for optimization' and the 'buyer's need for verification' in any market where output quality is hard to assess. Sean can exploit this by positioning his agent fleet not as a content generator, but as a verification engine. The consequence is that he enters the market through the 'fraud prevention' wedge, which is less crowded and more urgent than the 'efficiency' wedge. This allows him to charge for trust, not just labor.

- Sean should build his portfolio projects around 'proof of delivery' metrics rather than just creative output, making the audit trail the primary artifact.
