## Synthesis

This connection reveals a critical tension where operational metrics mask semantic stagnation across Sean's knowledge infrastructure and creative-studio workflows. Agents report health based on process execution, while knowledge integrity depends on successful synthesis of new insights. When the vault synthesizer fails silently, the system continues to generate metrics that suggest normalcy, but the underlying knowledge graph stops evolving. This creates a dangerous feedback loop where Sean perceives his infrastructure as healthy while his strategic clarity decays.

- The definition of 'system health' needs to be expanded to include data staleness metrics, not just process uptime.
