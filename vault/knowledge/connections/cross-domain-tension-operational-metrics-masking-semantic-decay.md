## Synthesis

This connection reveals a critical tension where operational metrics mask semantic stagnation, creating a systemic trust deficit that undermines Sean's ability to scale effectively. Agents report health based on process execution, while knowledge integrity depends on successful synthesis of new insights, leading to a dangerous gap where the system appears functional while its outputs degrade. The consequence is that Sean may perceive his vault as healthy and growing when it is actually becoming stale or irrelevant, requiring a shift from volume-based to fidelity-based success metrics.
