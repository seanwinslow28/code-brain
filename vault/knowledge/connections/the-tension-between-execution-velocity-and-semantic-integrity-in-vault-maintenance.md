## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the desire for rapid, automated knowledge synthesis (velocity) and the need for rigorous, cross-domain verification (integrity). When Sean prioritizes speed—evidenced by runs completing in under 50 seconds with minimal concept generation—the system sacrifices the 'contradiction checks' that prevent semantic decay. This creates a risk where the vault appears active but loses its strategic value, as the 'accountability gap' allows technically successful but semantically empty outputs to accumulate without triggering corrective action.
