## Synthesis

The core tension lies between the visible stability of an AI system's operational metrics and the invisible decay of its semantic validity. While the 'Data Flywheel' suggests compounding growth, it actually accelerates 'Data Stale-ness' when the model optimizes for engagement without accounting for distribution shift. This creates a false sense of health where the product appears functional but is strategically misaligned with user needs, leading to sudden churn that cannot be predicted by traditional monitoring.
