## Synthesis

The tension between operational autonomy and regulatory compliance creates a paradox for AI Product Managers: the more complex and autonomous a system becomes, the harder it is to provide the transparency required by frameworks like SR-11-7. This paradox is resolved not by simplifying the system, but by creating explicit, high-fidelity artifacts that map the system's reality to regulatory expectations. The consequence is that 'compliance' becomes a product feature rather than a legal burden, requiring PMs to possess both technical depth and regulatory fluency.
