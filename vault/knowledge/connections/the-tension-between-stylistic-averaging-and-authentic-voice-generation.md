## Synthesis

When agents attempt to replicate human creativity by ingesting multiple stylistic exemplars, they fall into a homogenization trap where the output becomes a mechanical average of the sources rather than a coherent new voice. This happens because the model lacks the 'soul' or lived experience that naturally unifies an author's style, leading to outputs that are technically correct but emotionally foreign. The resolution requires shifting from declarative instructions (describing the voice) to demonstrative evidence (feeding the actual work), which allows the agent to infer the underlying intent rather than just mimicking the surface mechanics.
