## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the speed of demo creation and the depth of production validation. Organizations prioritize rapid demonstration to satisfy leadership pressure, which inherently selects for easy-to-solve problems in controlled environments. This creates a systemic risk where the perceived value of AI is decoupled from its actual utility, leading to costly failures when deployed in complex, real-world scenarios.
