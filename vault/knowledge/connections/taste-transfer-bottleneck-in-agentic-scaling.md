## Synthesis

This connection reveals a critical tension where the scalability of agent fleets is directly offset by the finite capacity of human taste to encode itself into machine-readable constraints. As Sean increases his sampling volume, the cognitive load of supervision grows non-linearly because each new cluster requires calibration against his unique aesthetic standards. The consequence is that automation velocity becomes inversely proportional to creative distinctiveness, creating a bottleneck where value is determined by the efficiency of taste transfer rather than generation speed.
