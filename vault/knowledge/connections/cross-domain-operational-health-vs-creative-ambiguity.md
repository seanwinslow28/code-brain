## Synthesis

Sean's infrastructure suffers from a critical tension where robust protocol instrumentation masks epistemic blindness, creating an illusion of health that is particularly dangerous in creative and job-hunt contexts. The mechanism is that deterministic monitoring fails to capture the 'work-as-done' reality, leading to silent failure propagation where agents execute correctly but produce stale or incorrect outputs. This forces Sean to design 'degraded modes' that prioritize explicit uncertainty signaling over automated throughput, ensuring that his portfolio projects demonstrate reliability under surprise rather than just raw efficiency.
