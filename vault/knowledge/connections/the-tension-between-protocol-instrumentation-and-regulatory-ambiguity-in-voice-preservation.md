## Synthesis

Sean's attempt to preserve his unique 'writing-voice-modes' through raw data collection clashes with the inherent tendency of agentic systems to normalize and polish output. The mechanism of 'over-polish' acts as a regulatory force that smooths out the very irregularities (typos, run-ons) that constitute his authentic voice. This creates a critical tension: the more he automates his writing process to scale his Substack, the more he risks erasing the distinctive signal that makes his work valuable. The consequence is that automation must be explicitly constrained by 'negative space calibration'—knowing what not to fix—to preserve the integrity of the creative asset.
