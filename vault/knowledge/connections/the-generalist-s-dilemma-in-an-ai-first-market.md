## Synthesis

The tension lies between the industry's demand for 'generalist' designers who can handle both UX and visual craft, and the AI-driven flattening of these skills to a common baseline. As AI tools raise the floor for visual and functional quality, the unique value of the human generalist shifts from execution speed to high-level judgment and intent engineering. This creates a paradox where the 'ideal state' of a designer who can do it all becomes less about technical breadth and more about the ability to curate and direct AI outputs with the same depth of craft that was previously required for manual execution.
