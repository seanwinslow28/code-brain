## Synthesis

The tension lies between the commoditization of model evaluation capabilities and the scarcity of scientific governance. As tools like Braintrust and Langfuse make data curation easy, they inadvertently lower the barrier to 'optimizing against the test set,' creating a trust deficit in AI development. The insight is that the wedge for Sean's product is not better evals, but the enforcement of the 'scientific method' through structural constraints (holdouts, decision records) that existing tools ignore. This transforms the product from a utility into a compliance and integrity layer.
