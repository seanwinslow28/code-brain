## Synthesis

The tension between 'Taste as Evaluation Function' and the 'Verification-Governance Inversion' creates a legitimacy trap where creative authority is held hostage by unprovable accusations. Because accusers rely on taste (subjective aesthetic judgment) rather than activity proof (objective process evidence), creators are forced into an impossible defense: they must prove a negative against a moving standard of what 'human-made' looks like. This dynamic erodes the foundational trust required for any creative economy to function, as the cost of defending one's legitimacy exceeds the value of the work itself.
