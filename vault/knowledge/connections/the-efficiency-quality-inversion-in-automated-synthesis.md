## Synthesis

There is a tension between the efficiency of agent execution (uptime, success status) and the quality of the resulting semantic artifacts. As agents become more reliable and 'healthy', they may produce lower-value content if not constrained by rigorous rejection criteria. This inversion means that high operational uptime can mask a decline in the strategic value of the vault, leading to a false sense of progress.
