## Synthesis

The tension lies between the commoditization of AI models and the scarcity of reliable interaction protocols. By standardizing on a specific 'intent-engineering MCP server' and building an autonomous SDK fleet, Sean creates a moat around his expertise that cannot be easily replicated by those who only know how to prompt. The consequence is a redefinition of his role from a consumer of AI capabilities to an architect of the underlying infrastructure, which is critical for senior product roles where system design outweighs tactical execution.
