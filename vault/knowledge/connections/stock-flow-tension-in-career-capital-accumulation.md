## Synthesis

The tension lies between the agent fleet's optimization for high-frequency 'flow' (daily notes, status updates) and the career requirement for low-frequency 'stock' (durable artifacts, tested claims). When agents prioritize throughput, they generate ephemeral content that fails to compound into authority, creating a gap between operational activity and professional growth. This results in a system that is highly visible but strategically inert, mistaking volume for value.
