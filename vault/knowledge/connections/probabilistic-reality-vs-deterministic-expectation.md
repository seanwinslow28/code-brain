## Synthesis

The core tension in AI product management lies in the mismatch between the probabilistic nature of AI models and the deterministic expectations users have for software interfaces. While traditional software offers fixed logic, AI systems introduce variability through live datasets and environment loops, requiring a shift from static feature sets to dynamic network management. This creates a supervisory challenge where the PM must design constraints that hide this variability from the user while ensuring the underlying system remains robust against drift and edge cases.

### [[AI Product Manager]]

## Implications

- Sean must articulate his ability to manage 'probabilistic' products as a key differentiator in job interviews, showing he can bridge the gap between technical uncertainty and user reliability.
- Product designs should prioritize 'hiding' AI variability behind robust constraints rather than exposing it as a feature, unless explicitly designed for exploration.
