## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the tight verification loops that enable AI dominance in coding/math and the loose, decades-long loops that characterize scientific and creative progress. This asymmetry means that agentic systems optimized for RL on immediate feedback will fail catastrophically when applied to domains requiring 'Negative Capability'—the ability to hold imperfect models without resolution. The consequence is a structural blind spot where Sean might over-trust AI in strategic or creative domains, mistaking the absence of immediate error for correctness.

## Implications

- Sean should not use agentic workflows to 'solve' creative or strategic problems but rather to manage the ambiguity and track long-term signals where immediate verification is impossible.
- His vault synthesizer must be designed to flag 'premature closure' in its outputs, explicitly noting when a conclusion lacks sufficient verification loop data.
