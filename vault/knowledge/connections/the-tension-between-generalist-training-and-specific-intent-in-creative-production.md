## Synthesis

This connection reveals a fundamental tension between the LLM's nature as a generalist molded by 'thousands of OTHER people' and Sean's need for highly specific, idiosyncratic creative output. The consequence is that standard prompting fails because it attempts to bridge this gap with description rather than immersion; the only viable path is 'raising' the model through prolonged supervision and context compounding. This tension forces a shift in workflow from 'prompt engineering' to 'context architecture,' where the value lies in the accumulation of personal artifacts over time, creating a dependency on sustained human effort that cannot be automated away.

## Implications

- Sean must invest significant time in building and maintaining his 'reference universe' artifacts, as these become the primary leverage points for AI output quality.
- The 'Voiceprint Plugin' must be designed to enforce this context compounding process, preventing users from skipping the 'raising' phase in favor of one-shot prompts.
