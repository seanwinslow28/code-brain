## Synthesis

Sean's job hunt suffers from an illusion of health where high activity levels mask a lack of conversion at the critical bottleneck. The mechanism here is that general efficiency across all stages creates noise, while constraint-first focus creates signal. By subordinating non-bottleneck activities to the current limiting stage, he can reallocate his agent fleet's effort dynamically, preventing the waste of resources on stages that are already saturated.

### [[Constraint-First Automation vs. General Efficiency]]

## Implications

- Sean should implement a weekly bottleneck dashboard that tracks conversion rates at each stage, rather than just monitoring the uptime of his application feeds.
- When interview conversions drop, he must immediately subordinate portfolio production efforts to interview drills, even if the portfolio is incomplete, to address the current constraint.
