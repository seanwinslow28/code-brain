## Synthesis

The tension lies between the availability of empirical evidence (Braintrust's evaluation loop) and the authority to act on it (Sean's Golden Loop requirement). Braintrust provides a 'champion/challenger' mechanism that generates data but lacks the 'written promote/reject decision record' necessary for governance. This creates a 'Verification-Governance Inversion' where Sean must build the missing decision layer himself, effectively turning an evaluation tool into a partial workflow engine.
