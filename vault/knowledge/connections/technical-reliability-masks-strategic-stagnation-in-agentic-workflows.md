## Synthesis

The core tension lies in the decoupling between technical success metrics and strategic progress indicators within Sean's agentic infrastructure. Agents report 'status=success' based on process completion while producing no actionable value, creating a dangerous blind spot where operational health masks strategic failure. This disconnect forces Sean to manually verify output quality rather than trusting automated health reports, undermining the efficiency gains of automation.
