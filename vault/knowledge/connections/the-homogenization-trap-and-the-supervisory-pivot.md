## Synthesis

The core tension arises from the convergence of AI models toward 'median prose,' which systematically erodes stylistic uniqueness despite explicit instructions to preserve it. This homogenization trap forces a strategic pivot where the creator's value shifts from generation to rigorous supervision, requiring new tools that enforce intent specs as measurable constraints. The consequence is that taste becomes a scarce, high-value resource that must be protected by automated verification gates rather than relied upon in prompts.
