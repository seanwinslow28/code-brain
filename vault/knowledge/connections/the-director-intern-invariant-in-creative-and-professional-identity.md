## Synthesis

Sean’s professional identity crisis is resolved by reframing the human-AI relationship through the 'Director-Intern' invariant, where human value is decoupled from manual execution and re-coupled to supervisory taste. This tension manifests across his creative output (Pencil & Prompt) and his job hunt, where he must demonstrate that his 'earned taste' serves as a viable evaluation function for AI agents. The consequence is a unified strategy: his portfolio and publication both serve as evidence that he has successfully transitioned from the 'maker' role to the 'director' role, leveraging automation to scale his creative authority rather than replace it.
