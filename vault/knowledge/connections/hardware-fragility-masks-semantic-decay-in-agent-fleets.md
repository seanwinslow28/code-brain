## Synthesis

There is a critical tension between the operational visibility of agent health metrics and the semantic integrity of their outputs, driven by underlying infrastructure fragility. This leads to a dangerous illusion of health where systems report as stable while actually producing shallow or empty outputs, masking the true degradation of Sean's knowledge synthesis capabilities.
