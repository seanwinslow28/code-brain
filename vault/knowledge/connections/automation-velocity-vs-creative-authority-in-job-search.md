## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the velocity of automated job scraping and the authority required to make high-stakes career decisions. As the system increases its throughput (scraping 4 feeds, generating 8 entries), it dilutes the quality of the signal, forcing Sean to spend more time filtering noise than engaging with opportunities. This erodes his creative authority because he is no longer directing the search but reacting to the system's output, which prioritizes volume over fit.

## Implications

- Sean should reduce the number of feeds or increase the strictness of the initial filtering to prevent low-signal data from entering the workflow.
- The definition of 'success' in the job hunt must shift from 'number of entries processed' to 'number of high-fit opportunities engaged'.
