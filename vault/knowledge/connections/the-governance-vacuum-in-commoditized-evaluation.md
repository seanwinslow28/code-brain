## Synthesis

There is a structural tension between the commoditization of AI evaluation machinery and the scarcity of trustworthy governance protocols. As tools like Braintrust and Langfuse make data curation easy, they inadvertently create a 'governance vacuum' where rich comparison data exists but lacks the authoritative decision layer to act upon it. This inversion forces Sean to build custom 'decision record' artifacts to bridge the gap, turning evaluation tools into partial workflow engines and establishing 'Supervision as the New AI Edge' through rigorous epistemic hygiene.
