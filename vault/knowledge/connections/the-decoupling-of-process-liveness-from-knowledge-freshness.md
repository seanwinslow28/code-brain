## Synthesis

The fleet's monitoring layer conflates process liveness with functional utility, creating a blind spot where agents can be 'healthy' while contributing nothing to Sean's knowledge base. This decoupling means that operational metrics (uptime, success codes) are poor proxies for epistemic health. The consequence is that Sean may perceive his system as robust when it is actually stagnant, leading to false confidence in the freshness of his insights and job-hunt materials.
