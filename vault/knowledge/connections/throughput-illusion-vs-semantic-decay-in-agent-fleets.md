## Synthesis

The tension lies between the observable metric of automation velocity (concepts written) and the unobservable metric of semantic integrity (conceptual depth). As Sean's fleet scaled from 3 concepts to 150+ per run, the system optimized for volume, inadvertently triggering 'model collapse' dynamics where synthetic data degrades authenticity. This creates a dangerous feedback loop: higher throughput masks deeper knowledge erosion, leading to a false sense of progress while the underlying intellectual infrastructure decays.
