## Synthesis

Comedic scenes operate under a hidden 'protocol' of social rules (status, politeness, law) that characters attempt to follow or break. However, the 'regulatory ambiguity' of the environment (drunk bystanders, broken stalls, random violence) creates a zone where these protocols fail. The consequence is that characters must improvise new, often absurd, rules in real-time. This mirrors Sean's own work in AI agent fleets, where rigid automation protocols often break against the ambiguity of real-world data or user intent, requiring 'resilience' rather than just 'fixes'.
