## Synthesis

The tension between cheap and expensive verification creates a hard boundary for where AI can autonomously compound value versus where it requires human-in-the-loop governance. In Sean's job hunt, this means automated application tracking can scale because status updates are binary, while creative writing cannot because taste is subjective. This forces a hybrid architecture where agents handle the 'cheap' verification tasks and humans handle the 'expensive' ones.

## Implications

- Sean should automate only those job-hunt tasks with binary verification (e.g., application status) and keep creative writing in the loop.
- His AI PM curriculum must teach students to identify and lower verification costs as a primary design pattern.
