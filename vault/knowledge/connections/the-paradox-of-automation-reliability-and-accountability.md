## Synthesis

Increasing automation reliability often exacerbates the accountability gap because it removes human operators from the loop before errors can be caught. As systems become more autonomous, the ability to trace responsibility for failures diminishes, creating a 'blind spot' where high reliability coexists with low trustworthiness. This paradox forces Sean to choose between efficient but opaque automation and slower, more accountable manual processes.
