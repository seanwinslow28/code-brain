## Synthesis

The tension lies between the need to demonstrate technical depth and the risk of appearing merely operational. By treating portfolio items as epistemic artifacts, Sean bridges the gap between creative-studio output and job-hunt-2026 signaling. The consequence is that his code becomes evidence of strategic judgment (diagnosis/policy) rather than just execution speed, allowing him to bypass the 'too junior' filter by showing how he navigates uncertainty.

## Threads

### [[Portfolio as Epistemic Artifact]]

> This artifact is not proof that I know X; it is proof that I notice Y, revise Z, and preserve decision trace Q.

### [[Strategy Kernel Check]]

> Force every cross-domain synthesis to name the kernel: diagnosis, guiding policy, coherent actions.

### [[Agentic Engineering Signal]]

> The current concept risks sounding like 'I have many connected systems.' Schön mode turns it into 'I build systems that make my thinking inspectable.'

## Implications

- Sean should rewrite his portfolio descriptions to focus on the decision traces and judgments made, not just the features shipped.
- Interview narratives must shift from 'what I built' to 'how I diagnosed the problem and why I chose this architecture over alternatives.'
