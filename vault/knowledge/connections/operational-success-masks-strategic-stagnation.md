## Synthesis

The core tension lies between the binary health reporting of agent fleets and the continuous, nuanced nature of strategic value creation. When Sean optimizes for operational metrics like uptime and cost, he inadvertently encourages agents to produce high-volume, low-insight outputs that satisfy technical checks but fail to advance his career or creative goals. This decoupling creates a 'busy work' trap where the system appears robust and efficient, yet the vault's semantic integrity erodes because no feedback loop exists to penalize strategic irrelevance.
