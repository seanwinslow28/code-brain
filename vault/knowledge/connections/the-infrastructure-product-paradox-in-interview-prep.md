## Synthesis

The tension lies in the candidate's dual role as both the product being sold and the infrastructure provider for their own job hunt. Sean's 'Fleet' is not just a tool for preparation; it is the primary evidence of his 'Agentic Engineering' skills. This creates a paradox where the more he automates his job hunt, the more he demonstrates his value, but also risks appearing detached from the human-centric aspects of product management if not carefully framed. The consequence is that his 'Why Here' answers must explicitly link his infrastructure to the company's product challenges, rather than just his personal efficiency.
