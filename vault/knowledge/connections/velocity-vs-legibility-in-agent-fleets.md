## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the increasing velocity of automated execution and the decreasing legibility of its outputs. As Sean scales the concept generation from 3 to 153 concepts per run, the mechanisms for reporting status lag behind, creating a legibility gap. This forces supervision to shift from strategic oversight to forensic debugging, eroding trust in the system's health metrics.
