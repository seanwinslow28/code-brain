## Synthesis

The core tension lies between the need for precise brand identity and the inability of AI models to execute literal instructions. This forces a shift from positive instruction to negative constraint, where brand safety is enforced by excluding problematic elements (text, logos) rather than including them. The consequence is that creative governance becomes a process of defining boundaries through abstraction, turning the model's limitations into a structural feature of the brand identity.
