## Synthesis

The tension between the promise of decentralized agent commerce and the reality of centralized monetization chokepoints creates a strategic trap for builders. While startups like Skyfire and Nekuda propose new layers of identity and payment, they are backed by incumbents (Akamai, Coinbase) who control the underlying distribution surfaces. This means that any attempt to build 'agentic rails' inevitably reinforces the power of existing infrastructure providers, as value is captured through tolling and verification rather than open protocol adoption. The consequence is that Sean's creative or job-hunt efforts must either accept this toll structure or find ways to bypass it entirely, which requires significant technical leverage.

## Implications

- Sean should avoid building tools that rely on proprietary agent payment layers unless he controls the identity verification step.
- Strategic decisions must be based on independent demand signals rather than vendor-funded market research to avoid being misled by inflated valuations.
