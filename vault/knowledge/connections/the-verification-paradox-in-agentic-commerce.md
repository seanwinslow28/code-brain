## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the automation of market analysis and the need for independent verification. Agents are designed to synthesize information quickly, but they often inherit the biases and inaccuracies of their source data (vendor claims). This creates a 'verification paradox' where the more agents automate the process, the less reliable the output becomes unless explicitly constrained by independent, human-verified epistemic artifacts. The consequence is that true market insight requires breaking the automation loop to insert manual, evidence-grade checks.
