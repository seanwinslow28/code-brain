## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the decreasing cost of content generation and the increasing cost of its verification. As agents become more capable, they produce more plausible errors that are harder to detect, creating a 'verification trap' where efficiency gains in creation are offset by losses in review. This pattern suggests that future value lies not in faster output, but in tools that make verification cheaper and more reliable.
