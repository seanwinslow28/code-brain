## Synthesis

Sean faces a structural tension where the engineering desire for simplified, aggregate health metrics clashes with the operational reality of complex human-agent joint cognition. By prioritizing dashboard simplicity to reduce cognitive load, he inadvertently creates legibility debt that masks hidden coordination failures at agent handoff boundaries. This forces a critical design choice: either accept superficial reliability indicators that hide systemic fragility or invest in deep resilience engineering that exposes the messy, granular reality of agent dependencies.
