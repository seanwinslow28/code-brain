## Synthesis

As AI generation capabilities improve, the cost of verification increases relative to the cost of production, creating an inversion where the creator's primary value shifts from making to judging. This tension arises because the 'taste-fidelity decoupling' means that generic output is often indistinguishable from good output without deep inspection. The consequence is that Sean must build infrastructure (like his critique analyzer) not just to generate content, but to automate the proof of his unique taste, otherwise he becomes a supervisor of slop rather than a creator.
