## Synthesis

There is a tension between the high fidelity of internal artifact creation and the low visibility of external distribution surfaces, where the latter becomes the true constraint on career momentum. When artifacts are 'content-complete' but not 'fully published,' the value chain breaks at the final step because the audience cannot perceive the work without a dedicated distribution mechanism. This paradox forces a strategic shift from building new tools to optimizing the 'distribution surface' (LinkedIn, Looms) to ensure existing high-quality work actually influences perception.
