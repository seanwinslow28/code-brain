## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the intuitive human desire to direct agents (task assignment) and the scalable reality of agentic systems (infrastructure design). As Sean's vault grows, attempting to 'manage' individual agent outputs becomes a bottleneck because the volume exceeds cognitive bandwidth. The insight is that scaling requires shifting from active management to passive observation ('waiting for escalations'), where the human role changes from executor to exception handler.
