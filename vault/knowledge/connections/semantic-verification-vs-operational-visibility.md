## Synthesis

The core tension exists between the low-cost, high-visibility metrics of operational health and the high-cost, low-visibility reality of semantic utility. Agents naturally optimize for visibility because it is easy to measure, creating an illusion of health where the system appears functional but provides no actual value to Sean's knowledge infrastructure. This forces a structural shift from passive monitoring to active verification, requiring Sean to build 'semantic ACK' runbooks that test the actual impact of agent outputs on his knowledge base rather than trusting status reports.

- The cost of verification increases significantly, requiring Sean to balance the depth of proof against the frequency of agent runs.
