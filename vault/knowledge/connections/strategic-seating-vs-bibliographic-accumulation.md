## Synthesis

The tension lies between the agent's natural tendency to accumulate comprehensive data (bibliographic accumulation) and Sean's requirement for functional utility (strategic seating). Agents are optimized to capture 'everything' about a book, but Sean's infrastructure requires 'only what fits the seat.' This creates a friction point where high-fidelity extraction is actively suppressed to maintain low-latency decision-making. The consequence is a corpus that is sparse in content but dense in strategic alignment, preventing the 'fidelity trap' from degrading his product craft.
