## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the operational cost of maintaining open source and the professional signal it generates. Publishing creates dependency demand that can become an unfunded obligation if maintenance supply cannot keep up. This forces a strategic choice: either limit scope to ensure sustainable maintenance or leverage the project as a calibrated trust signal for job hunting. The consequence is that Sean must treat his public work as a managed product with clear boundaries, rather than an endless commitment.
