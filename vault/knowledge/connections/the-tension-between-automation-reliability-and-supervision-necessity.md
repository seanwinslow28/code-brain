## Synthesis

The core tension lies in the assumption that automation can be fully reliable without constant supervision, which is contradicted by the prevalence of silent failures in Sean's agent fleet. This contradiction forces a re-evaluation of where value is created: in the automation itself or in the supervision that ensures its correctness. The consequence is that as automation complexity increases, the cost of supervision must also increase to maintain integrity, challenging the notion that automation reduces workload.
