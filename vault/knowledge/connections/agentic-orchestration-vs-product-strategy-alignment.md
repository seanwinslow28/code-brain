## Synthesis

The tension lies between the technical complexity of agentic orchestration (requiring deep understanding of context windows and session states) and the product strategy goal of making these tools accessible to non-coders. As Anthropic pushes Cowork to bring these capabilities to non-coding work, the 'edge' shifts from those who can code the agents to those who can design the supervision and context management frameworks. This creates a new role for AI Product Managers who must define the 'rules of engagement' for these autonomous systems.
