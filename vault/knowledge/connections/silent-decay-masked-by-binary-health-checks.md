## Synthesis

The fleet's binary health reporting creates a dangerous blind spot where semantic decay is invisible to the operator. When agents report 'success' or 'empty-queue', they mask the lack of meaningful output, leading Sean to believe his knowledge base is growing when it may be stagnating. This tension between technical uptime and cognitive utility requires a shift from status-based monitoring to value-based verification.
