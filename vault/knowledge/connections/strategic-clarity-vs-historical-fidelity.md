## Synthesis

The tension between maintaining a lean strategic overview and preserving a rich historical record is resolved by decoupling the planning layer from the execution layer. This pattern prevents the 'bloat' that typically degrades the usability of living documents, ensuring that the strategic roadmap remains a tool for decision-making rather than a repository of history. The consequence is that agents can operate on the current state without being distracted by past iterations, while still having access to the full context when needed.
