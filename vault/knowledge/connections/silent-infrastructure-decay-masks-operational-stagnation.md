## Synthesis

The fleet's binary health reporting creates a blind spot where critical infrastructure failures (like the Alienware offline status) are treated as non-events by agents that do not depend on them directly. This leads to a state of 'functional stagnation' where the system appears healthy but is actually losing capability, specifically in deep research and cross-machine synchronization. The consequence is that Sean's daily note generation continues uninterrupted, giving him a false sense of continuity while his actual knowledge synthesis capabilities are degrading due to missing data sources.
