## Synthesis

The tension between generic competence and specific indispensability is critical for Sean's job hunt. In the AI/Agentic space, many candidates claim 'AI fluency' or 'innovation,' which are invisible differentiators. By anchoring his portfolio in specific, verifiable outcomes (like 'agentic checkout'), Sean creates a high-signal filter that attracts relevant opportunities while repelling generic ones. This specificity reduces the cognitive load on hiring managers, making Sean's value proposition immediately recognizable and memorable.
