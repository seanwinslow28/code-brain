## Synthesis

The tension lies between the visible metrics of agent activity (clusters sampled, concepts written) and the invisible metric of strategic value generation. As software becomes cheap, high activity can mask low value creation, creating an 'illusion of competence.' The consequence is that Sean may optimize for throughput (more runs, more concepts) while neglecting the harder task of ensuring those outputs actually contribute to his GDP or creative goals. This requires a shift from monitoring uptime to monitoring 'taste' and 'alignment'.
