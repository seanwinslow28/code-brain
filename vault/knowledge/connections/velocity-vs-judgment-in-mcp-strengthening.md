## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the velocity of automated synthesis and the costliness of signals required for career differentiation. High-velocity automation tends to produce de-differentiated content that fails to stand out in competitive job markets. This creates a paradox where increasing efficiency actually reduces the strategic value of the output, forcing Sean to introduce artificial friction to maintain quality.
