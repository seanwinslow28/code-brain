## Synthesis

There is a critical tension between the velocity of automated production and the necessity of human verification, where the latter becomes the primary bottleneck for scaling agentic systems. As Sean’s own fleet metrics show, high rejection rates (e.g., 78 rejections in one run) indicate that automation alone cannot guarantee quality; instead, governance must shift from pre-emptive rule-setting to post-hoc demonstration and verification. This inversion means that the value of an AI-native professional is no longer defined by their ability to write code or prompts, but by their ability to design systems where failures are visible and correctable through live interaction rather than silent decay.
