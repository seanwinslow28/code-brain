## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the goal of automating creative output and the necessity of human taste to define quality. As automation improves, the marginal value of generation decreases while the marginal value of curation increases. This means that investing heavily in automation without first solidifying one's own taste criteria leads to high-effort, low-value outputs.

- The 'weeks of work' spent building the skill may have been misallocated if the core issue was taste definition rather than technical implementation.
