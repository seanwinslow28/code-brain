## Synthesis

This connection reveals a critical tension where the efficiency of AI generation is directly offset by the increasing cognitive load of supervision, creating a bottleneck that undermines Sean's creative authority. As the cost of generating mediocre content drops to zero, the value of Sean's specific taste becomes the only differentiator, yet the tools encourage him to outsource the judgment that defines that taste. This leads to a paradox where he must work harder to supervise the machine than he would have to create the work himself, effectively reversing the efficiency gains of automation and eroding his tacit knowledge through reliance on surface-level metrics.
