## Synthesis

Sean's deep operational mastery of his agent fleet creates a tension with the need for architectural clarity. His ability to run the system successfully masks the lack of explicit vocabulary and decision-making frameworks, leading to an 'illusion of competence' where he believes he understands the system's design because he can operate it. This tension is critical because as the system scales or encounters novel failures, his intuitive knowledge may be insufficient, requiring a shift from tacit operation to explicit architectural reasoning.
