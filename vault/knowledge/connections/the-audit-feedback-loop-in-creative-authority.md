## Synthesis

There is a critical tension between Sean's role as a creative author and his role as a system supervisor. As he automates more of the creative process (via VoicePrint, MCPs), he risks becoming a passive consumer of machine-generated 'correctness' rather than an active shaper of taste. The consequence is that his creative authority may become dependent on the very tools he builds, creating a closed loop where his output reflects the limitations of his own evaluation metrics.
