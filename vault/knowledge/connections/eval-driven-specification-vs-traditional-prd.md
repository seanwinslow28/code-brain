## Synthesis

The tension lies in the shift from static documentation to dynamic validation. Traditional PRDs are read; eval-driven specs are executed. This creates a higher barrier to entry for specification quality but reduces ambiguity in implementation. The consequence is that the 'spec' becomes a living, testable artifact rather than a historical record of intent.
