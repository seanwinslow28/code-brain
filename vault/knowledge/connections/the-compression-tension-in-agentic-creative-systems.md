## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the richness of source material (feature-length scripts) and the constraints of the output medium (3–5 minute shorts). This connection reveals that successful agentic creativity requires an intermediate 'Architect' layer that explicitly defines the compression invariant. Without this layer, the agent faces an unbounded search space, leading to 'aimless' output; with it, the agent is forced to make specific structural trade-offs, such as sacrificing subtext for pace or visual metaphor for dialogue density.
