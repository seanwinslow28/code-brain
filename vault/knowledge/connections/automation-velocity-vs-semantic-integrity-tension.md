## Synthesis

There is a critical tension between the velocity of automated synthesis and the integrity of semantic meaning. As Sean increases the scale of his agent fleet (more concepts, more runs), the risk of 'Shifting the Burden' grows: he may prioritize throughput over the deep, manual verification required to maintain strategic clarity. The consequence is that the vault becomes a repository of plausible but hollow artifacts, where the appearance of progress masks a decay in actual insight quality.
