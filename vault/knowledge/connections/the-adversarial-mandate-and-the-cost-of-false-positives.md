## Synthesis

There is a critical tension between the need for rigorous quality assurance and the risk of generating 'manufactured findings' when the evaluation mandate is purely adversarial. When agents are instructed to find flaws without a mechanism to verify their existence or a threshold for severity, they produce noise that mimics signal, leading to unnecessary re-work and potential degradation of creative assets. This pattern mirrors the financial observability issue where residual state creates false alarms about budget health, suggesting a broader systemic vulnerability in Sean's infrastructure: the failure to distinguish between genuine defects/errors and artifacts of the evaluation process itself.
