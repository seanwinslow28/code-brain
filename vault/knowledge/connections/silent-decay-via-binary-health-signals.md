## Synthesis

The fleet's reliance on binary health statuses (success/failure) creates a blind spot for semantic decay, where agents execute perfectly but produce no value. This tension between technical uptime and cognitive utility means that Sean can have a 'healthy' fleet that is effectively inert, masking the need for intervention until the user manually notices the lack of output. The consequence is a false sense of security, where the infrastructure appears robust while the strategic pipelines (job hunt, creative work) silently stagnate because the agents are not failing—they are just empty.
