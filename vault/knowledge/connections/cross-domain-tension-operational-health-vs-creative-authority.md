## Synthesis

This connection reveals a critical tension where the scalability of Sean's agent fleet is limited by his cognitive capacity to supervise outputs, rather than by computational constraints. As the fleet scales, the volume of 'healthy' but semantically irrelevant outputs increases, forcing Sean into a supervision loop that undermines his creative authority. The consequence is that automation intended to reduce cognitive load actually increases it, creating a hard cap on effective scaling.

## Implications

- Sean must replace uptime dashboards with a 'Creative Progress Ledger' that tracks human acceptance rates rather than agent health.
- Agents that consistently produce healthy but irrelevant outputs should be deleted rather than maintained to reduce supervision load.
