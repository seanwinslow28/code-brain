## Synthesis

Sean's agent fleet exhibits a critical tension where operational health metrics are decoupled from strategic efficacy, creating a blind spot where the infrastructure appears robust but is functionally limited. The mechanism involves agents reporting 'healthy' status based on process uptime while failing to execute high-leverage tasks due to semantic failures or dependency blocks. This results in a false sense of progress and undetected productivity loss, as the monitoring system validates existence rather than value, preventing Sean from identifying when his vault is stagnating despite appearing active.

## Implications

- Sean must redefine 'health' metrics to include semantic output quality and task completion against strategic goals, not just process uptime.
- The current monitoring setup fails to detect when agents are idle or blocked by infrastructure limitations like missing MCP connections, leading to undetected productivity loss.
