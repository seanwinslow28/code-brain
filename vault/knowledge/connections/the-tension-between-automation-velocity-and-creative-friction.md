## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the drive for automation velocity and the need for creative friction. Sean's agentic workflows are optimized to accelerate output, but this efficiency can suppress the 'negative capability' required for original insight. The consequence is that his systems may become too smooth, eliminating the productive interruptions that force deeper reinterpretation of intent. To maintain creative agency, he must intentionally engineer constraints into his automation pipelines.

## Threads

### [[Negative Capability / Failure Literacy]]

> The concept currently treats agents as automation leverage: Sean has a workflow, agents accelerate or critique it. Missing is the opposite mode: agents that introduce productive constraint, interruption, and lateral pressure.

### [[Agentic Experience Design]]

> Agents should expose playable controls, not only produce final outputs, allowing the creative system to be performed, steered, and improvised in real time.

### [[Intent Engineering]]

> Intent specs are not command structures; they are navigational artifacts agents must reinterpret against live context.

## Implications

- Sean should design his portfolio projects to explicitly showcase 'friction points' where agents challenge his initial assumptions, rather than just executing them flawlessly.
- His job hunt materials should highlight his ability to balance automation efficiency with creative oversight, positioning him as a designer of complex systems rather than just an implementer.
