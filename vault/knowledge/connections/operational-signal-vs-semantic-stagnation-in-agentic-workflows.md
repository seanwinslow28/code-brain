## Synthesis

There is a critical tension between the agents' successful execution of low-level protocols and the stagnation of high-value strategic outcomes. The fleet reports 'healthy' status based on process completion, while knowledge integrity depends on the synthesis of novel connections that require specific hardware and active research queues. This decoupling creates a dangerous feedback loop where Sean perceives his infrastructure as robust while his knowledge base suffers from semantic decay, leading to a false sense of progress.
