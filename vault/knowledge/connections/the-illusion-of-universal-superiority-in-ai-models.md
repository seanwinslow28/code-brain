## Synthesis

The tension lies between the allure of high benchmark scores and the reality of context-dependent performance. While Opus 4.8 leads in overall scores, its superiority is not universal; it excels in specific areas like source discipline but fails in long-horizon tasks compared to other models. This pattern reveals that the 'smartest' model is not always the most useful, and the consequence is a need for nuanced, task-specific model selection rather than a default preference for the highest scorer.
