## Synthesis

The core tension lies in the need for a highly specific, tailored preparation strategy (the playbook) while simultaneously adapting to the highly variable and unpredictable nature of the interview format (vibe-coding). This creates a paradox where over-preparation can lead to rigidity, while under-preparation leads to generic responses. The consequence is that Sean must develop a meta-strategy that allows him to quickly diagnose the interview format and apply the appropriate heuristics from his playbook without getting stuck in a pre-rehearsed script.

- The playbook must be designed as a modular set of heuristics rather than a linear script to allow for dynamic application during the interview.
