## Synthesis

The pattern emerges when the desire to build an artifact conflicts with the need to prove causal demand, creating a risk of slogan generation without evidence. By forcing an assumption ledger before any code is written, Sean shifts from declaration to demonstration, requiring tiny demos or Substack experiments to pass/fail thresholds. This tension ensures that his portfolio projects are grounded in verified user behavior rather than hypothetical utility, reducing the cost of failure.
