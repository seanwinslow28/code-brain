## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between operational health metrics and strategic value generation in Sean's agent fleet. Agents report 'healthy' or 'success' based on technical execution, while their actual contribution to Sean's goals (job applications, creative synthesis) may be zero or degraded. This decoupling creates a false sense of security where the infrastructure appears robust while the underlying strategic pipelines are silently stalling or producing low-value output.
