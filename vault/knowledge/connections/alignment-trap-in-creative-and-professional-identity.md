## Synthesis

The mechanism of audience capture creates a feedback loop where the agent fleet optimizes for existing audience preferences, reinforcing Sean's current identity rather than allowing it to evolve. This alignment trap prevents the development of new skills or narratives that might initially underperform but offer long-term strategic advantage. The consequence is a static professional profile that fails to reflect Sean's actual learning frontier, leading to a mismatch between his capabilities and his market positioning.
