## Synthesis

The tension lies between the internal satisfaction of building complex, integrated agentic systems and the external requirement for legible signals that trigger recruiter interest. Sean’s current infrastructure prioritizes internal coherence (integration, automation) over external legibility (clear, concise demonstrations of value). This leads to a misallocation of effort where 'inventory' (demos, dashboards) accumulates without increasing interview throughput. The consequence is a job hunt that feels productive internally but fails to convert externally because the signal is obscured by complexity.

## Implications

- Sean must stop building new integrations until he has validated that existing ones are legible to recruiters.
- He should pivot from 'building more' to 'demonstrating less but clearer,' focusing on high-signal artifacts.
