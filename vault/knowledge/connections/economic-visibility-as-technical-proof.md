## Synthesis

The tension lies between the abstract nature of AI cost models and the concrete need for recruiters to verify technical competence. By making the economic invariant visible through an interactive tool, Sean converts a financial metric into a demonstration of engineering rigor. This shifts the evaluation from 'can you code?' to 'can you build systems that optimize for business reality?', which is a higher-order signal for AI Product Manager roles.
