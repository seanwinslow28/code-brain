## Synthesis

The tension between maintaining high operational uptime and preserving cognitive utility reveals a systemic risk where automation creates an illusion of productivity while masking semantic decay. As the fleet scales to maintain constant sampling rates, the volume of generated artifacts outpaces Sean's capacity for verification, leading to a trust deficit that forces manual intervention. This pattern demonstrates that scaling automation without scaling human oversight results in diminishing returns, as the cost of judgment exceeds the value of the generated concepts.
