## Synthesis

There is a direct tension between the density of constraints applied to Sean's job-hunt and content agents and the coherence of the resulting narrative. As constraint density increases (e.g., strict word locks, multiple negative rules), the agent's ability to maintain a consistent voice and logical flow degrades exponentially. This means that overly rigid automation in his resume or Substack drafts leads to fragmented, low-quality outputs that require more human intervention than less constrained, iterative approaches.

## Implications

- Sean should abandon the 'generation-time law layer' approach for his job-hunt agents and instead use post-draft verification to maintain narrative coherence.
- The current high-volume content machine runs (e.g., 123 concepts in run-2026-08-15) may be producing low-value artifacts due to this inversion, requiring a reduction in constraint density.
