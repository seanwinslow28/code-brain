## Synthesis

Sean's agent fleet exhibits a critical tension between operational reliability and semantic validity, where technical robustness masks the absence of human-centric data layers. The system excels at internal consistency—rejecting thin articles and maintaining provenance—but fails to address external validity problems arising from user choice dynamics and conflicting data incentives. This creates a 'competence illusion' that prevents Sean from recognizing his unpreparedness for AI PM roles, which require understanding the economic and user-choice layers that define real-world data quality.

## Implications

- Sean must stop treating his current indexing pipeline as sufficient preparation for AI PM roles, recognizing that technical robustness does not equate to product sense.
- Any future agent deployment must include explicit mechanisms for tracking how user choices reshape the data distribution, rather than assuming static data quality.
