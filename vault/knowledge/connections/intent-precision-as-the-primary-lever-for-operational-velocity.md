## Synthesis

The tension lies between the desire for high-throughput content generation and the reality that low-fidelity output creates a supervisory bottleneck that negates any speed gains. The underlying pattern is that model capability acts as a multiplier on intent precision: better models reduce the 'taste gap' even with moderate constraints, while weaker models require exhaustive constraint engineering to avoid rejection. The consequence is that Sean should prioritize model upgrades and intent refinement over increasing sampling volume, as the latter only amplifies the cost of poor supervision.
