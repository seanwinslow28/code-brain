## Synthesis

There is a critical tension between the fleet's technical health metrics and its strategic utility. Agents report 'healthy' status while producing zero value (e.g., job-feed fetching 0 items) or minimal semantic output (synthesizer with only 3 connections). This creates a dangerous blind spot where Sean perceives his infrastructure as robust while his actual goals (job applications, creative synthesis) are stagnating. The consequence is that maintenance efforts are misallocated to keeping 'healthy' but useless agents running, rather than fixing the silent decay in high-value pipelines.
