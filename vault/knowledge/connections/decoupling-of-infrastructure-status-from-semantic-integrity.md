## Synthesis

Sean's fleet exhibits a critical tension where infrastructure health metrics no longer correlate with the semantic validity of the knowledge vault. Agents may report 'healthy' status while delivering stale context, creating an illusion of competence that masks cognitive decay. This decoupling forces Sean to shift from trusting automated outputs to auditing them, turning his role from creator to auditor and increasing the risk of missed opportunities in his job hunt.
