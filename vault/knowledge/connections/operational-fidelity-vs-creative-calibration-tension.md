## Synthesis

This tension arises from the incompatibility between deterministic operational protocols and non-deterministic aesthetic calibration, where the very mechanisms that ensure reliability (explicit instructions) actively hinder the development of taste. As Sean's automation scales, his operational health improves, but his creative voice requires a separate, manual calibration layer that does not scale with his automation. The consequence is a structural decoupling where Sean must maintain two distinct mental models: one for execution and one for evaluation, creating a bottleneck in his creative output.
