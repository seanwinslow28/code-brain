## Synthesis

There is a critical tension between the rapid adoption of generative AI tools by creative teams and the lack of governance mechanisms tailored to their specific needs. While ML engineers have robust eval tooling, creative and marketing teams are left with uncontrolled generation that lacks traceability and accountability. This gap creates an opportunity for Sean to position his existing intent-engineering infrastructure as the missing 'judgment layer' that enables safe, scalable AI adoption in non-technical domains.

## Implications

- Sean can differentiate his offerings by focusing on governance and control rather than generation capabilities, targeting a market segment currently underserved by existing tooling.
- The translation of engineer-centric intent engineering into creative-friendly interfaces becomes a key product strategy for capturing this market.
