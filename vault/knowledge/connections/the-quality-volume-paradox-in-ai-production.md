## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the democratization of AI tools and the preservation of artistic quality. As tools like Gemini Omni and Kling lower the barrier to entry, the market is flooded with 'slop' that lacks emotional resonance. This creates a paradox where the value of AI-generated content is inversely proportional to its ease of generation. The consequence is that the 'edge' in the industry is no longer access to the tool, but the ability to supervise and curate the output to meet human standards of quality.
