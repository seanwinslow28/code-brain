## Synthesis

There is a direct trade-off between the velocity of automated synthesis and the viability of Sean’s manual fallback capabilities. As the fleet becomes more reliable, it erodes his tacit knowledge of the underlying processes, creating a hidden dependency on the automation itself. This tension means that high automation scores may mask a growing inability to recover from total system failure, which is critical for a professional who must demonstrate deep technical competence.
