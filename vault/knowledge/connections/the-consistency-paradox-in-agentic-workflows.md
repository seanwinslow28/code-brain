## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the 'memoryless' nature of current AI generation and the 'persistent identity' requirements of professional creative work. This paradox forces users to act as the external memory layer, manually re-anchoring context for every independent sampling event. The consequence is that automation velocity is capped by the human's ability to maintain state, creating a bottleneck where the tool's power is neutralized by its lack of continuity.
