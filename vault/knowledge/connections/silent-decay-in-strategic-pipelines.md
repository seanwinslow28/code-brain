## Synthesis

The tension between operational health and cognitive utility creates a 'silent decay' where strategic pipelines (like job hunting or knowledge synthesis) degrade without alerting the user. Because agents like job-feed report 'healthy' despite fetching zero items, Sean receives no signal that his strategic input is missing. This leads to a false sense of progress while the underlying data infrastructure atrophies, as the system optimizes for running rather than achieving.

## Implications

- Sean must implement 'semantic health checks' that verify data content (e.g., non-zero job scores) rather than just API success codes.
- The fleet's definition of 'healthy' needs to be redefined to include the freshness and relevance of its output, not just its execution status.
