## Synthesis

The tension lies between using markets to aggregate beliefs and the risk of letting price signals dictate value judgments. When agents treat Polymarket merely as a cash-flow surface, they fail to distinguish between pricing an event and deciding its importance. This leads to a failure mode where agents summarize market narratives without maintaining falsifiable theses. The consequence is a lack of calibrated forecast updates, which undermines the credibility of Sean's research outputs for both job interviews and creative projects.
