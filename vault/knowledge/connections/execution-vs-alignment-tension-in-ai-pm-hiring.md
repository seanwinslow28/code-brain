## Synthesis

Anthropic's interview loop creates a tension between demonstrating technical fluency through live execution (Vibe-Coding) and proving cultural resonance through narrative authenticity (Culture Interview). The consequence is that candidates cannot succeed by optimizing for one at the expense of the other; they must simultaneously prove they can build with AI tools and embody the company's values. This dual requirement forces a synthesis of technical capability and personal identity, making the interview process a holistic test of fit rather than just skill.
