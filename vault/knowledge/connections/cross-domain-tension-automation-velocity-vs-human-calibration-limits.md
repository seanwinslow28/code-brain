## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the exponential velocity of automated synthesis and the linear capacity of human calibration. As Sean's fleet increases its output (e.g., 125 concepts in one run), the marginal utility of each concept drops because his ability to verify and integrate them does not scale proportionally. This creates a 'calibration bottleneck' where the system's overall value is capped by Sean's attention, not the agents' processing power.
