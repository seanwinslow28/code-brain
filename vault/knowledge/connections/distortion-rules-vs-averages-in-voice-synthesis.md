## Synthesis

The mechanism of voice synthesis shifts from averaging influences to applying distortion rules that preserve the writer's agency. Averaging creates a bland composite that lacks distinctiveness, while distortion rules allow for productive misreading of precursors. This tension highlights the difference between imitation and influence: one consumes the source, the other metabolizes it. Sean’s system must enforce these rules to avoid becoming a 'cover band' of his literary heroes.

## Implications

- Sean needs a 'voice-mode runbook' that specifies which influences to deform for each specific writing mode.
- The system must explicitly forbid certain stylistic elements from specific influences to prevent cosplay.
