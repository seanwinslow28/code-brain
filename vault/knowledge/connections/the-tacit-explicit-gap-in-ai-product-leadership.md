## Synthesis

This connection reveals the critical tension between Sean's current operational strength (tacit, instinctive management of a complex fleet) and his future career goal (explicit, transferable design thinking). The mechanism at play is that high automation can mask the lack of foundational knowledge, creating an 'illusion of competence' that hinders strategic growth. If Sean cannot articulate the 'why' behind his fleet's architecture, he will fail to demonstrate the 'design system thinking' required for AI PM roles. The consequence is a potential mismatch between his perceived expertise and his actual theoretical depth.
