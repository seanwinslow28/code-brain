## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the financial efficiency of cost-capped workflows and the epistemic depth required for high-quality synthesis in Sean's job hunt and creative work. The mechanism at play is that deeper research requires more computational steps, which directly increases cost, creating a trade-off where budget constraints force premature termination of valuable inquiry. This leads to a consequence where Sean's automated systems may produce superficially complete but substantively shallow outputs, undermining the strategic value of his knowledge vault.

- Sean must define explicit quality profiles to ensure that cost savings do not come at the expense of strategic insight.
- The system needs an escalation protocol to justify additional spending when high-value opportunities are detected.
