## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the scalability of AI generation and the human capacity for curation. As tools like Freeplay make it easier to create datasets, they also risk overwhelming users with the volume of potential 'golden' examples. The mechanism here is that while automation reduces the cost of creation, it increases the cost of verification, creating a bottleneck where the quality of the system is limited by the speed at which humans can curate and label failures.
