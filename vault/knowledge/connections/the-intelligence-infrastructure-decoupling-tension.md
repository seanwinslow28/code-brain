## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the market's focus on raw model intelligence and the reality that infrastructure limitations are the true bottleneck for agent reliability. As models improve, their failures become more predictable and structural, revealing that the 'harness' is the critical invariant rather than the reasoning engine. This decoupling means that building better memory and state management systems offers a higher return on investment than simply upgrading model weights, as the latter only exposes deeper flaws in the former.
