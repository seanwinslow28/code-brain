## Synthesis

There is a critical tension between the goal of demonstrating strategic creative leadership and the reality of agentic tooling that forces deep technical engagement. When runtime-model coupling demands that creators debug SVG coordinates or timing functions, the 'abstraction tax' increases, pulling Sean out of his strategic lane and into implementation details. This not only reduces the velocity of portfolio creation but also risks misrepresenting his seniority level to potential employers who expect high-level architectural oversight rather than code-level correction.
