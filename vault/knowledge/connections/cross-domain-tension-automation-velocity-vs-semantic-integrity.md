## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the velocity of automated knowledge ingestion and the integrity of the resulting semantic graph. As Sean scales his agent fleet to process more data (increasing velocity), the risk of silent failures and low-quality synthesis increases, threatening the structural integrity of his personal knowledge base. This tension manifests as a trade-off where faster processing leads to more 'legibility debt' that must be manually corrected later, effectively negating the time savings gained from automation.
