## Synthesis

The pursuit of high-velocity automation through agent fleets often exacerbates semantic decay when underlying model capabilities are insufficient to maintain integrity at scale. This creates a systemic trust deficit where the user must invest more cognitive energy verifying outputs than they save by automating the task. The consequence is that scaling infrastructure without upgrading model judgment leads to diminishing returns, effectively inverting the intended efficiency gains into increased supervision costs.
