## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the declarative nature of prompting and the tacit nature of taste. While prompts allow for explicit instruction, they fail to capture the nuanced, often unconscious standards that define high-quality creative work. This gap necessitates a shift from 'prompt engineering' to 'calibration engineering,' where the primary activity becomes demonstrating preference through iterative feedback rather than describing intent through text.
