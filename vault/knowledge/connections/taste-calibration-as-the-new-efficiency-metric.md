## Synthesis

Sean's agent fleet is currently optimizing for throughput (concepts written, clusters sampled) while neglecting taste fidelity, creating a hidden cost where increased volume leads to decreased creative authority. The tension between these two metrics reveals that efficiency in agentic synthesis is not defined by speed or quantity, but by the ability to compress memory without altering Sean's decision outcomes. This implies that future runs must prioritize 'taste tournaments' and pairwise preference learning over raw concept generation to prevent the system from drifting into generic output.
