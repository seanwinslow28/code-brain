## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the collapsing cost of generation and the rising cost of validation in Sean's AI PM work. As agents become more capable, the primary risk shifts from inability to act to acting with high confidence but low fidelity. This creates a governance problem where traditional observability fails because it measures activity rather than truth, forcing Sean to build new supervisory mechanisms that focus on semantic correctness rather than operational uptime.

## Implications

- Sean must prioritize building semantic verification layers over operational monitoring for his agent fleets.
- The value of his AI PM role shifts from technical implementation to judgment-based auditing of automated outputs.
- Traditional success metrics like latency and throughput become misleading indicators of system health.
