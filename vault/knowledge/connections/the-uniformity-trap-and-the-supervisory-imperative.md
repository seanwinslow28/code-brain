## Synthesis

There is a direct tension between the market's saturation with uniform, AI-generated content and the creator's need to establish unique authority. As AI tools make it easier to produce 'polished' but 'intention-less' work, the cost of being average drops to zero, making differentiation increasingly difficult. This forces a strategic pivot where the value is no longer in the initial generation but in the rigorous supervision required to inject 'intention' and 'perspective' back into the workflow. The consequence is that creators must treat their taste as a proprietary asset, actively defending it against the flattening effect of automation.
