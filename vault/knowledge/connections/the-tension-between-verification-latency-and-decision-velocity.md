## Synthesis

There is a fundamental tension between the desire for high-confidence verification and the need for rapid decision-making in agentic workflows. When verification mechanisms are too rigid or slow, they create a bottleneck that prevents users from acting on potentially valuable but less-than-perfect evidence. This tension is resolved by treating verification as a gradient rather than a binary gate, allowing for 'good enough' signals to drive action while reserving deep verification for high-stakes decisions.
