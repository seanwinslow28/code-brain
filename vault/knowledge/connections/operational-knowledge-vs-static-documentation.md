## Synthesis

The tension between Knowledge-Centered Service (KCS) and traditional documentation lies in the source of truth: KCS treats knowledge as a dynamic byproduct of work, while traditional models treat it as a static artifact to be maintained. This tension manifests in AI knowledge systems where summarization often strips away the operational context that gives a document its meaning. The consequence is that AI systems may produce accurate summaries that are useless to stakeholders because they ignore the 'boundary object' nature of the original documents. Sean can leverage this insight to propose agent specs that prioritize operational relevance over static accuracy, aligning his AI-PM narrative with the reality of how knowledge actually flows in organizations.
