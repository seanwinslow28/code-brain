## Synthesis

Sean’s current observability relies on detecting failures after they occur through telemetry, which creates a lag between the error and the awareness of it. By integrating pre-mortem simulations, he shifts from reactive monitoring to proactive risk identification, allowing him to address structural weaknesses before they impact his job hunt or portfolio. This tension highlights the difference between knowing something is broken and understanding why it might break.

## Implications

- Sean must implement pre-mortem checklists for all high-stakes job hunt artifacts to prevent silent failures.
- Telemetry dashboards alone are insufficient for ensuring the integrity of his professional portfolio outputs.
