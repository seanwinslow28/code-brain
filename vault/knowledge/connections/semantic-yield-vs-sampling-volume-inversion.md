## Synthesis

There is a non-linear relationship between the volume of clusters sampled and the semantic yield of concepts written, where increased sampling does not guarantee higher quality insights. The tension arises because optimizing for operational visibility (sampling more) can lead to a degradation in taste fidelity if the agent's evaluation function is not calibrated to reject low-signal content. This inversion suggests that Sean should prioritize rejection rates and connection novelty over raw throughput metrics to maintain strategic value.
