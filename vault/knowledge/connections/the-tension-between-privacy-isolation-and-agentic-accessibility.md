## Synthesis

Sean faces a structural tension where the very data that gives his Substack series its unique value (personal, sensitive narratives) is also the data that must be excluded from his public, agent-accessible knowledge graph. The mechanism of 'Privacy-Aware Data Routing' resolves this by creating a sanitized proxy layer, but this introduces a dependency risk: if the scrubbing process fails or drifts, the public agent infrastructure could inadvertently propagate private information. This tension forces Sean to maintain two parallel states (private originals vs. public copies) and rely on strict gitignore rules rather than semantic understanding by the agents themselves, highlighting a limit in current agentic privacy capabilities.
