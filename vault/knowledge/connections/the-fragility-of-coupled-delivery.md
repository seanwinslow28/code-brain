## Synthesis

The tension between the desire for consistent audience engagement and the reality of complex tool development creates a structural fragility in Sean's publishing model. By coupling content release to tool readiness, he ensures high signal-to-noise ratio but sacrifices schedule predictability, forcing reactive rescheduling when engineering slips occur. This pattern reveals that his creative output is not just a function of writing capacity but of infrastructure reliability, making the build phase the true bottleneck of his distribution strategy.
