## Synthesis

This connection reveals a fundamental tension where the drive for automated throughput directly conflicts with the preservation of Sean's tacit knowledge, leading to a systemic trust deficit. As Sean scales his agent fleet, the erosion of uncodified expertise creates a supervision failure mode where agents execute tasks correctly but miss strategic intent. The consequence is a bottleneck where the speed of automation is capped by the slowness of codifying human judgment into machine-readable rules.
