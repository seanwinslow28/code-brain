## Synthesis

As AI lowers the barrier to content creation, the scarcity shifts to authentic human connection. This creates a tension where platforms seek scale through automation, while creators demand preservation of identity through strict supervision. The consequence is a market bifurcation: soulless, scalable content versus high-trust, human-curated experiences. Sean must position his work as enabling the latter, not the former.
