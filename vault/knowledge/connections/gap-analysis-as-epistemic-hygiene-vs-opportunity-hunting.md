## Synthesis

The tension lies in treating gap maps as either a hunting ground for opportunities or a hygiene check for knowledge deficits. When Sean's agents treat gaps as opportunities, they fall into the 'solutioneering' trap, building features for non-existent needs. By reframing gaps as epistemic deficits requiring investigation, the system shifts from creative output to rigorous inquiry, preventing the accumulation of 'slop' that looks like insight but lacks validity. This distinction is critical for maintaining the integrity of his competitive analysis and product discovery workflows.

## Implications

- Sean must implement explicit 'evidence status' labels in his gap maps to prevent agents from conflating silence with opportunity
- The synthesis pipeline should prioritize investigation prompts over solution generation when gaps are identified
