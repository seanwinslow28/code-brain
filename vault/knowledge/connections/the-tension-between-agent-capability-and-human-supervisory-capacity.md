## Synthesis

There is a fundamental mismatch between the increasing capability of AI agents to perform long-horizon tasks and the static capacity of human supervisors to manage them. As agents become more 'multiplayer' and integrated into workflows, they require more nuanced supervision rather than less, because their outputs lack intrinsic taste or accountability. This creates a 'Supervision Fatigue' risk where the user must reinvent their company's workflow from scratch to accommodate this new dynamic, shifting the bottleneck from technology to human adoption.
