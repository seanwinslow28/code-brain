## Synthesis

This tension reveals a systemic trust deficit where Sean's agent fleet appears healthy through operational metrics like uptime and throughput, while semantic value decays silently due to lack of human supervision. The consequence is that Sean cannot scale his knowledge infrastructure effectively because the cost of verifying novelty exceeds the marginal gain of additional output. This creates a 'Silent Decay' in strategic pipelines where high activity masks stagnation, leading to a false sense of progress that undermines his ability to make informed decisions in his job hunt and creative work.
