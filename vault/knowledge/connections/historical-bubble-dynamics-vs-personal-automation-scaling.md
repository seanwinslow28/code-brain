## Synthesis

There is a structural parallel between Carlota Perez's 'Installation' phase of technological revolutions and Sean's current automation scaling. Just as the bubble finances infrastructure that enables future growth, Sean's current high-volume, low-quality runs are financing the semantic infrastructure of his vault. However, the tension lies in the 'crash' or 'recomposition' phase: if the volume becomes too high without corresponding quality, the system risks a collapse in trust and utility, requiring a period of recomposition (manual curation) before sustainable growth can resume.

## Implications

- Sean should consider reducing the volume of his fleet's output to maintain quality and avoid supervision fatigue, effectively inducing a 'controlled crash' to force recomposition.
- The current high-volume runs are an 'Installation' phase investment; the value will only be realized in a future 'Deployment' phase after a period of manual curation.
