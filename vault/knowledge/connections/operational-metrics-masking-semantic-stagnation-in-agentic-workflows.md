## Synthesis

Sean's agentic infrastructure creates a dangerous feedback loop where operational health metrics mask semantic stagnation. Agents report success based on process execution, while knowledge integrity depends on successful synthesis of novel insights. This decoupling allows Sean to invest time in monitoring systems that are technically 'healthy' but strategically inert, leading to a gradual erosion of competitive advantage without any immediate error signals to trigger correction.

## Implications

- Sean must redefine success metrics for agents to include output quality and novelty, not just execution status.
- The fleet monitoring system should flag 'empty success' states as warnings to prevent complacency in strategic oversight.
- Interventions should be triggered by semantic stagnation rather than operational errors to catch decay early.
