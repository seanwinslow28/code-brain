## Synthesis

The tension between operational readiness and wardley mapping reveals a critical trade-off in how Sean presents his infrastructure. Operational readiness demands deep visibility into failure modes and recovery paths, which can overwhelm non-technical interviewers if not carefully curated. Wardley mapping provides the mechanism to hide this complexity behind commodity abstractions for certain audiences while exposing it for others. The consequence is that Sean must dynamically adjust the legibility of his systems based on the strategic value of the artifact in a given context.
