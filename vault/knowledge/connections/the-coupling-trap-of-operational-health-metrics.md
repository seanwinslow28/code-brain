## Synthesis

Sean faces a critical inversion where the governance of his professional output is delegated to systems that only verify operational health, not semantic truth. This creates a tension between the engineering desire for simplified, aggregate health metrics and the operational reality of complex human-agent joint cognition. When Sean prioritizes binary success rates over adaptive capacity, he masks the growing coupling fragility between agents, leading to a state where the system is technically 'alive' but functionally inert.
