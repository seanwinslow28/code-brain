## Synthesis

The tension lies between proving activity (building agents) and proving taste (resolving misfits). Current portfolio artifacts often fail to explain why one design is better than another, leaving a gap in demonstrating strategic judgment. By anchoring projects on Christopher Alexander’s synthesis of form, Sean can shift from showing tool exposure to showing the resolution of concrete forces like cost ceilings or stale context. This transforms the portfolio from a gallery of outputs into an evaluative argument for his design quality.
