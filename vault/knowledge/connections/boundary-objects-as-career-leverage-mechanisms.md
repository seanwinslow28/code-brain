## Synthesis

Boundary objects serve as the physical manifestation of Wardley mapping in Sean's career strategy. By treating his artifacts as boundary objects, he can translate complex engineering work into value propositions for different stakeholders. This mechanism allows him to maintain technical depth while presenting a simplified narrative to non-technical audiences. The consequence is that his portfolio becomes a dynamic set of translations rather than a static list of projects, increasing its adaptability across different job contexts.
