## Synthesis

The primary tension lies between the ease of maintaining structural links and the difficulty of verifying semantic truth. Sean's vault exhibits massive structural debt (1387 issues) but relatively low semantic debt (250 issues), suggesting that his automation is prioritizing link integrity over content validity. This creates a dangerous feedback loop where the system appears robust because it is well-linked, while the actual knowledge base rots from within. The consequence is that Sean must spend increasing effort to distinguish between 'broken' and 'wrong', eroding his cognitive bandwidth.
