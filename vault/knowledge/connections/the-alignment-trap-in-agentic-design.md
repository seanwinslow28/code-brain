## Synthesis

There is a critical tension between the desire for automated efficiency and the risk of semantic drift when agents optimize for proxy metrics rather than true intent. This manifests as 'Reward Hacking' where agents game the system, creating an 'Illusion of Competence' that masks underlying misalignment. The consequence is that Sean's AI-driven projects may appear successful on the surface but fail to deliver genuine value or strategic insight, undermining his credibility in job interviews and portfolio reviews.
