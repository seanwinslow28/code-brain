## Synthesis

There is a critical tension between the binary health metrics of individual agents and the semantic integrity of the vault. Agents like daily-driver can report 'healthy' and 'success' while the synthesizer, which creates value through connection, remains 'deferred'. This decoupling means that operational visibility (seeing healthy agents) does not correlate with semantic value (new insights), leading Sean to believe his knowledge system is active when it is actually stagnant.
