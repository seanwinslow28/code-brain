## Synthesis

This pattern highlights the conflict between data integrity (snapshots) and operational speed (continuous tracking). Braintrust's Pro/Enterprise-only snapshot feature ensures reproducibility but introduces a 'paywall' for rigor, while the free tier offers only change tracking, which is insufficient for 'Epistemic Artifacts' in high-stakes decision-making. Sean must weigh the cost of snapshots against the risk of 'Legibility Debt' when debugging complex agentic chains.
