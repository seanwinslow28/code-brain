## Synthesis

Sean's operational success with his agent fleet is built on instinctive fixes that create a robust but fragile system. This instinctive approach masks the lack of explicit structure needed for scaling and transfer, creating a tension between immediate utility and long-term resilience. As he scales his automation, he risks eroding the tacit knowledge required to supervise effectively, ultimately limiting his value as an AI PM who must define quality and safety criteria.

## Implications

- Sean must explicitly map his instinctive decisions to canonical primitives to avoid misdiagnosing failures in novel scenarios.
- His curriculum for AI PM skills should focus on decision surfaces he hasn't encountered rather than reinforcing existing intuition.
- The value of his fleet memory index lies not just in its data, but in its ability to force explicit articulation of operational logic.
