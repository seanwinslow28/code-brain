## What this is

Critiques from two external reasoners (gpt-5.5 via Codex CLI, Gemini 3 via Anti-Gravity CLI) of [[confidence-rituals]]. The synthesizer describes what the concept is; this expansion proposes what's missing.

## From Codex (gpt-5.5)

1. **Add “performance under observation” as a separate skill from generic confidence.**  
   Anchor it on Erving Goffman’s *The Presentation of Self in Everyday Life*, especially the distinction between front-stage performance and backstage preparation.

   Current concept treats confidence as an internal state to regulate. Missing move: interviews are staged social performances where the candidate must manage the *definition of the situation*. Add a pattern like: “When I’m thinking aloud, I’m not exposing uncertainty; I’m showing how I stabilize ambiguity.”

   This unlocks an **interview-performance runbook** for vibe-coding loops: opening frame, uncertainty narration, recovery lines, and “how I want you to read my silence” scripts. Without this, Sean risks sounding like he is merely coping with nerves instead of deliberately shaping the collaborative room.

2. **Add “self-efficacy calibration,” not just confidence projection.**  
   Anchor it on Albert Bandura’s paper “Self-Efficacy: Toward a Unifying Theory of Behavioral Change” and his book *Self-Efficacy: The Exercise of Control*.

   The missing facet is that confidence should be evidence-indexed. Bandura separates vague optimism from task-specific belief built through mastery experiences, modeling, verbal persuasion, and physiological regulation. Sean’s concept should distinguish “I feel confident” from “I have proof I can perform this task under these constraints.”

   This unlocks a **portfolio one-pager / interview prep artifact**: a table mapping target role competencies to concrete mastery evidence from Code-Brain, 16BitFit, intent-engineering, and llm-council. It would let Sean decide which stories deserve airtime, instead of rehearsing generic prompts or relying on mood management.

3. **Add “anti-fragile disclosure” for moments of not knowing.**  
   Anchor it on Amy Edmondson’s *The Fearless Organization*, plus her HBR article “Strategies for Learning from Failure.”

   Vibe-coding interviews reward transparency, but raw transparency can read as flailing. The missing technique is structured disclosure: name uncertainty, bound it, choose a next probe, and explain what evidence would change your mind. Sentence pattern: “I don’t know that yet; the smallest useful test is X, and if it returns Y I’ll do Z.”

   This unlocks an **agentic-engineering interview demo script** where Sean turns uncertainty into operational judgment: debugging live, choosing between agent approaches, setting stop rules, and narrating tradeoffs. The current concept can produce calming rituals; this addition produces a credible IC signal: “I can be observed while confused and still drive the system forward.”
