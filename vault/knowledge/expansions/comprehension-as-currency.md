## What this is

Critiques from two external reasoners (gpt-5.5 via Codex CLI, Gemini 3 via Anti-Gravity CLI) of [[comprehension-as-currency]]. The synthesizer describes what the concept is; this expansion proposes what's missing.

## From Codex (gpt-5.5)

1. **Add “legibility under adversarial reading” mode, anchored on Harry Collins’ _Tacit and Explicit Knowledge_.**  
   Current concept treats comprehension as something a recruiter can perceive from polished failure/resolution narrative. Missing facet: some knowledge is only convincing when it shows what *cannot* be fully written down, and how the writer knows where the boundary is.

   Sentence pattern: “The part I can document is X; the part that only became visible through practice was Y; the test that proved I had crossed from description into competence was Z.”

   This unlocks a stronger **portfolio EXPLANATION.md genre**: not “I understand my system,” but “I know which parts of this system resist documentation, and I designed probes anyway.” That is more credible for agentic-engineering IC roles, where tacit operational judgment matters more than clean diagrams.

2. **Add “decision-forcing case” structure, anchored on Harvard Business School case method, specifically C. Roland Christensen’s _Teaching and the Case Method_.**  
   Current concept frames docs as proof of thought after the fact. Missing: forcing the reader into the decision point *before* revealing the resolution. Recruiters and hiring managers should not just admire the explanation; they should feel the decision pressure.

   Sentence pattern: “At this point, I had three bad options: A would break X, B would preserve Y but cost Z, and C looked clean but hid failure mode W. I chose B because…”

   This unlocks **case-study artifacts** Sean currently cannot fully reach: interview-ready “engineering judgment briefs” where the artifact demonstrates prioritization under constraint, not just reflective fluency. Especially useful for AI-PM roles where the evaluation question is “Would I trust this person with ambiguous tradeoffs?”

3. **Add “epistemic status / confidence ledger,” anchored on Julia Galef’s _The Scout Mindset_ plus Gwern Branwen’s essay style in “The Melatonin FAQ.”**  
   Current concept rewards confident narrative. Missing contradiction: high-status technical writing often earns trust by exposing uncertainty, update history, failed hypotheses, and evidence quality. Comprehension is not only clarity; it is calibrated belief.

   Sentence pattern: “Confidence: medium. Evidence: two successful runs, one failed edge case, no adversarial eval yet. My current belief is X; the observation that would change my mind is Y.”

   This unlocks **agent runbooks, eval notes, and portfolio one-pagers** that read less like retrospective storytelling and more like operational command. It would help Sean avoid the generic failure mode of “smart explanation after success” and instead show the hiring signal of someone who can govern an unreliable agent system before it bites.
