## What this is

Critiques from two external reasoners (gpt-5.5 via Codex CLI, Gemini 3 via Anti-Gravity CLI) of [[interpersonal-dynamics]]. The synthesizer describes what the concept is; this expansion proposes what's missing.

## From Codex (gpt-5.5)

## 1. Add “Interaction Order Under Stress”

**What to add:** A micro-behavior layer: how candidates preserve face, repair ambiguity, and make competence legible while under evaluative pressure.

**Anchor:** Erving Goffman, *Interaction Ritual: Essays on Face-to-Face Behavior*.

**Sentence pattern:** “When I hit uncertainty, I will narrate the uncertainty without self-abasing: `I’m not certain yet whether the failure is in X or Y, so I’m going to isolate them with one cheap test.`”

**Unlocks:** An interview-performance runbook for vibe-coding screens. The current concept says “be transparent and humble”; Goffman lets Sean specify *observable repair moves*: acknowledge, bracket, test, re-enter. This would help him produce a “coding interview interaction protocol” instead of another soft-skills summary.

## 2. Add “Epistemic Status as Collaboration Primitive”

**What to add:** A named mode for separating confidence, evidence, and next action in live technical conversation.

**Anchor:** Julia Galef, *The Scout Mindset*.

**Sentence pattern:** “My current confidence is about 60%; the evidence is A and B; the fastest way to update is C.”

**Unlocks:** A reusable agentic-engineering interview artifact: “Epistemic Status Cards for Live Coding.” Sean’s current concept treats help-seeking as interpersonal virtue. This turns it into a decision interface: when to ask, when to proceed, when to state uncertainty, when to invite correction. It also maps directly to PM interviews where he needs to show judgment without overclaiming.

## 3. Add “Conversational Repair, Not Politeness”

**What to add:** A contradiction to the humility/transparency framing: the goal is not to appear agreeable; the goal is to keep the joint task moving after breakdowns.

**Anchor:** Charles Goodwin, “Conversational Frameworks for the Accomplishment of Meaning in Aphasia” and Emanuel Schegloff, Gail Jefferson, Harvey Sacks, “The Preference for Self-Correction in the Organization of Repair in Conversation.”

**Sentence pattern:** “I think we may be talking past each other on `X`; I’m using it to mean `Y`. Is that the definition you want for this exercise?”

**Unlocks:** A stronger Substack essay or portfolio one-pager: “The Interview Is a Debugging Session With Two Humans in the Loop.” The current concept risks generic “be receptive to feedback” advice. Repair theory gives Sean a sharper frame: interpersonal dynamics are the protocol layer for recovering shared state. That maps cleanly to agent fleets, MCP contracts, and human-in-the-loop orchestration.
