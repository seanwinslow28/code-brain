## What this is

Critiques from two external reasoners (gpt-5.5 via Codex CLI, Gemini 3 via Anti-Gravity CLI) of [[hiring-manager-appeal]]. The synthesizer describes what the concept is; this expansion proposes what's missing.

## From Codex (gpt-5.5)

1. **Add “costly-signal portfolio mode.”**  
   Anchor it on Michael Spence’s paper [“Job Market Signaling”](https://academic.oup.com/qje/article/87/3/355/1909092), not generic “personal branding.” The missing idea: hiring managers are not just seeking clarity; they are resolving asymmetric information. Sentence pattern: “This artifact was expensive for a weak candidate to fake because it required X, Y, and Z.”  
   Unlock: a portfolio one-pager that frames Code-Brain, 16BitFit, and intent-engineering as costly signals: shipped systems, operational constraints, eval loops, failure records. Current concept says “make it understandable”; this adds “make it hard to dismiss.”

2. **Add “positioning-before-explanation mode.”**  
   Anchor it on April Dunford’s [*Obviously Awesome*](https://www.aprildunford.com/books). The missing technique is competitive positioning: alternative, differentiated capability, value, best-fit audience. Sentence pattern: “For hiring managers choosing between PMs who prompt tools and engineers who ship infrastructure, Sean is the PM-turned-agentic-IC who can specify, build, operate, and critique agent systems end to end.”  
   Unlock: a job-hunt landing page / resume header / recruiter blurb where the category is explicit. Current concept risks “technically sound but clear”; Dunford mode forces “clear compared to what, for whom, and why now?”

3. **Add “smart-and-gets-things-done evidence grid.”**  
   Anchor it on Joel Spolsky’s [“The Guerrilla Guide to Interviewing”](https://www.cse.unr.edu/~sushil/researchAdvice/GIV1.html). The missing framework is brutally simple: hiring managers look for demonstrated judgment plus shipping power. Sentence pattern: “Problem: X. Constraint: Y. Decision: Z. Result: shipped artifact.”  
   Unlock: a hiring-manager-facing proof matrix: Code-Brain equals autonomous ops; 16BitFit equals creative technical execution; intent-engineering equals methodology formation; llm-council equals decision-quality tooling. Current concept names appeal, but not the evidence structure that converts “interesting projects” into “this person can be trusted with ambiguous work.”
