## What this is

Critiques from two external reasoners (gpt-5.5 via Codex CLI, Gemini 3 via Anti-Gravity CLI) of [[vibe-coding-playbook]]. The synthesizer describes what the concept is; this expansion proposes what's missing.

## From Codex (gpt-5.5)

1. **Add “Situated Repair Mode”**
   - **What to add:** Treat the playbook as a *resource for improvisation*, not an execution plan. Add a repair ladder: “pause → name the mismatch → restate the goal → offer a smaller next move.”
   - **Anchor:** Lucy Suchman, *Plans and Situated Actions: The Problem of Human-Machine Communication*; plus Schegloff, Jefferson, and Sacks, “The Preference for Self-Correction in the Organization of Repair in Conversation” (1977). Sources: [Google Books](https://books.google.com/books/about/Plans_and_Situated_Actions.html?id=AJ_eBJtHxmsC), [Cambridge PDF](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/5549B861FDE7180B75FA5C382821875E/S009785077702150Xa.pdf/preference_for_selfcorrection_in_the_organization_of_repair_in_conversation.pdf)
   - **Unlocks:** An **interview repair runbook** for moments where the interviewer rejects the frame, changes constraints, or says “that’s not what I meant.” Current concept helps Sean remember prepared tactics; this adds a way to recover live without sounding scripted.

2. **Add “Premortem Interview Failure Modes”**
   - **What to add:** Before each company-specific playbook, add a one-page failure premortem: “It is 30 minutes after the interview and I know I lost the loop because…” Then list 5 failure modes, each with a counter-move.
   - **Anchor:** Gary Klein, “Performing a Project Premortem,” *Harvard Business Review*, September 2007; also Klein’s *Sources of Power: How People Make Decisions* for recognition-primed decisions. Source: [HBR](https://hbr.org/2007/09/performing-a-project-premortem)
   - **Unlocks:** A **decision-prep artifact**, not just a memory aid. For Sean, this could produce interview-specific “anti-pattern cards”: “I over-indexed on agent plumbing,” “I sounded like a PM narrating IC work,” “I failed to translate Code-Brain into employer value.” The current concept optimizes recall; premortem mode optimizes risk control.

3. **Add “Frame Control / Footing Shifts”**
   - **What to add:** A named section for switching roles mid-answer: builder, PM, systems designer, operator, critic. Sentence pattern: “Wearing the builder hat…” / “If I switch to the product lens…” / “The operator concern is…”
   - **Anchor:** Erving Goffman, *Frame Analysis: An Essay on the Organization of Experience* and *The Presentation of Self in Everyday Life*. Source: [Internet Archive entry](https://archive.org/details/frameanalysisess0000goff)
   - **Unlocks:** A **portfolio interview performance spec** where Sean can deliberately demonstrate range instead of hoping the interviewer infers it. Current concept says “tailor to format”; frame-control mode lets him show AI-PM, senior PM, and agentic-engineering IC competence inside one answer without blending into generic hybrid-candidate mush.
