## What this is

Critiques from two external reasoners (gpt-5.5 via Codex CLI, Gemini 3 via Anti-Gravity CLI) of [[agentic-system-integration-for-job-hunt]]. The synthesizer describes what the concept is; this expansion proposes what's missing.

## From Codex (gpt-5.5)

1. **Add “strategy-kernel mode,” anchored on Richard Rumelt’s _Good Strategy/Bad Strategy_, specifically “The Kernel of Good Strategy.”** Sentence pattern: **diagnosis → guiding policy → coherent actions**. Rewrite “the roadmap, dashboard, and portfolio form an ecosystem” as: “The job hunt is constrained by insufficient externally legible IC evidence; therefore prioritize demonstrations of agent reliability; consequently pause artifacts that do not strengthen that signal.” This contradicts the article’s assumption that integration itself constitutes strategy. It unlocks a **one-page job-hunt strategy**, role-selection criteria, and defensible explanations of why Code-Brain, Battle Mode, or intent-engineering deserves the next week of work.

2. **Add an “XYZ hypothesis ledger,” anchored on Alberto Savoia’s _The Right It: Why So Many Ideas Fail and How to Make Sure Yours Succeed_.** Pattern: **“At least X% of Y will Z.”** Example: “At least 20% of AI-platform hiring managers who view the fleet incident replay will request a technical conversation.” Every flagship project should state its target audience, expected behavior, cheapest test, evidence collected, and pivot threshold. This introduces the missing external feedback loop: the current concept connects internal systems but never tests whether employers interpret their output as intended. It unlocks **portfolio experiment cards, recruiter-interview scripts, instrumented landing pages, and evidence-based ship/kill decisions** instead of another internally coherent roadmap.

3. **Add “constraint-first portfolio management,” anchored on Eliyahu Goldratt’s _The Goal_ and its Five Focusing Steps.** Sentence pattern: **identify the constraint → exploit it → subordinate everything else → elevate it → repeat**. Treat interview throughput—not artifact production—as the system objective. If the constraint is recruiter discovery, another agent demo is inventory; if it is technical credibility after screening, the governance demo or intent-engineering executable becomes the constraint-breaking work. This directly challenges the implicit belief that more integrated infrastructure necessarily advances the hunt. It unlocks a **weekly constraint memo, explicit WIP limits, a stop-doing list, and a throughput dashboard** measuring applications → screens → technical interviews → offers rather than fleet activity and portfolio output.
