## What this is

Critiques from two external reasoners (gpt-5.5 via Codex CLI, Gemini 3 via Anti-Gravity CLI) of [[knowledge-infrastructure-and-strategic-alignment]]. The synthesizer describes what the concept is; this expansion proposes what's missing.

## From Codex (gpt-5.5)

1. **Add “knowledge as boundary object” governance**

   Anchor it on Susan Leigh Star and James R. Griesemer’s paper **“Institutional Ecology, ‘Translations’ and Boundary Objects”** (1989).

   The current concept treats knowledge infrastructure as an efficiency layer: better docs → better alignment. Missing: shared knowledge systems work because different groups can use the *same artifact differently* without requiring full consensus.

   Add a mode like: **“Boundary-object test: which teams can act from this artifact without agreeing on the same interpretation?”**

   This unlocks a stronger artifact genre: a **knowledge-system design review** or **Confluence overhaul RFC** that evaluates docs by cross-functional usability, not completeness. For Sean’s portfolio, this turns “I improved documentation” into “I designed translation infrastructure between product, revenue, engineering, and agents.”

2. **Add “single source of truth is a trap”**

   Anchor it on David Weinberger’s **_Too Big to Know_** and his essay/book argument that networked knowledge is not centralized certainty but linked, provisional, and contested.

   The current concept leans toward unified knowledge base = strategic clarity. Missing: large organizations often fail when they over-centralize knowledge into one canonical surface. The better model is not one truth repository, but **traceable pluralism**: multiple partial views with provenance, freshness, ownership, and contradiction handling.

   Add a named counter-frame: **“Federated truth over canonical truth.”**

   Sentence pattern: **“Do not ask whether the system has the answer; ask whether it exposes who believes what, why, since when, and what would change their mind.”**

   This unlocks a sharper **agent fleet spec**: agents should not just retrieve “the answer,” but return confidence, source lineage, owner, timestamp, and disagreement state. That gives Sean a concrete bridge from Obsidian/Confluence theory into agentic-engineering infrastructure.

3. **Add “doctrine, not documentation”**

   Anchor it on John Boyd’s **“Organic Design for Command and Control”** and the military concept of **commander’s intent** as used in mission command.

   The current concept says knowledge systems should align operations and strategy. Missing: alignment does not come from more information; it comes from doctrine that lets actors make good decisions when information is incomplete, late, or contradictory.

   Add a doctrine layer: **“Strategic alignment artifacts must specify intent, constraints, tempo, and acceptable local improvisation.”**

   Sentence pattern: **“When the map is stale, what principle should the operator preserve?”**

   This unlocks Sean’s strongest missing artifact: an **intent-engineering runbook** for autonomous agents and product teams. Instead of “Confluence supports AI PM work,” he can ship a one-pager showing how product strategy becomes executable doctrine: objective, tradeoff hierarchy, stop rules, escalation triggers, and local-decision rights.
