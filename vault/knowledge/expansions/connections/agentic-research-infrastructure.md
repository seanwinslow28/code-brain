## What this is

Critiques from two external reasoners (gpt-5.5 via Codex CLI, Gemini 3 via Anti-Gravity CLI) of [[agentic-research-infrastructure]]. The synthesizer describes what the concept is; this expansion proposes what's missing.

## From Codex (gpt-5.5)

1. **Add “Research Debt Accounting” anchored on Tom DeMarco & Timothy Lister, _Waltzing with Bears: Managing Risk on Software Projects_**

   The concept currently routes questions by execution tier, but it does not account for *research debt*: unanswered questions whose uncertainty is actively distorting decisions.

   Add a mode like:

   > “Every research item must declare the decision it de-risks, the cost of being wrong, the cheapest disconfirming evidence, and its expiry date.”

   This gives Sean a stronger artifact than a queue: a **research-risk ledger**. It would let him ship a runbook or portfolio demo showing that his fleet does not merely “do research,” it manages uncertainty as an operational liability. That is a much sharper AI-PM signal than “I have LDR and Gemini DR wired up.”

2. **Add “Adversarial Evidence Triage” anchored on Julia Galef, _The Scout Mindset_**

   The current concept optimizes for finding and synthesizing information, but not for protecting Sean from confirmation-seeking research agents. “Critic” exists elsewhere in the fleet, but this article does not encode an adversarial evidence standard inside the research infrastructure itself.

   Add a named lane:

   > “Scout lane: before synthesis, list the strongest evidence that would make this research question irrelevant, badly framed, or already answered.”

   This unlocks a **decision pre-mortem artifact**: a one-page “what would change my mind?” brief for job-hunt strategy, portfolio positioning, technical architecture, or Substack claims. Without this, the infrastructure risks sounding like a throughput machine for plausible summaries.

3. **Add “Provenance-First Knowledge Production” anchored on Peter Suber, _Open Access_ + Simon Willison’s essay “Things we learned about LLMs in 2024”**

   Sean has citation-quality concerns in the supporting context, but this concept does not make provenance a first-class output. It says LDR/Gemini produce research; it does not define the artifact contract that separates grounded knowledge from model-shaped confidence.

   Add a rule:

   > “Every research output must separate source claims, model inferences, rejected sources, and unresolved uncertainties before it is allowed to become vault knowledge.”

   This unlocks an **evidence packet** genre: a durable research artifact with claim tables, source grades, contradiction notes, and “safe to reuse?” status. That would let Sean publish a credible Substack piece or portfolio one-pager on agentic research infrastructure where the differentiator is not “multi-agent research,” but **audit-ready knowledge formation**.
