## What this is

Critiques from two external reasoners (gpt-5.5 via Codex CLI, Gemini 3 via Anti-Gravity CLI) of [[ai-product-management-ecosystem]]. The synthesizer describes what the concept is; this expansion proposes what's missing.

## From Codex (gpt-5.5)

1. **Add “eval-led AI PM” as the missing operating mode.**  
   Anchor it on Hamel Husain’s essay “[Your AI Product Needs Evals](https://hamel.dev/blog/posts/evals/)” and Chip Huyen’s *AI Engineering*. The sentence pattern: “An AI PM owns not just the roadmap, but the failure taxonomy, eval set, acceptance threshold, and release gate.”  
   This unlocks a portfolio artifact Sean does not currently get from this concept: an **AI PM eval case study** for Code-Brain, showing before/after examples where the fleet stopped producing summaries and began producing generative critique.

2. **Add “Software 2.0 product strategy,” not just “AI roles are hot.”**  
   Anchor it on Andrej Karpathy’s “[Software 2.0](https://karpathy.medium.com/software-2-0-a64152b37c35)” and Matt Welsh’s CACM essay “[The End of Programming](https://cacm.acm.org/opinion/the-end-of-programming/)”. The missing distinction: AI PM jobs split into three kinds: model-as-feature, model-as-platform, and model-as-labor-force. Sean’s edge is the third.  
   This unlocks a **role-screening matrix**: “Is this company asking for an AI feature PM, an ML platform PM, or an agent-fleet operator?” Current concept treats Perplexity and Robinhood as equivalent signals; this would let Sean reject generic AI-PM postings faster.

3. **Add “positioning against the generic AI PM blob.”**  
   Anchor it on April Dunford’s *[Obviously Awesome](https://www.aprildunford.com/books/obviously-awesome)*. The contradiction to add: “AI Product Manager” is too broad to be a useful category; Sean needs a sharper market position, like “agentic systems PM who can also ship the harness.”  
   This unlocks a **portfolio one-pager or Substack essay** with the claim: “The next PM role is not prompt fluency; it is operating-model design for semi-autonomous software teams.” Without this, the concept leaves Sean sounding like a PM who knows AI. With it, he sounds like someone who has already built the category he wants to be hired into.
