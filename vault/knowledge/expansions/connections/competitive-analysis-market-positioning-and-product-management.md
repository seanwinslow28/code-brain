## What this is

Critiques from two external reasoners (gpt-5.5 via Codex CLI, Gemini 3 via Anti-Gravity CLI) of [[competitive-analysis-market-positioning-and-product-management]]. The synthesizer describes what the concept is; this expansion proposes what's missing.

## From Codex (gpt-5.5)

1. **Add “competitive alternatives first” positioning**
   - **What to add:** A positioning pass that starts with the customer’s real alternative, not the competitor list. Sentence pattern: “For buyers who currently use `X workaround`, Code-Brain is a `Y category` that gives them `Z outcome`, unlike `A/B/C`.”
   - **Exemplar:** April Dunford, [*Obviously Awesome*](https://www.aprildunford.com/books).
   - **Unlocks:** A portfolio one-pager and role-specific interview pitch where Sean stops saying “agent fleet / second brain” and instead names the buyer’s current bad substitute: scattered prompts, fragile cron jobs, generic Obsidian summaries, or SaaS automation glue.

2. **Add Wardley Mapping for strategic terrain**
   - **What to add:** A “value-chain + evolution” map for Sean’s personal moat: user need at top, capabilities below, each plotted from genesis → custom → product → commodity.
   - **Exemplar:** Simon Wardley, [*Wardley Maps*](https://www.swardleymaps.com/).
   - **Unlocks:** A decision artifact the current concept cannot produce: “Where should Sean invest next?” Example: local launchd orchestration may already be commodity plumbing; intent-engineering specs may be left-edge differentiator; recruiter-facing demos may be the visible user-need layer. This turns competitive analysis from vibes into resource allocation.

3. **Add “Power” as a separate test from positioning**
   - **What to add:** A 7 Powers screen: which advantage, if any, becomes stronger because Sean built it? Scale economies, network economies, counter-positioning, switching costs, branding, cornered resource, or process power.
   - **Exemplar:** Hamilton Helmer, [*7 Powers: The Foundations of Business Strategy*](https://7powers.com/).
   - **Unlocks:** A defensibility memo for AI-PM / agentic-engineering roles. The current concept can say “differentiate”; Helmer forces: “What persists after everyone has Claude Code, MCP, and local models?” Likely answer: process power from hundreds of lived agent failures plus a public artifact trail.
