## What this is

Critiques from two external reasoners (gpt-5.5 via Codex CLI, Gemini 3 via Anti-Gravity CLI) of [[agentic-engineering-and-professional-roles]]. The synthesizer describes what the concept is; this expansion proposes what's missing.

## From Codex (gpt-5.5)

1. **Add “joint cognitive system” mode, not “agent health” mode.**  
   Anchor it on David D. Woods & Erik Hollnagel, *Joint Cognitive Systems: Foundations of Cognitive Systems Engineering*.

   The current concept treats agent fleets as automation routines that need monitoring. That is too weak. Add the frame that Sean is designing **human-agent joint work**, where the unit of analysis is not “the agent” or “the PM,” but the coupled system under pressure.

   Sentence pattern to add: “The product manager’s job shifts from specifying outputs to shaping the coordination surface between human judgment, machine initiative, exception handling, and recovery.”

   This unlocks a stronger **portfolio one-pager / interview narrative**: “I build joint cognitive systems for product work,” rather than “I automate PM workflows.” It also gives him a better artifact genre: a **coordination runbook** showing handoffs, authority boundaries, escalation points, and failure recovery across his fleet.

2. **Add “accountability scaffold” mode from algorithmic management.**  
   Anchor it on Karen Levy, *Data Driven: Truckers, Technology, and the New Workplace Surveillance*.

   Sean’s article currently assumes agentic engineering integrates smoothly into professional roles if agent health and workflow alignment are designed well. Missing contradiction: automation often changes who is watched, blamed, interrupted, and forced to compensate for system brittleness.

   Add a section that asks: when an AI-PM fleet acts, **who absorbs the residual responsibility**? The PM? The agent designer? The manager? The customer-facing team? The current concept has “health” but not “liability, surveillance, and blame routing.”

   Sentence pattern to add: “Agentic PM systems do not merely remove toil; they redistribute accountability, often toward the human closest to the exception.”

   This unlocks a sharper **Substack essay**: “The AI PM Will Be Judged by the Exceptions.” It also helps him produce an **enterprise agent governance spec** with explicit blame boundaries, audit trails, override rights, and non-automatable judgment zones.

3. **Add “professional vision” mode: roles are trained ways of seeing.**  
   Anchor it on Charles Goodwin, “Professional Vision,” *American Anthropologist* 1994.

   The article defines AI Product Manager too generically: a role combining PM and AI expertise. That misses the sociological point. Professions are not just task bundles; they are disciplined ways of noticing, coding, highlighting, and arguing about reality.

   Add the claim that agentic engineering changes professional roles by changing what practitioners can see. A PM with a fleet does not just move faster; he develops new perceptual instruments: drift reports, contradiction graphs, health traces, research queues, synthesis failures, decision residue.

   Sentence pattern to add: “Agentic engineering upgrades the PM’s professional vision: it turns scattered work signals into inspectable objects that can be named, challenged, and redesigned.”

   This unlocks a differentiated **AI-PM artifact** Sean does not currently have: a “PM observability” demo or essay showing before/after perception. Not “here are my automations,” but “here is what a product manager can now see that was previously invisible.”
