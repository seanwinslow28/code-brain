## What this is

Critiques from two external reasoners (gpt-5.5 via Codex CLI, Gemini 3 via Anti-Gravity CLI) of [[deep-research-ecosystem-constraints-and-alternatives]]. The synthesizer describes what the concept is; this expansion proposes what's missing.

## From Codex (gpt-5.5)

1. **Add “ACH research mode” for contradictory-source work**
   - **Anchor:** Richards J. Heuer Jr., *Psychology of Intelligence Analysis*, especially “Analysis of Competing Hypotheses.”
   - **What to add:** A routing category for questions where the danger is not timeout but premature convergence: “List hypotheses, collect evidence, mark which evidence is inconsistent with each hypothesis, prefer the hypothesis least disconfirmed.”
   - **Unlocks:** A reusable **research-brief template** for job-market claims, AI tooling bets, and agent architecture choices. Current concept routes by task shape; ACH would route by epistemic risk.

2. **Add “information foraging / scent budget”**
   - **Anchor:** Peter Pirolli, *Information Foraging Theory: Adaptive Interaction with Information*.
   - **What to add:** A scoring layer before dispatch: expected value of information, source scent, patch-switching rule, and stop condition. Sentence pattern: “Continue local LDR while scent is increasing; switch to Gemini DR when scent fragments across patches; stop when marginal novelty drops below threshold.”
   - **Unlocks:** An **agent spec** for the Deep Research Queue that decides when to keep digging, switch tools, or abandon a topic. Right now the concept knows “single-shape vs compound”; it lacks a theory of when a research trail is worth following.

3. **Add “systematic review protocol lite”**
   - **Anchor:** Matthew J. Page et al., “The PRISMA 2020 Statement: An Updated Guideline for Reporting Systematic Reviews.”
   - **What to add:** Not full academic PRISMA, but a lightweight protocol: research question, inclusion/exclusion criteria, search strings, source classes, dedupe rule, evidence table, excluded-source log.
   - **Unlocks:** A publishable **Substack / portfolio artifact**: “How I run cheap deep research without hallucinated citations.” Current concept is internal ops hygiene; PRISMA-lite turns it into an externally credible method Sean can show to AI-PM or agentic-engineering reviewers.
