## What this is

Critiques from two external reasoners (gpt-5.5 via Codex CLI, Gemini 3 via Anti-Gravity CLI) of [[ai-product-management-and-vibe-coding-interview-integration]]. The synthesizer describes what the concept is; this expansion proposes what's missing.

## From Codex (gpt-5.5)

1. **Add “situated-action interview mode,” not “vibe-coding prep.”**  
   Anchor it on Lucy Suchman’s *Plans and Situated Actions* / *Human-Machine Reconfigurations*, which argues that plans are resources for action, not scripts that determine action. Current concept treats the interview as a stage where PM strategy plus coding ability are demonstrated. Missing move: the interviewer is really watching how Sean repairs a plan when the machine, repo, prompt, or spec stops matching reality.  
   Sentence pattern: “The signal is not that I can follow an AI workflow; it is that I can notice when the plan has become false and renegotiate the human-machine arrangement.”  
   Unlocks: a Substack essay or portfolio one-pager titled “Vibe Coding Is a Situated-Action Interview,” with a concrete rubric for breakdowns, repairs, escalations, and stop rules. Source: [Suchman overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucy_Suchman).

2. **Add “recognition-primed interview drills.”**  
   Anchor it on Gary Klein’s *Sources of Power: How People Make Decisions* and the Recognition-Primed Decision model. Sean already has agent health monitoring as a concept, but the missing facet is expert pattern recognition under time pressure: “I have seen this failure shape before; the next probe is X.”  
   Sentence pattern: “In a live AI-PM interview, I do not enumerate all options; I match the situation to a known failure pattern, mentally simulate the first workable move, and act.”  
   Unlocks: an executable interview-practice artifact: 12 timed scenarios where Sean gets a broken agent workflow, names the cue, names the likely failure class, chooses the first probe, and writes the recovery note. This would make his preparation look operational instead of generic. Source: [RPD / Klein reference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recognition-primed_decision).

3. **Add “invisible asymptote diagnosis” for AI-PM roles.**  
   Anchor it on Eugene Wei’s essay “Invisible Asymptotes.” The current concept says AI-PM plus vibe coding is a hiring signal. Missing move: every AI product has a hidden ceiling where adoption, trust, workflow fit, or eval quality flattens. Sean should show he can find that ceiling before adding agents.  
   Sentence pattern: “The question is not ‘where can we add AI?’ but ‘where will this AI workflow hit its adoption ceiling if nothing structural changes?’”  
   Unlocks: a portfolio teardown template for target companies: “AI workflow asymptote map” with one candidate product, one adoption ceiling, one eval, one agent-health metric, and one intervention. That is much sharper than claiming AI-PM readiness. Source: [Wei, “Invisible asymptotes”](https://www.eugenewei.com/blog/2018/5/21/invisible-asymptotes).
