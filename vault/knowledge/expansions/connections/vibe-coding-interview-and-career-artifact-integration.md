## What this is

Critiques from two external reasoners (gpt-5.5 via Codex CLI, Gemini 3 via Anti-Gravity CLI) of [[vibe-coding-interview-and-career-artifact-integration]]. The synthesizer describes what the concept is; this expansion proposes what's missing.

## From Codex (gpt-5.5)

1. **Add “show-your-work interview dramaturgy,” anchored on Bret Victor’s _Inventing on Principle_ talk.**  
   **What to add:** A live-demo pattern where the interview artifact is not just “I built Code-Brain,” but “watch the principle operate.” Sentence pattern: “The principle is X; here is the tool behavior before X; here is the tool behavior after X; here is the tradeoff I accepted.”  
   **Exemplar:** Bret Victor, **_Inventing on Principle_** (CUSEC 2012 talk).  
   **Unlocks:** A **vibe-coding demo script** where Sean’s portfolio stops being a capability inventory and becomes a legible engineering philosophy. Current concept says “use the artifact in interviews”; this adds the missing performance grammar for making the artifact feel inevitable, memorable, and senior.

2. **Add “brag document as evidence architecture,” anchored on Julia Evans’ _Get your work recognized: write a brag document_.**  
   **What to add:** A structured evidence layer under the Karpathy Single-Top Rec: shipped artifact, before/after state, measurable operating constraint, decision made, lesson reusable by a team. Sentence pattern: “I changed SYSTEM from BEFORE to AFTER by doing MECHANISM, under CONSTRAINT, which matters because TEAM-RISK.”  
   **Exemplar:** Julia Evans, **_Get your work recognized: write a brag document_**.  
   **Unlocks:** A **career artifact one-pager** and **interview packet** that converts Sean’s sprawling fleet into employer-readable proof. The current concept has “canonical achievement,” but not a portable evidence schema that hiring teams can scan without already caring about agents, MCP, Obsidian, or local models.

3. **Add “taste-based selection pressure,” anchored on John Boyd’s _Destruction and Creation_ / OODA loop briefings.**  
   **What to add:** A contradicting framework to “single top rec”: instead of one canonical artifact, frame interviews as rapid orientation under uncertainty. The artifact should prove Sean can observe a messy system, orient through constraints, decide what matters, and act without overfitting to the prompt. Sentence pattern: “Given this ambiguous system, I would discard A, preserve B, test C, and stop if D happens.”  
   **Exemplar:** John Boyd, **_Destruction and Creation_** and the **OODA loop briefings**.  
   **Unlocks:** A **decision-drill runbook** for FDE / AI-PM interviews: five 20-minute scenarios where Sean demonstrates judgment, not just tooling. This fixes the current concept’s risk: anchoring too hard on a prebuilt artifact can make him sound like a systems hobbyist showing his favorite machine, rather than an operator who can re-orient inside the employer’s problem space.
