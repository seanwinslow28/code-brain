## What this is

Critiques from two external reasoners (gpt-5.5 via Codex CLI, Gemini 3 via Anti-Gravity CLI) of [[tier-1-tier-2-separation-for-cross-domain-insight-integrity]]. The synthesizer describes what the concept is; this expansion proposes what's missing.

## From Codex (gpt-5.5)

1. **Add “controlled trading-zone bridges,” not just tier separation.**  
   Anchor it on Peter Galison’s *Image and Logic: A Material Culture of Microphysics* and his “trading zones” concept: groups can collaborate through local exchange rules even when their meanings differ globally. This would turn the concept from “prevent contamination” into “define where cross-domain exchange is allowed.”  
   **Unlocks:** an agent spec or vault runbook for “bridge notes”: when job-hunt, agent-fleet, and creative-studio concepts may share terms, what metadata they need, and when knowledge-lint should flag them. Source: [Trading zones / Galison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trading_zones).

2. **Add “provenance contracts” for every synthesized claim.**  
   Anchor it on Luc Moreau and Paul Groth’s W3C PROV model, especially the entity/activity/agent distinction for assessing trust and reliability. Your current concept says Tier 1 and Tier 2 should stay separated, but not how a reader can audit *who generated a claim, from what source, through what transformation*.  
   **Unlocks:** a concrete schema upgrade for concept frontmatter: `derived_from`, `synthesis_agent`, `source_domain`, `transform_type`, `confidence`, `review_status`. That would let Sean ship a portfolio one-pager on “auditable personal knowledge agents” instead of another second-brain summary. Source: [W3C PROV overview](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-overview/) / [PROV summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W3C_Prov).

3. **Add “bisociation mode” as the sanctioned exception to integrity rules.**  
   Anchor it on Arthur Koestler’s *The Act of Creation* or Fauconnier and Turner’s *The Way We Think*. Both argue that creativity often comes from blending incompatible frames, not keeping domains pure. Sentence pattern to add: “Tier separation protects ordinary retrieval; bisociation mode deliberately violates it under labeled, reversible, provenance-rich conditions.”  
   **Unlocks:** a Substack essay or executable demo where the vault critic proposes weird but traceable cross-domain inventions: 16BitFit mechanics applied to job-search rituals, agent-fleet observability applied to personal energy, intent-engineering applied to game NPC behavior. Source: [Koestler, *The Act of Creation*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Act_of_Creation), [Fauconnier & Turner, *The Way We Think*](https://www.wired.com/2002/04/the-way-we-think-conceptual-blending-and-the-minds-hidden-complexities/).
