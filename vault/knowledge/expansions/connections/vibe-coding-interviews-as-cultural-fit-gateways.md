## What this is

Critiques from two external reasoners (gpt-5.5 via Codex CLI, Gemini 3 via Anti-Gravity CLI) of [[vibe-coding-interviews-as-cultural-fit-gateways]]. The synthesizer describes what the concept is; this expansion proposes what's missing.

## From Codex (gpt-5.5)

1. **Add “Work-Sample Validity vs Culture-Fit Smuggling”**

   Anchor it on Frank L. Schmidt & John E. Hunter, **“The Validity and Utility of Selection Methods in Personnel Psychology”**.

   The current concept treats vibe-coding as both skills test and culture-fit assessment, but it does not separate *valid signal* from *bias-prone interpretation*. Add the missing distinction: structured work samples are among the highest-validity hiring tools; vague “fit” judgments are where interviewers launder taste, similarity bias, and class-coded communication norms.

   Sentence pattern to add:  
   “The interview is legitimate when it measures observable work behavior; it becomes suspect when the same behavior is reinterpreted as personality evidence.”

   This unlocks a **job-hunt interview rubric** Sean can use to evaluate companies: “Is this a real work sample, or a culture-fit vibe check wearing a coding mask?” It also gives him a sharper Substack essay: *The Vibe-Coding Interview Is Either the Best Hiring Tool in AI or the Most Plausible Bias Machine Ever Built.*

2. **Add “Interaction Ritual / Face-Work Mode”**

   Anchor it on Erving Goffman, **Interaction Ritual: Essays on Face-to-Face Behavior**, especially the essays on face-work.

   The article says “transparency, humility, feedback receptivity,” but those are generic corporate virtues. Goffman gives Sean a better lens: the interview is a live ritual where both people are managing face, status, repair, embarrassment, competence, and conversational footing. In vibe-coding, the candidate is not only solving the problem; he is constantly repairing small failures in public without collapsing the shared interaction.

   Sentence pattern to add:  
   “A strong candidate narrates uncertainty without transferring anxiety to the interviewer.”

   This unlocks an **interview performance playbook** with concrete micro-moves: how to recover after a wrong turn, how to invite correction without sounding helpless, how to disagree with a hint, how to keep dignity while debugging aloud. The current concept can say “be receptive”; Goffman lets Sean script the actual social mechanics.

3. **Add “Pair-Programming Driver/Navigator Contract”**

   Anchor it on Laurie Williams & Robert Kessler, **Pair Programming Illuminated**.

   Vibe-coding interviews are often closer to broken pair-programming than solo assessment. The missing facet is role negotiation: who is driving, who is navigating, when the interviewer’s hint becomes a requirement, when the candidate should pause and ask for alignment, and how collaboration changes once an AI assistant is also in the loop.

   Sentence pattern to add:  
   “Treat the interviewer as a temporary navigator, not an audience member.”

   This unlocks an **agentic-engineering interview demo format** Sean can ship: a 20-minute recorded “AI pair session” where he explicitly alternates between driver, navigator, and agent-orchestrator roles. That artifact would show the thing his resume claims: not just that he can code with agents, but that he can coordinate humans and agents inside an ambiguous work loop.
