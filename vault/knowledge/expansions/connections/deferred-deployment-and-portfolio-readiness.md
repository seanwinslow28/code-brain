## What this is

Critiques from two external reasoners (gpt-5.5 via Codex CLI, Gemini 3 via Anti-Gravity CLI) of [[deferred-deployment-and-portfolio-readiness]]. The synthesizer describes what the concept is; this expansion proposes what's missing.

## From Codex (gpt-5.5)

1. **Add “Deployment as Real Option” mode**
   - **What to add:** Treat deferred deployment as an options problem, not a readiness mood. Add a note section that separates **option value**, **exercise trigger**, **expiry risk**, and **cost of waiting**.
   - **Anchor:** Avinash K. Dixit and Robert S. Pindyck, *Investment Under Uncertainty*; also Richard Rumelt, *Good Strategy/Bad Strategy*, especially the “kernel” of diagnosis, guiding policy, coherent action.
   - **Unlocks:** A portfolio deployment decision memo Sean can actually use: “ship now / wait / soft-launch / noindex preview / private reviewer route.” The current concept says “don’t launch half-finished.” This would force a sharper artifact: a one-page **Portfolio Launch Options Brief** with reversible vs irreversible moves, explicit triggers, and search-indexing risk priced against job-market timing.

2. **Add “Progressive Disclosure Launch” as the contradicting framework**
   - **What to add:** The concept assumes public indexing is binary: either dark or fully launched. Add a staged-release pattern: **private artifact → passworded review → noindex public URL → niche-indexed page → canonical homepage**.
   - **Anchor:** Eric Ries, *The Lean Startup*, specifically the MVP/validated-learning loop; paired with Basecamp’s “Shape Up” by Ryan Singer, especially appetite and hill-chart framing.
   - **Unlocks:** A deployable **recruiter feedback loop** instead of a readiness freeze. Sean could ship a `noindex` portfolio preview to 5 trusted reviewers, instrument what they misunderstand, and revise against evidence. The current concept cannot reach this because it treats “first impression” as something to protect from, not something to test under controlled exposure.

3. **Add “Artifact-Led Credibility Stack”**
   - **What to add:** Replace generic “portfolio readiness” with a stack of proof objects: **claim → evidence artifact → reproducible demo → narrative wrapper → recruiter skim path**.
   - **Anchor:** Patrick McKenzie, “Don’t Call Yourself A Programmer, And Other Career Advice”; Julia Evans, “Write good examples by starting with real code”; Simon Willison’s public project notes around Datasette, especially how he turns working software plus running commentary into durable credibility.
   - **Unlocks:** A portfolio one-pager that does not merely describe Code-Brain, 16BitFit, or intent-engineering. It would produce a **proof-first portfolio structure**: each project gets a claim, a concrete shipped artifact, a short technical walkthrough, and a hiring-manager translation. Current failure mode: “Sean has impressive systems.” Better artifact: “Here is the executable evidence that Sean can design, govern, and ship agentic workflows.”
