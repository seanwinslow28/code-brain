## What this is

Critiques from two external reasoners (gpt-5.5 via Codex CLI, Gemini 3 via Anti-Gravity CLI) of [[job-hunt-automation-and-knowledge-vault-integration]]. The synthesizer describes what the concept is; this expansion proposes what's missing.

## From Codex (gpt-5.5)

1. **Add “job search as discovery system,” anchored on Steve Blank’s _The Four Steps to the Epiphany_**

   Sean’s concept treats the job hunt as a pipeline integrated into the vault. What’s missing is the canonical customer-development loop: hypotheses, contact scripts, disconfirming evidence, segment pivots, and learning velocity.

   Add a section like: **“Every target role is a customer hypothesis.”** Pattern: `I believe [role archetype] values [proof artifact] enough to trade [attention/interview/referral] for it; evidence will be [observable response].`

   This unlocks a **job-hunt experiment ledger**: a vault-backed artifact that converts applications, recruiter calls, coffee chats, and portfolio views into falsifiable market-learning loops. Right now the concept can summarize market insights; this would let Sean decide which positioning to kill, double down on, or split-test.

2. **Add “career capital signal design,” anchored on Cal Newport’s _So Good They Can’t Ignore You_**

   The concept says the vault can synthesize job-market insights, but it does not distinguish between “I know things” and “I have scarce, legible career capital.” Newport’s useful frame is not passion advice; it’s the claim that valuable work follows rare and valuable skills.

   Add a mode called **“proof-of-career-capital extraction.”** Pattern: `For each target role, name the scarce capability, the observable proof, the hiring-manager inference, and the artifact Sean can ship in public.`

   This unlocks a **portfolio one-pager / recruiter proof matrix** that maps Code-Brain, 16BitFit, intent-engineering, and llm-council to buyer-facing claims. Current failure mode: “impressive personal system.” Better artifact: “evidence that Sean can design, govern, and ship agentic workflows under cost, reliability, and privacy constraints.”

3. **Add “weak-tie opportunity brokerage,” anchored on Mark Granovetter’s paper “The Strength of Weak Ties”**

   The article mentions automation and synthesis, but it misses the sociology of job search: opportunities often arrive through weak ties, not the strongest personal network or the cleanest job board. For Sean, the missing unit is not “job posting”; it is **bridge person plus context packet**.

   Add a **weak-tie activation mode**. Pattern: `This person is adjacent to [domain], likely sees [opportunity class], and should receive [one artifact] plus [one precise ask].`

   This unlocks a **network-intelligence runbook**: ranked bridge contacts, tailored artifact packets, and lightweight asks tied to specific roles or companies. The current concept can connect job hunt plus vault; this would let it produce outbound moves: referral briefs, warm-intro notes, “here’s the demo that explains why I’m relevant” packets, and follow-up timing decisions.
