## What this is

Critiques from two external reasoners (gpt-5.5 via Codex CLI, Gemini 3 via Anti-Gravity CLI) of [[ai-career-path-integration-with-daily-routine-automation]]. The synthesizer describes what the concept is; this expansion proposes what's missing.

## From Codex (gpt-5.5)

1. **Add “Temporal Structure Of Work” anchored on Zerubavel’s *Hidden Rhythms***

   **What to add:** A rhythm-design layer: daily automation is not just productivity support, it is a temporal architecture that allocates attention, recovery, research, outreach, and portfolio production.

   **Exemplar:** Eviatar Zerubavel, *Hidden Rhythms: Schedules and Calendars in Social Life*.

   **Unlock:** This turns the concept from “my agents help my job hunt” into a sharper Substack essay or portfolio one-pager: **“The Calendar Is The Product Surface For Agentic Work.”** Sean can show how his fleet is not a pile of scripts but a designed temporal operating system. Artifact: a weekly rhythm map with agent runs, human decision points, review gates, and career-facing outputs.

2. **Add “Developmental Job Crafting” anchored on Wrzesniewski & Dutton’s paper**

   **What to add:** A job-crafting frame: Sean is not merely preparing for AI-PM roles; he is actively reshaping his current routines into evidence for the next role category.

   **Exemplar:** Amy Wrzesniewski and Jane Dutton, “Crafting a Job: Revisioning Employees as Active Crafters of Their Work,” *Academy of Management Review*, 2001.

   **Unlock:** This gives him a better interview narrative than “I built automations to be productive.” It supports a decision artifact: **role-fit matrix by crafted evidence**, mapping each daily routine output to AI-PM, senior PM, or agentic-engineering IC signals. Current concept cannot distinguish productivity from career capital. This addition makes the daily note, agent digest, critic output, and portfolio refresh legible as deliberate proof-of-work.

3. **Add “Deliberate Practice Feedback Loop” anchored on Ericsson’s *Peak***

   **What to add:** A practice-loop standard: every automated daily routine should identify one skill being trained, one feedback source, one stretch target, and one artifact produced.

   **Exemplar:** Anders Ericsson and Robert Pool, *Peak: Secrets from the New Science of Expertise*.

   **Unlock:** This prevents the concept from collapsing into passive routine optimization. It would let Sean ship an **agent spec or runbook** called “Career Practice Agent”: each morning it selects one AI-PM or agentic-engineering micro-skill, pulls evidence from the vault, generates a task, and scores the output against a rubric. The current concept says automation improves retention and focus; this would make it produce measurable professional reps.
