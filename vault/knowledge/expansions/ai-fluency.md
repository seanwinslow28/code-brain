## What this is

Critiques from two external reasoners (gpt-5.5 via Codex CLI, Gemini 3 via Anti-Gravity CLI) of [[ai-fluency]]. The synthesizer describes what the concept is; this expansion proposes what's missing.

## From Codex (gpt-5.5)

1. **Add “Judgment Under Uncertainty” Fluency**
   - **What to add:** AI fluency is not just acting early; it is knowing when agent output is unreliable, when to defer, and how to structure decisions under incomplete evidence.
   - **Anchor:** Daniel Kahneman, Paul Slovic, and Amos Tversky, *Judgment under Uncertainty: Heuristics and Biases*; especially the availability, representativeness, and anchoring frames.
   - **Unlocks for Sean:** A stronger job-hunt artifact: **“AI Fluency Is Calibration, Not Tool Usage.”** This lets Sean distinguish himself from generic “I use agents” candidates by showing decision hygiene: confidence bands, pre-mortems, dissent prompts, escalation rules, and evidence thresholds. Current concept says “act before the playbook catches up”; this adds “know what kind of error you are probably making while acting.”

2. **Add “Situated Action” As A Contradiction To Playbook Collapse**
   - **What to add:** The concept assumes playbooks lag reality, so fluent people improvise with tools. Add the stronger claim that work is never fully captured by playbooks in the first place; real competence lives in situated adaptation.
   - **Anchor:** Lucy Suchman, *Plans and Situated Actions: The Problem of Human-Machine Communication*.
   - **Unlocks for Sean:** A sharper **agent-spec / intent-engineering artifact**: “When Plans Fail, Agents Need Situated Intent.” This would let him argue that agent systems should not only execute predefined workflows, but carry stop rules, context probes, repair moves, and human handoff triggers. Current concept reaches “use tools faster”; Suchman gets him to “design agents for the gap between plan and situation.”

3. **Add “Fluency As Articulation Work”**
   - **What to add:** AI fluency should include the invisible coordination labor required to make AI outputs usable: translating fuzzy goals into constraints, reconciling stakeholders, maintaining provenance, and repairing downstream ambiguity.
   - **Anchor:** Susan Leigh Star and Anselm Strauss, “Layers of Silence, Arenas of Voice: The Ecology of Visible and Invisible Work.”
   - **Unlocks for Sean:** A portfolio-ready **runbook or operating model**: “The AI PM’s Articulation Layer.” This gives him a vocabulary for the work between prompting and shipping: evidence capture, review loops, decision records, eval rubrics, source hygiene, and cross-agent coordination. Current concept risks sounding like speed worship; articulation work reframes Sean’s advantage as making agent output organizationally legible and safe to act on.
