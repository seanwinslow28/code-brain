## What this is

Critiques from two external reasoners (gpt-5.5 via Codex CLI, Gemini 3 via Anti-Gravity CLI) of [[ai-product-manager]]. The synthesizer describes what the concept is; this expansion proposes what's missing.

## From Codex (gpt-5.5)

1. **Add “product sense under uncertainty” as a distinct AI-PM facet**

   Anchor it on Shreyas Doshi’s essay **“Product Sense: A Practical Guide”** and Julie Zhuo’s **_The Making of a Manager_**, especially the parts on ambiguous judgment rather than process ownership.

   Current concept frames AI PM as “leveraging AI to shape strategy,” which is too generic. Add a sharper claim: AI PM is not just PM plus AI fluency; it is the ability to make product calls when model behavior, eval quality, user trust, and cost curves are all unstable.

   This unlocks a portfolio artifact Sean cannot currently produce: a **one-page AI product judgment memo**. Example: “When should an agent ask, act, or refuse?” That is stronger for Anthropic/Sierra-style loops than another description of agent infrastructure.

2. **Add “AI as material, not feature”**

   Anchor it on Matt Jones’s talk/essay pattern around **“Designing with AI as a Material”** and John Maeda’s **_Designing for Simplicity_**. The missing facet is that AI PM is partly a material-practice role: understanding the weird affordances of latency, hallucination, personality, opacity, sampling, memory, and stochastic failure.

   Sentence pattern to add: “An AI PM does not merely decide where to insert AI; they decide which AI properties should become part of the user experience and which must be hidden behind product constraints.”

   This unlocks a **product teardown genre** Sean should ship: “Five AI products that misunderstand the material.” It would let him critique ChatGPT-like, agentic, or copilot products through interaction design rather than just strategy. Right now the concept risks sounding like “PM who knows agents”; this makes him sound like someone who can shape AI-native UX.

3. **Add the contradicting frame: “AI PM may be a transitional role, not a durable job category”**

   Anchor it on Clayton Christensen’s **_The Innovator’s Solution_** job-to-be-done framing and Ben Thompson’s essay **“Aggregation Theory”**. The contradiction: “AI Product Manager” may not stabilize as a title because AI capability gets absorbed into every PM role, while specialized value migrates either downward into builders or upward into strategy/governance.

   Add this as a tension, not a rejection: “The AI PM title is useful in hiring markets, but the durable capability may be agentic product judgment: translating ambiguous user intent into systems that act safely, observably, and economically.”

   This unlocks a **job-hunt positioning artifact**: a sharper portfolio headline and interview answer. Instead of “I’m targeting AI PM roles,” Sean can say: “I’m strongest where PM judgment meets working agent systems: evals, autonomy boundaries, product strategy, and shipped prototypes.” That is more defensible for both AI-PM and agentic-engineering IC loops.
