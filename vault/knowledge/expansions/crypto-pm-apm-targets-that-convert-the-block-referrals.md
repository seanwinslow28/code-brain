## What this is

Critiques from two external reasoners (gpt-5.5 via Codex CLI, Gemini 3 via Anti-Gravity CLI) of [[crypto-pm-apm-targets-that-convert-the-block-referrals]]. The synthesizer describes what the concept is; this expansion proposes what's missing.

## From Codex (gpt-5.5)

1. **Add “brokerage map” mode, not target-list mode**
   - **Anchor:** Ronald S. Burt, *Structural Holes: The Social Structure of Competition*.
   - **What to add:** Treat Larry/Matt referrals as brokerage capital, not generic endorsements. Add columns for `broker`, `bridge reason`, `recipient trust path`, `warmth`, `proof needed`, and `second-hop ask`.
   - **Unlocks:** A referral graph / portfolio artifact showing Sean can reason about network leverage like an operator. Current concept says “Coinbase/Binance/Coindesk”; this would produce a living “who can actually open which door, and why” map.

2. **Add “trigger-event hiring” mode**
   - **Anchor:** Bob Moesta and Greg Engle, *Demand-Side Sales 101*.
   - **What to add:** Stop ranking companies only by referral fit. Rank them by hiring pain signals: new AI initiative, PM attrition, new funding, product pivot, compliance expansion, data-product rebuild, agent tooling rollout. Sentence pattern: “Because COMPANY just did EVENT, they likely need CAPABILITY; Sean’s proof artifact is ARTIFACT.”
   - **Unlocks:** A weekly job-hunt runbook where Sean targets moments of demand, not static companies. This lets him ship sharper outreach briefs and referral packets instead of a stale company list.

3. **Add “credential translation packet” mode**
   - **Anchor:** Pierre Bourdieu, “The Forms of Capital” in *Handbook of Theory and Research for the Sociology of Education*.
   - **What to add:** The Block referral is social capital, but Sean still needs to convert it into cultural/technical capital legible to each hiring audience. Add three packet variants: `crypto-native PM`, `AI PM`, and `agentic-engineering IC`. Each should translate the same experience into different proof: market judgment, product leadership, or executable systems.
   - **Unlocks:** A portfolio one-pager per target type. Current concept can say where referrals matter; this addition lets Sean decide what artifact Larry/Matt should attach when they make the intro.
