## What this is

Critiques from two external reasoners (gpt-5.5 via Codex CLI, Gemini 3 via Anti-Gravity CLI) of [[signal-vs-safety-trade-off-in-resume-architecture]]. The synthesizer describes what the concept is; this expansion proposes what's missing.

## From Codex (gpt-5.5)

1. **Add “Proof-of-Work Resume Architecture” anchored on Laszlo Bock’s hiring rubric**

   **What to add:** A counter-framework to “signal vs safety”: resumes should foreground *evidence density*, not novelty. Use Bock’s formula from **Laszlo Bock, _Work Rules!_** and his Google hiring advice: “Accomplished X, as measured by Y, by doing Z.”

   **Exemplifies it:** Laszlo Bock, **_Work Rules!: Insights from Inside Google That Will Transform How You Live and Lead_**.

   **Unlocks:** A resume rewrite pattern where Sean’s artifacts stop being “cool things I built” and become measurable hiring evidence. Artifact genre: **proof ledger resume bullets**.

   Sentence pattern:

   > Built `Code-Brain`, a 7-agent local knowledge loop, reducing nightly synthesis cost to `$0/run` while maintaining scheduled indexing, critique, research, and daily planning across `~110` concepts and `~260` links.

   This lets the concept distinguish “section order signal” from “claim verifiability signal.”

2. **Add “Credential-Breach Framing” anchored on Marc Andreessen’s “product-market fit”**

   **What to add:** The concept currently assumes two audiences: traditional recruiters and AI-forward hiring managers. Missing third audience: the person who thinks Sean is *too weird to map*. Add a “credential breach” facet: when artifact-first positioning violates the expected ontology of PM resumes, the artifact must create undeniable market pull.

   **Exemplifies it:** Marc Andreessen, **“The Only Thing That Matters”** essay on product-market fit.

   **Unlocks:** A decision rule for when to lead with artifacts versus title history:

   > Lead with artifacts only when the artifact creates pull strong enough that the reader forgives category confusion.

   Artifact genre: **portfolio one-pager / hiring-manager brief** with PMF-style evidence: who has used it, what pain it solves, what changed because it exists. Right now the concept says “signal over safety”; this would specify the threshold where that bet is rational rather than aesthetic.

3. **Add “Illegibility Tax” anchored on James C. Scott**

   **What to add:** A contradiction: artifact-first resumes increase signal for humans who understand the artifacts, but may become illegible to institutions that need standardized categories. Name this as the **illegibility tax**.

   **Exemplifies it:** James C. Scott, **_Seeing Like a State: How Certain Schemes to Improve the Human Condition Have Failed_**.

   **Unlocks:** A two-track resume architecture: one artifact-forward “human hiring manager” version and one legible “institutional parser” version. Artifact genre: **resume routing runbook**.

   The missing decision rule:

   > If the path depends on ATS, recruiter screen, or compliance workflow, preserve legibility. If the path depends on founder, VP Product, eng manager, or AI tooling lead, spend illegibility for signal.

   This prevents the concept from collapsing into a single heroic stance. The sharper version is: Sean is not choosing signal over safety universally; he is choosing where to pay the illegibility tax.
