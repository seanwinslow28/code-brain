## What this is

Critiques from two external reasoners (gpt-5.5 via Codex CLI, Gemini 3 via Anti-Gravity CLI) of [[context-compounding]]. The synthesizer describes what the concept is; this expansion proposes what's missing.

## From Codex (gpt-5.5)

1. **Add “Progressive Formalization” as the missing bridge from vibes to durable agent specs.**  
   **Anchor:** Gerhard Fischer, “Beyond ‘Couch Potatoes’: From Consumers to Designers and Active Contributors” and the broader progressive-formalization work from Fischer’s Center for LifeLong Learning & Design.  
   **Pattern to add:** “Start with situated mess, preserve the informal trace, then formalize only the parts that repeatedly matter.”  
   **What this unlocks:** A stronger artifact than “context compounding”: a **Voiceprint Formalization Runbook** showing how Sean turns Cheese Gauntlet reactions, screenplay fragments, and revision notes into progressively stricter schemas, rubrics, evals, and agent instructions. Right now the concept risks sounding like “more personal context makes outputs better.” Progressive formalization gives him the operational move: when to keep context messy, when to crystallize it, and what gets promoted into infrastructure.

2. **Add “Reflective Practice” to explain supervision as skill acquisition, not just data accumulation.**  
   **Anchor:** Donald Schön, *The Reflective Practitioner: How Professionals Think in Action* (1983).  
   **Pattern to add:** “Reflection-in-action: the practitioner changes the frame while acting, not after the fact.”  
   **What this unlocks:** A **Substack essay mode** where Sean can argue that good human-AI work is not prompt optimization or context stuffing, but live reframing under uncertainty. This would sharpen “Raising Claude” because the current concept overweights stored artifacts and underweights Sean’s situated judgment during the session. Schön gives him language for the thing agents cannot infer from the vault alone: the human seeing a bad draft and knowing which assumption to attack.

3. **Add “Forgetting / Context Pruning” as the contradicting framework.**  
   **Anchor:** Jorge Luis Borges, “Funes the Memorious” (1942). Pair with Viktor Mayer-Schönberger, *Delete: The Virtue of Forgetting in the Digital Age* (2009), if he wants a nonfiction policy frame.  
   **Pattern to add:** “Compounding only works if forgetting is designed; otherwise memory becomes interference.”  
   **What this unlocks:** An **agent spec or eval harness** the current concept cannot reach: a `context-promotion / context-expiry` protocol for Code-Brain. Criteria: what raw reactions stay ephemeral, what gets summarized, what becomes a reusable voice rule, what must be deleted because it overfits the agent to yesterday’s taste. This is the strongest missing counterweight. Without it, “context compounding” can collapse into hoarding. With it, Sean can ship a defensible portfolio artifact: **Context Compounding Requires Designed Forgetting**.
