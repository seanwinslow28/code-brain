## What this is

Critiques from two external reasoners (gpt-5.5 via Codex CLI, Gemini 3 via Anti-Gravity CLI) of [[crypto-pm-apm-role-specifications]]. The synthesizer describes what the concept is; this expansion proposes what's missing.

## From Codex (gpt-5.5)

1. **Add “on-chain product telemetry mode” anchored on Dune’s Spellbook repo**

   **What to add:** A role-spec facet that treats crypto PM work as *instrumentation design*, not just “blockchain knowledge.” Sentence pattern: “This PM can define the event/account/address-level metric that proves whether the product is working.”

   **Exemplar:** `duneanalytics/spellbook` on GitHub, especially community-maintained decoded tables and sector-specific models.

   **Unlocks:** A portfolio one-pager or interview artifact titled “How I’d Measure Product-Market Fit for a Crypto Product Without Waiting for BI.” Current concept says “experience with exchange platforms”; this would let Sean show a concrete PM edge: translating wallets, contracts, flows, cohorts, liquidity, retention, and protocol behavior into measurable product decisions.

2. **Add “mechanism-design PM mode” anchored on Alvin Roth’s _Who Gets What — and Why_**

   **What to add:** A contradicting framework to normal SaaS PM role specs: in crypto, product surfaces often *are markets*, so PMs need to reason about incentives, congestion, liquidity, adverse selection, and strategic behavior. Sentence pattern: “The PM does not only optimize UX; they choose the rules under which rational users will exploit the system.”

   **Exemplar:** Alvin E. Roth, _Who Gets What — and Why: The New Economics of Matchmaking and Market Design_.

   **Unlocks:** A Substack essay or case-study artifact: “Crypto PM Is Market Design Wearing a UX Mask.” This gives Sean a stronger senior-PM positioning than generic “web3 experience,” especially for exchanges, prediction markets, lending, wallets, rewards, staking, governance, and tokenized marketplaces.

3. **Add “regulatory product surface mode” anchored on TuongVy Le’s policy writing at Bain Capital Crypto**

   **What to add:** A missing facet that crypto PM specs often hide: the PM must design under legal ambiguity without becoming a lawyer. Add criteria for regulatory sensemaking: custody, disclosures, jurisdictional rollout, token classification risk, compliance UX, and what features should not ship yet.

   **Exemplar:** TuongVy Le, Bain Capital Crypto essays and policy analysis, especially her writing on crypto regulation and market structure.

   **Unlocks:** A runbook or interview decision memo: “Feature Launch Risk Triage for a Crypto PM.” Current concept can map referrals to role titles; this addition lets Sean produce a hiring-manager-grade artifact showing judgment: which tradeoffs get escalated, which risks become product constraints, and how to keep speed without cosplaying legal certainty.
