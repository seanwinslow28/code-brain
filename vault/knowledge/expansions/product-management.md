## What this is

Critiques from two external reasoners (gpt-5.5 via Codex CLI, Gemini 3 via Anti-Gravity CLI) of [[product-management]]. The synthesizer describes what the concept is; this expansion proposes what's missing.

## From Codex (gpt-5.5)

## 1. Add **Product Sense as Taste Under Constraint**

**What to add:**  
Add a “product taste” facet: PM is not just lifecycle guidance; it is the repeated act of choosing what *not* to build when evidence is incomplete.

**Anchor work:**  
Julie Zhuo, “How to Develop Product Sense” and *The Making of a Manager*. Pair with Brian Eno’s idea of “scenius” from Kevin Kelly’s essay “Scenius, or Communal Genius.”

**Sentence pattern:**  
“Product sense is the judgment layer that turns ambiguous user, market, and system signals into a defensible bet.”

**What this unlocks for Sean:**  
A portfolio artifact he currently cannot produce from this concept: a **Product Taste Memo**. For each project, especially Code-Brain or 16BitFit, he could show: rejected options, constraints, user stakes, why the chosen path was better, and what would change his mind. This is stronger than “I guide lifecycle” because it demonstrates executive-quality judgment.

## 2. Add **Jobs-to-Be-Done Demand-Side Causality**

**What to add:**  
Add JTBD as a contradiction to generic “market needs.” The missing move is: products do not satisfy markets; people “hire” products inside struggling moments.

**Anchor work:**  
Clayton Christensen, Taddy Hall, Karen Dillon, and David Duncan, *Competing Against Luck*. Also Bob Moesta, *Learning to Build*, for interview technique.

**Sentence pattern:**  
“Instead of asking what segment wants, ask what progress a person was trying to make when they switched behavior.”

**What this unlocks for Sean:**  
A **job-hunt positioning artifact**: “What job is a hiring manager hiring Sean to do?” That could become a one-page role-fit map for AI-PM interviews: struggling moment, current workaround, desired progress, anxiety, hiring criteria, proof artifact. It would also sharpen Substack essays by moving from “AI agents are useful” to “the user was trapped in X recurring situation and hired an agent fleet to escape it.”

## 3. Add **Product Ops / Decision Systems as PM’s Missing Infrastructure**

**What to add:**  
Add Product Operations as the operational substrate of PM: decision cadence, evidence hygiene, instrumentation, rituals, and feedback loops. This directly connects PM to Sean’s agent-fleet work.

**Anchor work:**  
Melissa Perri, *Escaping the Build Trap*. Pair with Marty Cagan, *Inspired*, specifically the product discovery/product delivery distinction.

**Sentence pattern:**  
“Product management fails when it becomes feature throughput; product ops makes the learning loop inspectable.”

**What this unlocks for Sean:**  
A **runbook or agent spec**: “Agentic Product Ops for a Solo Builder.” The current concept cannot reach this because it treats PM as human coordination. This addition lets Sean define agents as PM infrastructure: opportunity intake, evidence scoring, assumption tracking, experiment review, decision logs, and portfolio refresh. It gives him a concrete bridge between senior PM credibility and agentic-engineering IC signal.
