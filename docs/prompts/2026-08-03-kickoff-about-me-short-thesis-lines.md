## 3. The contract the lines must satisfy (from the brief — do not re-litigate)

**The question (Sean's only spoken line in the film):**
- Lands immediately after the held quiet beat — the narration/picture gap has
  just closed, so the register is **sincere**; no gag rides on it.
- Must demonstrate method, not mere pausing (R1's failure test: "the question
  could have been asked by anyone who merely paused").
- Must cue the screen-replay beat — the answer is SHOWN on the big TV (the
  cursor circling, never finding the buy button), so the question should
  naturally produce a *shown* answer, not a spoken one.
- Recruiter-legible with zero PM vocabulary; short enough to caption; reads in
  one breath.

**The narrator's button line (the film's last audio):**
- One line, serene, over the colorized USER's smile; the narrator's composure
  is intact to the end and he never acknowledges the chaos or describes the
  visible joke.
- Goofy-grammar: it inverts or double-reads the premise — no moral, no
  growth-lesson, no sincerity spike. The theme lands as a further joke.
- Silent-proof: it must also work as a caption.
