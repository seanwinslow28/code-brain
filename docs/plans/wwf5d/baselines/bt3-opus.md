## Artifact 1 — Chain-level Seam Report

One bulleted finding per handoff, grouped by stage-pair. Every finding is tagged exactly one of `dangerously-wrong` / `structural` / `minor`, names the stage-pair, names the dropped/re-derived input, and states the concrete failure a reader (or, where the failure is a false verdict, the reader who receives the shipped slop) would observe.

### Stage-pair: storytelling-architecture → substack-value-engine

- **[`structural`] The seam beat is emitted but re-derived instead of consumed.** storytelling's Handoff Block names FOUR markers — open-loop ledger, central loop, crest beat, and **seam beat** ("the beat where value / the Transfer lands, the SO-CAN-YOU beat"). value-engine consumes the crest beat (for the pivot line) and the central loop (for the close) *by name*, but it never consumes the seam beat by name — instead it "annotates the beat map with... where the artifact/payoff/sideways-ask land," re-deciding the value location itself. **What a reader observes:** the how-to/Transfer can land at a beat the story never built toward, so the value section reads bolted-on ("...and here are 5 tips") — the exact "bolted-on appendix" anti-pattern both skills explicitly try to prevent, reintroduced by the two skills each independently deciding "where value goes."

- **[`minor`] No completeness check on the Handoff Block at its first consumer.** value-engine assumes the Handoff Block exists and is well-formed ("attaching to the crest beat and central loop the Handoff Block names *rather than re-deriving them*"). Nothing makes it verify all four markers are present or that every ledger loop has a scheduled close before it consumes them. **What a reader observes:** if storytelling under-fills the block (a loop with no close) or a beat map enters the chain hand-authored with no block at all, value-engine silently falls back to deriving crest/central-loop by eye — the drift the block was added to kill, now undetected at the very first consumer, and indistinguishable in the final post from an ordinarily mediocre piece.

### Stage-pair: substack-value-engine → writing-voice-modes

- **[`structural`] The locked single takeaway is handed over but never consumed *as the governing spine*.** value-engine's core output on PASS is the locked takeaway ("lock the Transfer as the single takeaway... the spine every section serves") and it says "a fuzzy takeaway here becomes a fuzzy piece four stages later." But voice-modes' own instructions tell it to "write 100% of the prose fresh against the **beat map**" — the beat map, not the takeaway. The takeaway is never named as an input voice-modes holds as the spine while composing. **What a reader observes:** a fluent, well-voiced post they finish without being able to state what they can now *do* — the one welded capability got diluted across paragraphs because the stage that wrote every sentence was steering by the beat order, not by the locked takeaway. (Fix must not touch voice content; it adds an input contract *around* the stage — see Spec.)

- **[`minor`] Rule-of-One's "cut the rest" collides with the contract's "does not reorder beats."** When triage surfaces multiple itches, value-engine must reduce to one ("pick the realest and **cut the rest**"). But the chain contract also states value-engine "does **not** reorder beats." Cutting a dropped itch's beats is not reordering, yet the contract never says so, so a pedantically literal value-engine could leave the losing itch's beats in the map. **What a reader observes:** a post that promises one thing in the title/hook but wanders into a second, half-solved itch — the precise broken promise Rule-of-One exists to prevent — because voice-modes faithfully voiced beats the gate meant to delete.

### Stage-pair: writing-voice-modes → writing-critique

- **[`dangerously-wrong`] The locked voice stage is an unowned context chokepoint for every upstream intent artifact; critique's gates are conditional and fail *open*.** critique checks structure "against storytelling's open-loop ledger **when the ledger is present in context**" and re-checks value "**when the named Value Gate verdict is present**." Both checks are guarded by "when present." The three artifacts they depend on (open-loop ledger, Value Gate verdict, locked takeaway) were all born *before* the Tier-1 voice stage, which emits only prose and is charged with carrying nothing forward. No stage owns the crossing. **What a reader observes:** a published post with an open loop (a question the hook raised and the body never answers) or a missing Transfer (a capability promised, never delivered) that a working critique gate would have blocked — but the gate found the ledger/verdict absent, *silently skipped both checks*, and still returned `ship`. The owner trusts a `ship` that verified nothing about structure or value. A gate that fails open on missing input is worse than no gate, because it manufactures false confidence.

- **[`structural`] Voice choice (mode + dial %) is not a named artifact, so critique cannot tell deliberate restraint from accidental flatness.** The voice decision — which mode, what Professional-Dial level, which signature moves were deliberate — is made at S3 and is exactly the kind of "decided input" grounding (a)/(c) says must survive. But nothing records it. critique's Voice dimension "defers to writing-voice-modes," yet it receives only the draft to defer to. Worse, critique's stdlib analyzer diffs the draft against `baseline.json`, which is built from Sean's *full-voice* corpus. **What a reader observes:** a piece Sean deliberately dialed to 40% for a professional audience gets a false "monotonous vs your voice" flatness flag and burns the single grounded revise pass being "corrected" back toward full-voice — or, symmetrically, a genuinely flat piece passes because its flatness looked intentional. Either way the register the author chose is not the register that ships. The analyzer has degraded paths for a missing baseline and for tiny drafts, but none for an intentionally dialed-down register.

### Stage-pair: writing-critique → writing-humanity-pass

- **[`dangerously-wrong`] The critique verdict gates whether the chain should proceed, but nothing enforces it as a hard stop before the scrub — and in the headless path the chain proceeds anyway.** critique can return `structural-rework` (broken spine) and, in the headless chain-gate path, it caps at one grounded revise, re-critiques once, and then "pass[es] through to writing-humanity-pass" — with no branch specified for "the re-critique still holds a blocking finding," and "any second pass would require new external input," which a launchd run does not have. humanity-pass, for its part, has no "refuse if the verdict is structural-rework" gate; it just scrubs. **What a reader observes (headless, e.g. Substack-Drafter):** a post that is clean at the sentence level but structurally broken (open loop, unlanded Transfer) gets a flawless humanity-pass scrub and ships looking publish-ready — the cosmetic stage launders a failed verdict into polished slop, which value-engine itself names as "the worst outcome."

- **[`structural`] The critique fix list names the findings but not the *spans*, so humanity-pass cannot reliably protect the fixes it is told to protect.** humanity-pass must treat "every prose change made to satisfy a critique finding as protected input... never scrub a critique fix back out," and it acknowledges a fix "can *look* like a tell (a deliberately blunt sentence, a repeated word kept for emphasis)." But the fix list it consumes is "the findings carried forward, the single highest-leverage fix, and the verdict" — finding-level text, with no span anchor pointing at the exact sentence that embodies the fix. **What a reader observes:** the specific reader-cost fix the gate just made gets silently reverted — a deliberately blunt clarifying sentence smoothed back into the flat phrasing it replaced — regressing the very draft that just passed the gate, because the last stage had no anchor telling it *which* span was the protected fix versus an ordinary tell.

### Cleared cross-stage paths (walked, found harmless — logged, not padded as findings)

skill-audit says never invent issues to hit a count. Two cross-stage paths I traced end up safe, and I log them so the coverage is auditable rather than tagging them as defects:

- **Forward-pull line flags (S1 → S5, three hops across the voice stage).** storytelling flags section-end "forward-pull" lines and defers their punctuation to humanity-pass. This flag rarely survives three stages — but it does not need to: humanity-pass scrubs `—`, `–`, ` -- ` *globally* and re-scans for any hit as a hard gate, so a forward-pull line realized with a dash is caught whether or not the flag arrived. The decided input is emitted but its survival is not load-bearing. No reader-observable failure. Cleared.
- **Voice choice → humanity-pass (S3 → S5).** The missing voice-decision artifact (F6) hurts *critique*, but not the scrub: humanity-pass's do-not-flag allowlist *is* the entire signature-move catalog, so it preserves every move regardless of which were deliberate this run. Its failure mode is over-preservation (the safe direction). Cleared — and this is *why* F6's fix targets critique, not humanity-pass.

## Artifact 2 — Intent-Carrying Improvement Spec (chain-level)

Structured per skill-audit Step 5, Artifact 2: Objective and Desired Outcome pulled from the grounding (never invented); a per-finding fix carrying the *why* a weaker implementing model needs to make the same call on an unspecified edge case; and an explicit What-NOT-to-Change so the implementer doesn't "fix" what already works. Every `dangerously-wrong` and `structural` finding gets full reasoning; the two `minor` findings are listed with the change but without a full writeup.

### Objective

The chain must carry **one unbroken thread of intent** from the beat map to the published post (grounding (a), (d)). Today, five decided inputs can be silently re-derived or dropped at a handoff — and the danger concentrates at the Tier-1 voice stage, which every upstream artifact must cross and which no stage is charged with carrying them across (grounding (c)). This matters because **a dropped intent artifact never errors — it degrades silently**: a `ship` verdict that skipped its own gates, a diluted Transfer, a false flatness flag that burns the one revise pass, a deliberately-blunt fix scrubbed back out, a structurally-broken draft polished until it looks publishable. In each case the owner receives output that *looks* like it got a check it never actually got. Silent trust in an unverified result is the specific harm this spec removes.

### Desired outcome

From the owner's perspective (answers (c) and (d)): every downstream stage either **consumes the named upstream artifact by name**, or — if the artifact is absent — **flags the absence as a finding and refuses to fail open.** "The intent artifact didn't arrive" becomes loud, never silent. Concretely, at the end of a run: all opened loops are verified closed against the ledger (or the run is blocked because the ledger was lost); the locked Transfer is verified landed and welded to its artifact; the register that ships is the register the author *chose* (a dialed-down piece is judged against its chosen dial, not against maximum voice); and every critique fix is still present in the scrubbed draft. A `ship` becomes a claim the chain can actually back.

### The fix — the spine that carries most findings

Introduce a single **Chain Intent Ledger**: an orchestration-owned block, distinct from the prose, that *accretes* each stage's named outputs and travels alongside the draft through every hop, **including across the locked voice stage.** It starts as storytelling's Handoff Block (open-loop ledger + central loop + crest beat + seam beat), gains value-engine's Value Gate verdict + locked takeaway, gains a one-line voice-decision note at S3, and gains critique's fix list + verdict at S4. Because the *orchestration* owns the ledger, it survives S3 regardless of the fact that voice-modes emits only prose — **this is the move that respects Tier-1**: it adds a carrier *around* the voice stage instead of asking the locked stage to forward anything, and it would keep working even if voice-modes were swapped out entirely.

Paired with the carrier is one uniform rule that flips every downstream check from optional to enforced: **each named artifact is a REQUIRED input at the stage that consumes it; its absence is itself a blocking finding, never a silent skip.** A weaker model must internalize the inversion here — a missing ledger is the *alarm*, not the *all-clear*.

### The fix — per finding

- **F5 (`dangerously-wrong`) — context chokepoint / gates fail open.** (1) Carry the open-loop ledger, Value Gate verdict, and locked takeaway to critique in the Chain Intent Ledger above, so their arrival no longer depends on surviving the voice stage's prose emission. (2) Change critique's structure and value checks from "**when** the ledger/verdict is present, check it" to "the ledger and verdict are **required** inputs; if either is absent at the critique gate, that absence is a blocking `structural-rework` finding, because it means the chain lost its intent-carrying artifacts and no `ship` can be trusted." **Reasoning a weaker model needs:** the current "when present" wording reads as optional politeness, so a weaker model will treat a missing ledger as "nothing to check → pass." The intent is the exact opposite. A `ship` verdict is a *promise* that the loops closed and the Transfer landed; if the gate could not verify that because its inputs were gone, the honest verdict is "cannot clear," not "ship." Do **not** implement this by making voice-modes forward the artifacts — that risks touching the locked stage and still breaks if the stage is ever replaced. Put the carrier in the orchestration and the enforcement in critique.

- **F7 (`dangerously-wrong`) — verdict not enforced as a stop before the scrub.** Add a hard gate at the critique → humanity-pass handoff: humanity-pass reads the critique verdict *first*. If it is `structural-rework`, or if (headless) the single allowed re-critique still holds a blocking/major finding, humanity-pass does **not** scrub-and-pass — it routes the draft back into the chain (to S1/S2 for a broken spine, to S3 for a lingering line-fix) and, in a headless run, emits an explicit "blocked, not shipped" status instead of clean prose. **Reasoning a weaker model needs:** humanity-pass is cosmetic. Running a cosmetic scrub on a structurally-broken draft produces the single worst artifact the chain can emit — polished slop that *looks* shippable, which value-engine already names as the worst outcome — because the clean surface hides the broken spine from whoever ships it. The one-revise cap is correct and must stay (see What-NOT-to-Change); the fix is not "iterate more," it is "when still failing after the capped pass, STOP loudly instead of proceeding quietly." The scrub must never be the step that launders a failed verdict into publish-ready text.

- **F1 (`structural`) — seam beat re-derived.** value-engine consumes storytelling's named **seam beat** as the location where the Transfer/artifact lands, exactly as it already consumes the crest beat (pivot) and central loop (close) by name; it stops independently re-deriving "where value lands." If the seam beat and the beat where the central loop closes are not the same beat, value-engine raises that mismatch back to storytelling rather than silently reconciling it. **Reasoning a weaker model needs:** the entire "value = fulfillment of the hook" craft depends on the Transfer landing where the story built its payoff. Two stages each deciding "where value goes" independently is precisely how they drift apart; consume the marked beat, do not re-pick it.

- **F3 (`structural`) — locked takeaway not held as spine at the voice stage.** Because voice-modes is Tier-1 and its voice content is off-limits, encode the spine as an input contract *around* it: (a) the orchestration labels the locked takeaway `SPINE:` in the handoff into S3, and (b) critique's Value dimension is extended from "verify the Transfer landed" to "verify **every section serves the locked takeaway**," reading the takeaway from the Chain Intent Ledger. **Reasoning a weaker model needs:** "write against the beat map" and "keep every section serving this one sentence" are different instructions — a fluent draft steered only by beat order will wander off the promise. Naming the takeaway as the governing spine, and having the auditor check adherence to it, is what keeps the one capability from diffusing. This changes no voice content; it adds a label and a check.

- **F6 (`structural`) — voice choice not carried; false flatness flags.** Emit a one-line **voice-decision note** as a named artifact at S3 — chosen mode + dial % + any deliberately-restrained register — carried in the Chain Intent Ledger to critique. This records the run's *dial*, not any technique's internals, so it is **not voice content** and the Tier-1 constraint holds. critique's Voice dimension and its analyzer then read the note: a piece marked "dial 40%" is judged against a restrained target, and the analyzer's low-burstiness "monotonous vs your voice" signal is annotated or suppressed (never fired as a finding) when the note says the dial was intentionally below full. **Reasoning a weaker model needs:** `baseline.json` is built from full-voice corpus, so comparing any deliberately-professional piece to it *guarantees* a false flatness signal — the tool is working correctly against the wrong target. Give the auditor the register the author chose, so "flat" is measured against intent, not against maximum voice. Never treat below-baseline burstiness as an AI-flatness tell when the voice-decision note says the dial was turned down on purpose.

- **F8 (`structural`) — fix list has no span anchors.** Extend the critique fix-list format so each protected fix carries a **span anchor** (a quoted phrase, or a beat/paragraph locator), not just the finding text. humanity-pass matches on the anchor before scrubbing and never removes an anchored span. **Reasoning a weaker model needs:** "protect the fixes" is unenforceable if the last stage cannot *locate* them, and the fixes most at risk are exactly the ones that read like tells (a deliberately blunt sentence, a repeated word kept for emphasis) — the precise things the scrub targets. Without an anchor, the protection fails silently on its highest-risk cases. A critique fix is protected input on the same footing as a signature move; when in doubt whether a span is a fix, the fix list's anchors are the source of truth — leave anchored spans alone.

- **F2 (`minor`).** value-engine asserts the Handoff Block is present and well-formed (all four markers named; every ledger loop has a scheduled close) before consuming it; a missing/malformed block is flagged, not silently worked around.

- **F4 (`minor`).** Clarify the chain-contract wording so "does not reorder beats" explicitly permits value-engine to **cut** a dropped itch's beats under Rule-of-One (cutting ≠ reordering); the map that reaches the voice stage contains only the surviving itch's beats.

### What NOT to change

- **writing-voice-modes' voice content — untouched, entirely.** Sean Mode, the four borrowed author techniques (Sedaris/Thompson/Kerouac/Vonnegut), the full signature-move catalog, the House Style grit register, the Professional-Dial *semantics*, and the Do-Not-Promote list are all Tier-1. No fix above edits any of them. F3 and F6 deliberately add a carrier and a one-line dial label *around* the stage; neither reaches inside its voice.
- **The em-dash hard rule and humanity-pass's global dash scan.** Already correct, and load-bearing: the forward-pull cross-stage path (Cleared Paths) is safe *because* the scan is global. Do not make it conditional on a surviving flag.
- **The one-grounded-revise-pass cap in critique.** It is a deliberate guard against un-anchored self-iteration degrading prose toward bland. F7 does not loosen it — it makes the STOP *after* the cap loud instead of silent. Do not "fix" the cap into unbounded iteration.
- **The already-well-wired handoffs.** Crest-beat + central-loop consumption at storytelling→value-engine works — leave it; F1 extends the *same* pattern to the seam beat, it does not rebuild it. The mutual sideways-ask / "Desperation Posing as Self-Deprecation" deference between value-engine and voice-modes is correctly split (value-engine owns the structural gate, voice-modes owns the tone) — leave it. The critique-fix-list → humanity-pass protection *contract* is correct — F8 sharpens its *format* with span anchors, it does not remove the contract.
- **humanity-pass's full-catalog signature-move allowlist.** Its over-preservation is the safe failure and is exactly why the missing voice-decision note harms only the critique gate, not the scrub. Keep the allowlist broad; do not narrow it to "only moves used this run."
