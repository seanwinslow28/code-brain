# Lane manifest — Growth & Distribution

Mandatory first read for the [Growth & Distribution seat](../bench/growth-distribution.md).
Entries are title + pointer + one-line when-to-read — shelf labels, never the books: the
content lives in the private corpus (`../corpus/`, gitignored), so pointers resolve only on
a machine that has it (degradation ladder otherwise). Follow only the pointers relevant to
the task. Seats stop at `## Reading path`; that section is the owner's.

Three sources divide the lane: Balfour supplies the model, Verna the motion and the org,
Poyar the dated numbers. Every benchmark in the Poyar file carries the year it was
published — carry that year forward or do not carry the number.

## The four fits

- **The Four Fits, and why product–market fit isn't enough** — [`balfour.md`](../corpus/canon/balfour.md) — a growth plan is being justified by product quality or PMF alone, before any channel or pricing decision is locked.
- **Market–Product Fit: defining the market before the product** — [`balfour.md`](../corpus/canon/balfour.md) — the team can name its category and persona but not the problem, or adoption is coming from a segment nobody targeted.
- **Product–Channel Fit: channels dictate product** — [`balfour.md`](../corpus/canon/balfour.md) — picking an acquisition channel, a channel that worked has stopped, or someone proposes porting the product onto a new platform unchanged.
- **Channel–Model Fit and the ARPU–CAC danger zone** — [`balfour.md`](../corpus/canon/balfour.md) — before any pricing or packaging change, and whenever a proposed tier sits between self-serve and sales-assisted economics.
- **Model–Market Fit: the $100M arithmetic** — [`balfour.md`](../corpus/canon/balfour.md) — a revenue target has been set without counting the customers in the market.
- **The four fits as one system, with the HubSpot Sales case** — [`balfour.md`](../corpus/canon/balfour.md) — a company is moving up or down market, adding a tier, or launching a new line inside an existing one.

## Loops, not funnels

- **Growth loops, not funnels — including launch as loop protection** — [`balfour.md`](../corpus/canon/balfour.md) — growth is being modelled as a funnel, strategy is siloed by function, or a launch would expose the product to a broad untargeted audience.
- **Retention, authentic growth, and metric discipline** — [`balfour.md`](../corpus/canon/balfour.md) — a north-star metric is being chosen or defended, or retention and engagement are being used interchangeably.
- **Stages: traction, transition, growth, and the canyon between** — [`balfour.md`](../corpus/canon/balfour.md) — deciding team shape, channel focus or optimisation altitude for the company's stage, or post-PMF growth has gone flat.

## Motions: which engine, and who owns it

- **Motions and levers: the growth-model matrix** — [`elena-verna.md`](../corpus/canon/elena-verna.md) — before choosing or changing which team owns a growth lever, or when someone proposes switching from one motion to another.
- **What PLG is not, and the offence/defence call** — [`elena-verna.md`](../corpus/canon/elena-verna.md) — deciding whether to invest in self-serve at all, or a stakeholder is treating PLG as a cheaper substitute for sales.
- **Product-led sales: PQA, PQL, and routing** — [`elena-verna.md`](../corpus/canon/elena-verna.md) — sales wants access to self-serve users, or you are building the qualification and hand-off logic between product signals and reps.
- **Product-led sales, sales-assist, and PQA scoring** — [`kyle-poyar.md`](../corpus/canon/kyle-poyar.md) — a self-serve product is adding its first sales motion, or MQLs are still the reporting unit.

## Free tiers, trials and conversion

- **Freemium, trials, and the reverse trial** — [`elena-verna.md`](../corpus/canon/elena-verna.md) — designing or revising the free tier, the paywall or the trial mechanics, and before quoting any free-to-paid conversion benchmark.
- **The freemium / free-trial / reverse-trial call, with PLG funnel math** — [`kyle-poyar.md`](../corpus/canon/kyle-poyar.md) — the free-tier model is undecided, or you need to forecast signups and conversion from traffic.

## Moving upmarket

- **The team escalator and account-level metrics** — [`elena-verna.md`](../corpus/canon/elena-verna.md) — a B2B product with strong individual adoption cannot land enterprise deals, or acquisition is still counted in users.
- **The nine unoriginal PLG sales problems** — [`elena-verna.md`](../corpus/canon/elena-verna.md) — a self-serve company is moving upmarket, or enterprise revenue is climbing while the self-serve engine quietly degrades.

## Building the growth org

- **Squad sequencing and the growth org chart** — [`elena-verna.md`](../corpus/canon/elena-verna.md) — before standing up a growth team, adding a squad, or arguing about where the growth function should report.
- **Six rules of hiring for growth** — [`elena-verna.md`](../corpus/canon/elena-verna.md) — before opening a growth req, or when assessing whether the company has earned a growth team yet.

## The AI-era rewrite

- **Trust as the constraint: the AI-era rewrite** — [`elena-verna.md`](../corpus/canon/elena-verna.md) — the acquisition channels in a plan are SEO, SEM or paid social, or growth targets assume a pre-AI distribution model.
- **The AI-era rewrite and the platform distribution shift** — [`balfour.md`](../corpus/canon/balfour.md) — an AI-cost or AI-competitor question hits margins or PMF, and before betting distribution on any platform whose gates are currently open.
- **The metrics that broke for AI-native products** — [`kyle-poyar.md`](../corpus/canon/kyle-poyar.md) — an AI product is being measured on a classic SaaS dashboard, or ARR and DAU are carrying a decision they cannot support.
