# Lane manifest — Product Leadership

Mandatory first read for the [Product Leadership seat](../bench/product-leadership.md).
Entries are title + pointer + one-line when-to-read — shelf labels, never the books: the
content lives in the private corpus (`../corpus/`, gitignored), so pointers resolve only on
a machine that has it (degradation ladder otherwise). Follow only the pointers relevant to
the task. Seats stop at `## Reading path`; that section is the owner's.

This is the studio's thinnest free layer and its most book-carried lane. The essays below
are the argument and the hiring apparatus; the operating-model apparatus — charters,
planning cadence, feedback mechanisms, the six-page narrative, managerial leverage — is in
the books and nowhere else. Nine leadership topics the free layer does not reach are named
in the Ken Norton file's Gaps section, each routed to its book. Check there before
concluding this lane has an answer.

## What product leadership owes teams

- **Three Team Types and the Four Risks** — [`svpg.md`](../corpus/canon/svpg.md) — diagnosing whether a team is actually empowered, before proposing any change to how it works.
- **Product Leadership Is Hard** — [`svpg.md`](../corpus/canon/svpg.md) — scoping a head-of-product role, or defining what leaders owe teams beyond approving work.
- **The Art of Product Management (the Six C's)** — [`ken-norton.md`](../corpus/canon/ken-norton.md) — a leadership deliverable keeps reaching for frameworks and the actual gap is human skill.

## Leverage, delegation, and the leader's own time

- **The three levels of product work — impact, execution, optics** — [`shreyas-doshi.md`](../corpus/canon/shreyas-doshi.md) — a review keeps ending in conflict and the parties are arguing details instead of the disagreement.
- **LNO, opportunity cost, and Radical Delegation** — [`shreyas-doshi.md`](../corpus/canon/shreyas-doshi.md) — a leader is fully busy, delivering, and producing no singular impact, or cannot say what to drop.
- **How Product Leaders Can Learn to Say No** — [`ken-norton.md`](../corpus/canon/ken-norton.md) — an engagement is stalled on a decision nobody will force, or a leader needs language for refusing without formal authority.

## Coaching and developing PMs

- **Coaching Tools: the Assessment, the Plan, the One-on-One** — [`svpg.md`](../corpus/canon/svpg.md) — standing up a PM development practice, or a manager asks where to start with a struggling report.
- **From Reactive to Creative Leadership, with The Drama Triangle** — [`ken-norton.md`](../corpus/canon/ken-norton.md) — a leader's default under pressure needs naming and generic feedback has already failed.
- **Preparing to Have a Difficult Conversation** — [`ken-norton.md`](../corpus/canon/ken-norton.md) — a deliverable requires someone to open a conversation they have been avoiding.
- **Learning to Receive Feedback, with Personal User Manuals** — [`ken-norton.md`](../corpus/canon/ken-norton.md) — the stated feedback problem is really an intake problem, or you are designing how a team declares how it wants to be worked with.
- **Micromanagement types, manager anti-patterns, and feedback hygiene** — [`shreyas-doshi.md`](../corpus/canon/shreyas-doshi.md) — before deciding to get deeply involved in someone's project, or a manager reads well from above and badly from below.

## Hiring, and the career ladder

- **How to Hire a Product Manager, with the 10- and 20-year retrospectives** — [`ken-norton.md`](../corpus/canon/ken-norton.md) — designing a PM hiring loop or scorecard, or a stakeholder wants to keep an interview practice the author himself has since withdrawn.
- **Dual Product Management Career Path, with Rabbit or Kangaroo** — [`ken-norton.md`](../corpus/canon/ken-norton.md) — designing a PM job ladder, or senior-PM attrition is people-management gravity rather than compensation.

## Org design and topology

- **Team Topology and Org Design** — [`svpg.md`](../corpus/canon/svpg.md) — a reorg is being proposed as the fix, or team boundaries are being redrawn.
- **Don't Ship the Org Chart (Parts 1 and 2)** — [`ken-norton.md`](../corpus/canon/ken-norton.md) — drawing PM ownership boundaries or sizing a product team, before any re-org proposal leaves the studio.
- **CEOs and Product Leaders, with The PM Mind Meld** — [`ken-norton.md`](../corpus/canon/ken-norton.md) — the presenting problem is CPO/CEO drift or an undefined split of ownership.

## Influence, agency, and thinking clearly

- **High agency, influence, and the limits of PM power** — [`shreyas-doshi.md`](../corpus/canon/shreyas-doshi.md) — someone asks for more authority to get things done, or you are weighing talent against drive.
- **Pre-mortems: Tigers, Paper Tigers, Elephants** — [`shreyas-doshi.md`](../corpus/canon/shreyas-doshi.md) — one to three months out from a launch that would be expensive to get wrong.
- **The Antithesis Principle and clear thinking under ambiguity** — [`shreyas-doshi.md`](../corpus/canon/shreyas-doshi.md) — a persuasive observation about how people work is about to become your own operating default.
