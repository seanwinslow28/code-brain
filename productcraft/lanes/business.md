# Lane manifest — Business & Economics

Mandatory first read for the [Business & Economics seat](../bench/business-economics.md).
Entries are title + pointer + one-line when-to-read — shelf labels, never the books: the
content lives in the private corpus (`../corpus/`, gitignored), so pointers resolve only on
a machine that has it (degradation ladder otherwise). Follow only the pointers relevant to
the task. Seats stop at `## Reading path`; that section is the owner's.

Both sources are dated and know it. Skok's definitions are durable but his thresholds were
last calibrated around 2016; Poyar's benchmarks are 2023–2026 and each carries its own year
in the file. Before this seat quotes any number, agree which formula is in play and carry
the vintage with it — a threshold without a year is a defect, not a shortcut.

## Reading the business, not the income statement

- **What SaaS Breaks About Ordinary Accounting** — [`saas-metrics.md`](../corpus/canon/saas-metrics.md) — a stakeholder is reading the income statement as the health signal, or bookings, billings, revenue and ARR are being used interchangeably in a doc.
- **Gross Margin — The Assumption Under Every Threshold** — [`saas-metrics.md`](../corpus/canon/saas-metrics.md) — before applying any classic SaaS benchmark to an AI-inflected or services-heavy product.

## The two sides of unit economics

- **CAC: The Cost Side, and How It Is Faked** — [`saas-metrics.md`](../corpus/canon/saas-metrics.md) — a CAC figure looks flattering, acquisition cost is being treated as fixed, or a go-to-market motion change is on the table.
- **LTV: Four Escalating Formulas, and Where Each One Breaks** — [`saas-metrics.md`](../corpus/canon/saas-metrics.md) — before accepting or writing any LTV number, especially if the business has expansion revenue.
- **The Two Viability Guidelines — and Their Revision History** — [`saas-metrics.md`](../corpus/canon/saas-metrics.md) — someone cites 3× LTV:CAC or twelve-month payback as settled law and you need what it was actually calibrated against.

## Retention and expansion

- **Churn, Cohorts, and the Path to Negative Churn** — [`saas-metrics.md`](../corpus/canon/saas-metrics.md) — a retention argument is being made, customer and dollar churn point in different directions, or an expansion-pricing bet needs its mechanism named.

## Efficiency, cash, and the shape of the plan

- **Efficiency Ratios the Capital Markets Actually Use** — [`saas-metrics.md`](../corpus/canon/saas-metrics.md) — a board or exec is benchmarking by magic number, quick ratio, rule of 40 or burn multiple, and you need to know what each ratio hides.
- **The Cash Flow Trough: The Shape, With Numbers** — [`saas-metrics.md`](../corpus/canon/saas-metrics.md) — a growth investment is being challenged on near-term losses, or a prepay/billing-terms decision needs its cash consequence made concrete.
- **The Operating Layer Beneath the Metrics** — [`saas-metrics.md`](../corpus/canon/saas-metrics.md) — the argument is about which segment or motion to fund, or funnel and hiring plans need to reconcile with the revenue target.

## Pricing and packaging

- **Pricing model mix, 2024–2026** — [`kyle-poyar.md`](../corpus/canon/kyle-poyar.md) — a team is choosing what to charge on, or defending per-seat against "everyone is moving to usage."
- **The broken-pricing diagnostic, by model you already run** — [`kyle-poyar.md`](../corpus/canon/kyle-poyar.md) — pricing exists and someone is proposing a replatform rather than a fix inside the current model.
- **Packaging: good-better-best, the add-on filter, and what never to gate** — [`kyle-poyar.md`](../corpus/canon/kyle-poyar.md) — tiers are being drawn, an add-on is proposed, or a feature is about to go behind a paywall.
- **AI monetization: credits, outcomes, and the margin floor** — [`kyle-poyar.md`](../corpus/canon/kyle-poyar.md) — pricing an AI feature or agent, or someone assumes SaaS gross margins still apply.
- **Price-change cadence and the pricing page** — [`kyle-poyar.md`](../corpus/canon/kyle-poyar.md) — before a price increase, an AI add-on-to-bundle move, or a pricing-page redesign.

## Dated benchmarks

- **SaaS benchmarks by ARR band, 2023–2025** — [`kyle-poyar.md`](../corpus/canon/kyle-poyar.md) — a growth, retention, margin or ARR-per-employee number needs a dated peer comparison.
- **CAC payback × NRR, and the odds of reaching each ARR milestone** — [`kyle-poyar.md`](../corpus/canon/kyle-poyar.md) — efficiency is being defended, a long payback needs justifying, or a plan assumes a milestone will arrive.

## Before it leaves the seat

- **The Mis-measurement Catalogue** — [`saas-metrics.md`](../corpus/canon/saas-metrics.md) — the review checklist for any incoming metrics deck, dashboard spec or business case before it goes to a gate.
