# Generic-AI prose corpus (the "what we're moving away from" baseline)

This is a small corpus of deliberately generic, flat, large-language-model-default
prose. It is NOT anyone's voice. VoicePrint's proof step computes its fingerprint
(sentence-length burstiness, lexical diversity) once and stores it as
`generic-ai-baseline.json`, then asks: is the reader's draft closer to their own
samples, or to this? Closer to their own samples is the win.
