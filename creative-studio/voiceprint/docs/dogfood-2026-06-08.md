# Dogfood Report — 2026-06-08

In-session validation of VoicePrint against a **simulated** stranger persona,
deliberately chosen to be maximally un-Sean. Proves the two things that are the real
risk: (1) zero Sean leakage, (2) the tool builds a *distinct, specific* voice, not a
template with a name in it. Real-human dogfood is the post-build step; this report +
the re-runnable scan make that cheap.

Artifacts: `dogfood/priya/voiceprint/` (evidence pile in `_work/`, generated bundle in
`my-voice/`). The `dogfood/` directory is dev evidence and is excluded from the
packaged `.plugin`.

## The persona

**Priya** — a warm, humble community-garden newsletter writer. No profanity, no
dive-bar grit, no bro pop-culture; her register is gentle, domestic, anti-hustle,
landing on soft earned aphorisms. The opposite of Sean's voice on every axis, by
design. Her wells: Mister Rogers, the quiet parts of Ghibli, M.S. Subbulakshmi,
a Tamil saying from her patti, slow-TV Norwegian train videos. Her signature move:
deflating uplift with the literal ("It's not magic, it's compost").

## The hard gates (deterministic)

| Gate | Result |
|---|---|
| **Sean-leakage scan** (grep her bundle for sean/ferry/Phuket/Guy Fieri/dive bar/the author modes/vault/etc.) | **CLEAN — zero markers** |
| **Proof pipeline** (her draft vs her samples vs the generic-AI baseline) | reader burstiness **0.69**, draft **0.57**, generic-AI **0.39** → draft **closer_to: reader** ✓ |
| **Dashboard + state render** | OK (renders her pile + next action offline) |

The proof number is the important one: her gentle, even-tempered prose is *less* bursty
than a grittier writer's, but still clearly above generic-AI flatness (0.39) and closer
to her own samples than to the machine baseline. The measurement is honest across very
different registers.

## Independent audit (fresh subagent, no priming)

A general-purpose subagent reviewed the generated bundle cold and scored it:

- **Distinctness: 8/10** — "reads as one specific, real person… I could pick Priya out
  of a lineup," anchored by idiosyncratic proper nouns no template would invent (Doreen
  next door, the iluppai flower, the tool shed). Not a 10 only because the *genre*
  (literary garden essayist) is a recognizable lane — the proper nouns make her
  unmistakable, the style category is shared.
- **Evidence-backing: mostly clean** — every signature-move row and every rejected
  register cites a verbatim quote. The move-level claims are quote-grounded.
- **Foreign content: none of another writer** — internally coherent; references,
  samples, cheese bank, and SKILL all point at the same person.

It then caught **two real template bugs** (the point of the exercise):

1. **"Do-Not-Promote" section = foreign jargon.** Brand/marketing-strategist language,
   tonally foreign to a writer whose whole thesis rejects that register, shipped as an
   unfilled stub.
2. **Professional Dial over-claimed contextual range.** "Her voice barely changes by
   context" + "dialing down means substitution" were asserted with no sample proving
   it (the corpus is one register) — the distillation trap wearing a confident face.

Plus two minor, by-design notes: the universal anti-slop block is shared boilerplate
(intentional — it's the validated spine, labeled "universal"), and the gauntlet rows
are gap-numbered (honest: only the revealing reactions were kept).

## Fixes applied (template + synthesis, so every future reader benefits)

- **`SKILL.template.md` + `voiceprint-synthesis/SKILL.md`:** the Professional Dial is
  now a HARD-gated section — build the table ONLY with two-or-more-register evidence;
  otherwise state honestly that the corpus shows one register and stop. Explicitly bans
  asserting range or "dial-down-by-substitution" without a proving sample.
- **`SKILL.template.md`:** "Do-Not-Promote" renamed to **"Off-limits — things to keep
  out,"** with guidance to write it in the writer's own plain register, not strategist
  jargon.
- **Priya's bundle** corrected to demonstrate both fixes (honest single-register dial;
  warmer Off-limits section).

## Verdict

VoicePrint builds a real, specific, quote-grounded, **Sean-free** voice from a
stranger's evidence, and the proof measurement is honest across registers. The dogfood
caught exactly the genericness/over-claim failure it was designed to catch, and the
fixes harden the generator for every future reader — not just Priya.
