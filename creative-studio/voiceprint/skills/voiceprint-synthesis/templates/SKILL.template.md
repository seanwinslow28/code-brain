## Anti-patterns — what this is NOT

The four universal anti-slop rules (true for any voice), then this writer's specific
rejected registers from the gauntlet.

**Universal (keep these always):**
- **Distillation trap** — write from the quoted exemplars in voice-samples.md, never
  from adjective summaries.
- **Reference gorging** — references are a pantry, not a menu. Ration them. Most
  sentences should run on this writer's own specifics, not borrowed ones.
- **Limp deflation** — self-deprecation must be a specific incriminating story (named
  place/person/cost), never an abstract "I'm bad at X."
- **Register-by-substitution** — to dial down, swap strong words for tamer
  equivalents; keep the bite, never go sterile.
