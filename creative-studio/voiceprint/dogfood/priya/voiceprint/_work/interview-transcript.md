# Interview Transcript — Priya (dogfood persona, NOT a real user)

> Dogfood evidence for VoicePrint. Persona deliberately chosen to be maximally

## Transcript (verbatim Q&A)

**Q: Give me the 2 a.m. stuff — what do you actually quote, not the impressive list.**
A: "Honestly? Mister Rogers. 'Look for the helpers.' My mom said it every time the
news got scary and now I say it to my volunteers when a frost wipes out the seedlings."

**Q: Push — a specific line you say, not just the show.**
A: "'Won't you be my neighbor' — but I say it dead serious when someone new shows up
to the garden and won't make eye contact. It breaks the ice every time."

**Q: Music — what you grew up on, and the embarrassing tier.**
A: "My grandmother's Carnatic records, M.S. Subbulakshmi on a Sunday morning. That's
the real one. Embarrassing tier: I know every word to the entire Lion King soundtrack
and I will not apologize."

**Q: Where you're from — a specific place, a person, a phrase.**
A: "Fremont, California, then Portland. My patti (grandmother) had a Tamil saying she'd
hit me with whenever I rushed: 'Aalai illaadha ooril iluppai chakkaram' — basically,
in a town with no sugarcane press, the iluppai flower is sweet enough. Make do. Notice
what's already sweet."

**Q: The thing you love but are a little embarrassed to admit.**
A: "I cry at those slow-TV Norwegian train videos. Seven hours of a train going
through snow. I find it more moving than most films. Also I name my tomato plants."

**Q: A reference you reach for when you explain something.**
A: "Ghibli, but the quiet parts — not the action. The scene in Totoro where they just
wait at the bus stop in the rain. That's what patience in a garden feels like."
