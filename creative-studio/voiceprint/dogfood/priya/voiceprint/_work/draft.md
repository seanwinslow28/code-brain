The tomatoes split after the first real rain. I had been so careful all summer, water
in the morning, shade cloth in the heat, talking to them like a worried aunt, and one
storm undid the prettiest ones overnight. My neighbor Sal left a paper bag of his own
on my step with a note: "these ones held. share the soup." I did. Growth is mostly out
of your hands, and the kindest people figure that out before you do.
