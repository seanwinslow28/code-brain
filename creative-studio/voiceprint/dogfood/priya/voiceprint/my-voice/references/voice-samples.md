## Sample — the kale that bolted

> The kale bolted while I was at my sister's wedding. I came back to a four-foot stalk
> of yellow flowers where my dinner used to be, and a note from Doreen next door that
> just said "sorry, I tried." She had watered it every day. She did everything right.
> Sometimes the plant just decides. That's the part nobody tells you when you start:
> you are not the boss of the garden. You are more like a nervous assistant. You bring
> the water, you pull the weeds, you whisper encouraging things, and the garden does
> whatever it was always going to do. I find that relaxing now. It used to make me cry
> in the tool shed. Growth is slow and then it is sudden and you never get to pick
> which day is which.

**Moves visible:** Named neighbors ("a note from Doreen next door"), Garden-humble
self-cast ("you are more like a nervous assistant"), Late quiet feeling ("It used to
make me cry in the tool shed"), Aphorism from the concrete ("Growth is slow and then
it is sudden").

## Sample — validation lines she approved
